## The behaviour

Under the MIDI Note Editor's note area sit the expression lanes: Velocity, Chance and Release Velocity, each a row of markers, one at each note's start, as tall as the note's value. The Lane Selector, a small triangle at the lanes' left, shows and hides each lane, and a switch beside it hides them all; a new editor shows Velocity alone. Dragging a marker changes its note's value, or every selected note's by the same amount; with Draw Mode on, dragging across a lane draws the values. A note's velocity deviation, the random range it plays in, shows in the Velocity lane as a shaded band from its marker, and Cmd-dragging a marker sets it. The footer under the lanes holds the active lane's controls: Randomize and its amount, the Ramp's two values, and the deviation for Velocity; Randomize, Play All and Play One for Chance. Keys raise and lower the selected notes' velocity, deviation and chance, and typing a number sets their velocity. Pressing 0 deactivates the selected notes, which stay in place, drawn grey, and don't play.

Notes can be grouped, so they roll their chance together. In a Play All group every note plays or none does; in a Play One group one note, chosen at random, plays. A group shows one marker in the Chance lane, a diamond for Play All and a triangle for Play One.

The note operations change the notes themselves. Holding E and clicking a note splits it where it was clicked; Cmd-E chops the selected notes on the grid, or splits the notes crossing the insert marker or the time selection's edges; Cmd-J joins the selected notes of each pitch into one; Cmd-Option-J stretches the selected notes over the time selection. Two stretch markers at the ends of the selection scale the notes between them in time. Cmd-Shift-E splits the Arrangement clip itself at the time selection.

This spec covers the lanes, the footer's value controls, the value keys, deactivating, probability groups in the document, the engine and the editor, and the note operations. Undo step names written in code type are Beatlayer's own. It leaves the rest of the editor to [piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md), playback of single notes to [midi-clips.md](midi-clips.md), the MIDI Tools and the Pitch and Time Utilities to WP-6.11 to WP-6.16, and the MPE tab to WP-6.18.

## Rules

**The expression lanes and the footer (WP-6.1)**

- **R1.** Under the note area sit three expression lanes, in this order from the top: Velocity, Chance and Release Velocity (the order is Beatlayer's own). Each lane shows one value of each note in the clip's span ([piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R4) as a marker: a stem at the note's start, from the lane's foot to the value's height, over three labels at its highest, middle and lowest values (127, 64 and 1 for Velocity and Release Velocity; 100 %, 50 % and 0 % for Chance). The Lane Selector, a small triangle at the lanes' left (L-CLIP-014), lists the three lanes, ticked while shown, and choosing one shows or hides it; the Show/Hide All Expression Editors switch beside it hides every lane and shows them again as they were. A new editor shows the Velocity lane alone. Which lanes show, and their heights, are view state: no undo step, not saved with the set. (S1, S2, S3, S12)
- **R2.** The lane header's context menu (C-LANE) has Swap Lane, which hides that lane and shows in its place the first hidden lane after it in R1's order, going round to the first (the order is Beatlayer's own). Dragging the split between the note area and the lanes, or between two lanes, resizes them, and dragging a lane down to nothing hides it; Option with the wheel (Alt on Windows), or a pinch, over the lanes makes them taller or shorter. (S1, S10)
- **R3.** One lane is active: the lane last clicked, or last chosen in the Lane Selector, and Velocity in a new editor. Its header shows the value of the marker under the pointer or being dragged, and the footer (L-CLIP-015) shows its controls (R10). `view.lane` makes a lane active and shows it; its `velocity_deviation` makes the Velocity lane active. (S1, S2, S11)
- **R4.** A note's body shows its velocity and whether it's deactivated (R18) as [piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R43 draws them; a note whose chance is below 100 %, and every grouped note (R24), shows a small triangle at its top-left corner while its key row is at least 9 points tall (the height is Beatlayer's own). Hovering a note marks its markers, and hovering a marker marks its note; selected notes' markers are drawn in the selection colour. (S2, S3, S4)
- **R5.** Dragging a marker up or down changes its note's value, or, when the note is selected, every selected note's value by the same amount, each held within its range, so dragging past an end brings them all to that end. Clicking a marker selects its note, and Shift-clicking adds or removes it ([piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R26). While a marker is dragged its value shows in the lane header and the Status Bar. The drag is one undo step, and Esc puts the values back. (S2, S3, S13: I-DRAG-036)
- **R6.** With Draw Mode on, dragging across a lane draws: each note whose start lies in a grid step the pointer passes takes the value at the pointer's height there, only the selected notes when some are selected. With snapping off (Cmd-4, or Cmd held, Alt on Windows) each note takes the value at its own start. Option-dragging (Alt on Windows) draws a straight line from where the drag began to the pointer, and with Shift too a level line at the starting value. The drag is one undo step. (S2, S13: I-DRAG-037)
- **R7.** In the Velocity lane, a note with a velocity deviation shows it as a shaded band from its marker's top, upward for a positive deviation and downward for a negative one, to velocity plus deviation, and a dot in its marker. Cmd-dragging (Ctrl-dragging on Windows) up or down from a marker sets the deviation of its note, or of every selected note when it's selected, and double-clicking a marker that has a deviation sets it to 0; each is one undo step. A note's deviation stays within the room its velocity leaves: at most 127 minus its velocity and at least 1 minus its velocity. (S2; the lower bound is Beatlayer's own)
- **R8.** `note.set_value` and `note.adjust_value` with `velocity_deviation` hold each note's deviation within R7's room, and a value outside -127 to 127 is still refused. A later change of velocity leaves the deviation as it is, and playback holds the played velocity within 1 to 127 ([midi-clips.md](midi-clips.md) R6). (S2, S14)
- **R9.** The Chance lane edits each note's chance from 0 to 100 % as R5 and R6 say; while selected notes' chances differ, the lane header shows the lowest and highest of them. The Release Velocity lane edits each note's release velocity, from 1 to 127, which reaches the instrument with the note-off ([midi-clips.md](midi-clips.md) R7). (S2, S3, S12)
- **R10.** The footer shows the active lane's controls after the MIDI tool menu (L-CLIP-015): for Velocity, Randomize with its amount, then Ramp's start and end values, then Deviation; for Chance, Randomize with its amount, then Play All, Play One and Ungroup (WP-6.2, R22 and R23); for Release Velocity, Randomize with its amount, then Ramp's start and end values (Release Velocity's controls and the Ungroup button's place are Beatlayer's own). A value field shows the selected notes' value, or the lowest and highest where they differ, and takes a typed value on Enter. With no clip in the editor every control is disabled. (S2, S3, S4, S10, S11, S13: L-CLIP-015)
- **R11.** Ramp's start value sets the first selected note's value, by start, and its end value the last's; the selected notes between take values on a straight line between the two by their start times, notes that start together taking the same value (`note.ramp_value`, one undo step). With one note selected only the start is available, and with every selected note starting at the same time the end is disabled. (S2, S11; spreading by start time is Beatlayer's own)
- **R12.** The Deviation field sets every selected note's velocity deviation (`note.set_value`), each within R7's room, and shows the lowest and highest where they differ. (S2, S10)
- **R13.** Randomize's amount, from 0 to 127 for Velocity and Release Velocity and from 0 to 100 % for Chance, is 0 in a new editor (Beatlayer's own) and is view state. With notes selected, moving the amount or typing one and pressing Enter randomises them at once from the values they had when the gesture began: each note takes a value drawn evenly from its starting value less the amount to its starting value plus the amount, held within its range and whole for velocities, and each note keeps its own draw through the gesture, so a larger amount moves each note further the same way. With no notes selected, changing the amount changes nothing until Enter or the Randomize button. The Randomize button applies the amount once to the selected notes, or to every note in the clip's span when none are selected, from their values now, with a new draw. Each gesture or press is one undo step. `note.randomize` takes the value, the amount and an optional seed, and the same seed with the same notes gives the same values. (S2, S3, S10; the even draw and the seed are Beatlayer's own)
- **R14.** With notes selected and no number being typed, a digit from 1 to 9 starts a velocity, further digits add to it, Enter sets every selected note's velocity to it, and Esc drops it (S-NOTE-026); a number outside 1 to 127 changes nothing. While a number is being typed, 0 is a digit; otherwise 0 deactivates (R18). One undo step. (S2, S9; how 0 is told apart is Beatlayer's own)
- **R15.** With notes selected, Cmd-Up and Cmd-Down (Ctrl on Windows; S-NOTE-027) raise or lower their velocity by 10, Cmd-Shift-Up and Cmd-Shift-Down (S-NOTE-028) their velocity deviation by 10, within R7's room, and Cmd-Option-Up and Cmd-Option-Down (Ctrl-Alt on Windows; S-NOTE-029) their chance by 10 %; each note is held within its range (`note.adjust_value`). Each press is one undo step, and a press that changes nothing is none. (S2, S3, S9; the deviation's step is Beatlayer's own)
- **R16.** After a marker is clicked, its lane has the keyboard focus until the note area is clicked: there, in the Velocity and Release Velocity lanes, Cmd-Up and Cmd-Down change the selected notes' value by 10 and Cmd-Shift-Up and Cmd-Shift-Down by 1 (S-NOTE-050), and in the Chance lane, Up and Down by 10 % and Shift-Up and Shift-Down by 1 % (S-NOTE-051). (S2, S3; the fine steps are Beatlayer's own)
- **R17.** Cmd-dragging a note up or down in the note area (Alt-dragging on Windows; S-NOTE-016, I-DRAG-025) raises or lowers its velocity, or every selected note's when it's selected, by one for each point dragged (the rate is Beatlayer's own), each held within 1 to 127. The drag is one undo step. (S2, S9)

**Deactivating notes (WP-6.1)**

- **R18.** 0 with notes selected (S-NOTE-041), Edit ▸ Deactivate Note(s) (M-EDIT-008 with notes selected), and Deactivate Note(s) in the editor's and the piano ruler's context menus (C-NOTE, C-KEYS) deactivate the selected notes, or activate them again when every one of them is already deactivated (`note.deactivate`, one undo step); the item reads Activate Note(s) then (the wording is Beatlayer's own). A deactivated note keeps its place and values, is drawn grey (R4), and never plays: it is the note's `mute` ([midi-clips.md](midi-clips.md) R8). (S5, S9)
- **R19.** Right-clicking a key in the piano ruler selects that key track's notes first ([piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R26), so the menu's Deactivate Note(s) acts on them. (S5; Beatlayer's own)
- **R20.** The lanes' and the footer's actions are registered commands, run by the mouse, the keys and agents alike: `view.lanes` (shows and hides each lane and all of them, swaps a lane, sets Randomize's amounts) and `view.lane`, both in the read class; `note.set_value`, `note.ramp_value`, `note.adjust_value`, `note.randomize` and `note.deactivate` in the edit class. The query tool's clip reads the notes' values (`data.midi.notes`), and `view.state`'s `clip_view` reads the shown lanes, the active lane and the amounts. Assistive technology reads each lane as a group named for its value, each marker with its note's name and value, and each footer control with its name and value. (S15)

**Probability groups (WP-6.2)**

- **R21.** A probability group is two or more notes of one clip that roll their chance together, of one of two kinds, Play All or Play One. A note belongs to at most one group. A group has one chance, which every note in it holds as its own. (S4, S10)
- **R22.** Group Notes (Cmd-G, Ctrl-G on Windows, S-NOTE-031; Edit ▸ Group Notes, M-EDIT-014 with notes selected; the editor's context menu) puts the selected notes in a new group of the kind made last in this session, Play All until a kind is chosen (keeping it for the session is Beatlayer's own). The footer's Play All and Play One buttons put them in a new group of that kind and make it the kind made last. Notes leave the group they were in, and a group left with fewer than two notes ends. The new group's chance is that of its first note in the clip's order (Beatlayer's own), which every note of it takes. With fewer than two notes selected the command is refused. One undo step, `Group Notes`. (S4, S10)
- **R23.** Ungroup Notes (Cmd-Shift-G, S-NOTE-032; Edit ▸ Ungroup Notes, M-EDIT-015; the editor's context menu; the group marker's context menu, C-PGROUP; the footer's Ungroup button) takes the selected notes out of their groups, each keeping the group's chance as its own, and a group left with fewer than two notes ends; from the group marker's menu it ends that whole group. Play All and Play One in the marker's menu, the current kind ticked, change the group's kind. Each is one undo step, `Ungroup Notes` and `Change Group`. (S4)
- **R24.** In the Chance lane a group shows one marker in place of its notes' markers, at its first note's start (the place is Beatlayer's own): a diamond for Play All, a triangle for Play One. Dragging it sets the group's chance, as R5 says. Hovering a grouped note or the marker marks every note of the group. While notes are selected, the Status Bar names their group's kind, with an asterisk when they belong to groups of both kinds. Every grouped note shows R4's corner triangle whatever its chance. (S4, S10)
- **R25.** Any change to a grouped note's chance, by a marker, the footer, a key (R15, R16), Randomize (R13) or `note.set_value`, sets the chance of every note in its group, and Randomize draws once for each group. (S4; Beatlayer's own)
- **R26.** A pass of a clip is one play of its contents from its Start marker to its loop brace's end (or its End marker, or its end, with the loop off), each later repetition of its loop brace, and a new pass begins whenever the clip is launched or the transport starts. In a pass, when playback reaches the first note of a Play All group that the pass plays, the group's chance is rolled once: if it passes, every note of the group in that pass plays, each with its own velocity deviation ([midi-clips.md](midi-clips.md) R6); if not, none does. The roll holds until the pass ends. (S4, S12, S14)
- **R27.** For a Play One group, the chance is rolled once at the same point (R26); if it passes, one of the group's notes that the pass plays from that point on is chosen, each with the same chance of being chosen, and only it plays. (S4, S12; the even choice is Beatlayer's own)
- **R28.** A deactivated note of a group never plays and is never Play One's choice; a group whose notes are all deactivated plays nothing. (S4, S5; Beatlayer's own)
- **R29.** The rolls and choices use the clip player's seeded generator ([midi-clips.md](midi-clips.md) R5), so a set rendered from the same point with the same seed plays the same notes at any block size. Each clip's group state is made when its clip list is built, off the audio thread, so the audio thread neither allocates, locks nor blocks to roll, choose or forget a group. (S14, S15)
- **R30.** The set saves each group with its clip: its kind and its notes. A set saved before groups loads with none. A group with fewer than two notes, or whose notes' chances differ, and a note in two groups don't load: those notes load ungrouped. (S4; what a broken group does on load is Beatlayer's own)
- **R31.** Copies of grouped notes, by Copy and Paste, Duplicate or an Option-drag ([piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R28, R29), form new groups of the same kinds, one for each group they came from, so a copy rolls on its own; moving notes keeps their groups. Consolidate on a MIDI track ([audio-clip-editing.md](audio-clip-editing.md) R23) keeps each group, and each repetition of a looped clip it writes out holds groups of its own. A part made by Split or Chop stays in its note's group, and a note made by Join keeps the group of its first note (R39). Deleting grouped notes leaves the rest of their group, which ends below two notes. MIDI file export writes the notes without their groups. (S12; the copies', repetitions', parts' and export's groups are Beatlayer's own)
- **R32.** `note.group` (with the kind) and `note.ungroup` are registered commands in the edit class, run by the keys, the menus, the buttons and agents alike. The query tool's clip reads each note's group and each group's kind (`data.midi`), the readback both name. (S15)

**Split, Chop and Join (WP-6.3)**

- **R33.** With Draw Mode off, holding E and clicking a note splits it at the clicked tick (S-NOTE-006, I-DRAG-026); holding E and dragging across notes splits each note the drag crosses at the tick where it crosses it; and holding E and dragging sideways inside a note splits it and moves the split with the pointer until the button is released. The split lands at the pointer, or with Cmd held (Ctrl on Windows) at the nearest grid line. A split at or outside a note's start or end splits nothing. The gesture is one undo step, `Split Notes`, and Esc puts the notes back. (S6, S9, S10)
- **R34.** A note split or chopped becomes parts that together cover it: the first keeps its start, the last its end, and each keeps the note's pitch, velocity, release velocity, chance, deviation, deactivation and group (R31). The note's own pressure points ([midi-controller-data.md](midi-controller-data.md) R2), and any other expression a note carries, go with the part they fall in, counted from that part's start. (S6, S10, S14; what the parts keep is Beatlayer's own)
- **R35.** With no notes selected, Cmd-E (Ctrl-E; S-NOTE-003) splits every note the insert marker crosses at the insert marker, or, with a time selection, every note crossing one of its edges at that edge ([piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R23, R24); Split Note(s) in the Edit menu and the editor's context menu does the same and shows only while a note would be split. One undo step. (S6, S9)
- **R36.** With notes selected, Cmd-E chops them on the grid: each selected note is cut at every line of the editing grid that falls strictly inside it (Chop Note(s) on Grid, in the Edit menu and the editor's context menu). Then, while Cmd stays held, Up and Down (S-NOTE-047) chop the same notes again into equal parts, one more or one fewer than each note has, from 1, the note whole again, to 64, and with Shift too, the next or previous power of two. The chop and every change of its parts until Cmd is released are one undo step, `Chop Notes`. (S6, S9, S10; counting from each note's own parts and the limit of 64 are Beatlayer's own, the limit after the Chop tool's)
- **R37.** With notes selected, holding E with Option (Ctrl on Windows), or Cmd and E as S-NOTE-004 binds it, and dragging up or down on a selected note chops every selected note into equal parts, one part more for each 8 points dragged up and one fewer down, from 1 to 64; with Shift too (S-NOTE-005) the count moves through the powers of two. The Status Bar shows the count while dragging. The drag is one undo step, and Esc puts the notes back. (S6, S9, S10, S13: I-DRAG-039; the rate is Beatlayer's own)
- **R38.** Split and Chop act in the MPE tab as in the Notes tab once the MPE tab is built (WP-6.18). (S10)
- **R39.** Join Notes (Cmd-J, S-NOTE-007; the Edit menu and the editor's context menu) makes the selected notes of each pitch that has two or more of them one note, from the earliest start among them to the latest end, with the values of the earliest of them and its group; it keeps every joined note's pressure points, and any other expression they carry, at their places in time, and where two overlap the later note's points win. A pitch with one selected note is left alone, and with nothing to join the command is refused. One undo step, `Join Notes`. (S6, S9, S10; the values kept and the overlap are Beatlayer's own)
- **R40.** Fit to Time Range (Cmd-Option-J, Ctrl-Alt-J on Windows; S-NOTE-008; Edit ▸ Fit to Time Range, M-EDIT-034; the editor's context menu) gives every selected note the time selection's start and end: the span of the selected notes, or the time selection Enter selected them from ([piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R24). With no notes selected it is refused. One undo step, `Fit to Time Range`. (S7, S9, S10)
- **R41.** Split Clip (Cmd-Shift-E, Ctrl-Shift-E on Windows; S-NOTE-036; the editor's context menu) splits the Arrangement clip the editor shows at the time selection's start and end, each that falls strictly inside the clip's span, as [arrangement-editing.md](arrangement-editing.md) R8 splits it (`arrangement.split`, with the clip and the song positions of the edges), and selects the part inside. With a Session clip, or no time selection, it is refused. (S9, S12)

**Note stretch markers (WP-6.3)**

- **R42.** While two or more notes, or a time range, are selected, two stretch markers show in the strip under the editor ruler (L-CLIP-020), at the start and end of the selection: the time selection ([piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R24). (S8)
- **R43.** Dragging a stretch marker sideways moves that end of the selection and scales the notes it holds about the other marker: the selected notes, or with none selected the notes that start inside the time selection. Each note's start and end keep their place in proportion to the span, so starts and lengths scale together. The marker moves freely until it reaches the nearest grid line on either side of where it began, and from there snaps to grid lines; with Cmd held (Alt on Windows), or the grid off, it doesn't snap. Dragged past the other marker it mirrors the notes' order in time. The drag is one undo step, `Stretch Notes`, and Esc puts the notes back. Each note's expression envelopes scale with it ([mpe.md](mpe.md) R4; manual 12.2). (S8, S13: I-DRAG-040)
- **R44.** With the pointer between the markers a pseudo marker shows under it. Dragging it moves that point in time while both markers stay: what lies between the first marker and the point is scaled to end at the point's new place, and what lies between the point and the second marker to start there. It snaps as R43 says, and is one undo step. (S8)
- **R45.** A stretch keeps every note at least one tick long and starting inside the clip's contents; a drag stops at that limit, and `note.stretch` asking past it is refused. (S8; Beatlayer's own)
- **R46.** A stretch moves the breakpoints of the clip's linked envelopes ([clip-envelopes.md](clip-envelopes.md) R2) that lie in the stretched span by the same mapping, and leaves unlinked envelopes alone; a note's own pressure points scale with it. (S8, S14; the pressure is Beatlayer's own)
- **R47.** The note operations are registered commands in the edit class, run by the gestures, the keys, the menus and agents alike, with the notes named or the selection: `note.split` (at given ticks), `note.chop` (on the grid, or into a number of parts), `note.join`, `note.fit_to_time` (to a range, or the time selection) and `note.stretch` (from one range to another, with an optional point moved inside it); Split Clip runs `arrangement.split`. The query tool's clip reads the result (`data.midi.notes`, `envelopes`). (S15)
