## The behaviour

The detail area along the bottom of the window holds two views: the Clip View, which edits the selected clip, and the Device View, which shows the selected track's devices. Since Live 12 each has its own switch, so the detail area shows one of them, both, or neither. With both showing, the Clip View sits above the Device View and the main view gives up the room. The Status Bar has a toggle and a selector for each view: the toggle shows or hides its view, Option-click on either toggle shows both, and a click on a selector switches to that view alone. Shift+Tab or F12 switches between the views when one shows, and moves the keyboard focus between them when both do. Cmd+Option+L hides the detail views and brings them back.

The Clip View is made taller by dragging the detail area's top edge, and dragging the edge to the bottom closes it. Expand Clip View (Cmd+Option+E) gives the Clip View its largest height and puts it back.

Inside the Clip View, the clip's panels sit at its left under their selector row, the left group's panel and the right group's side by side, or one above the other, with the editor to their right. Dragging the panels' right edge, or the View menu's three Arrange Clip View Panels items, chooses between the two, and Automatically chooses by the Clip View's height. Double-clicking the clip's title bar, or Fold in its menu, folds the panels away and gives the editor the room.

This spec covers the detail area of the main window: which views it shows, where they sit, their heights, their switches, their keys and the keyboard focus between them; and inside the Clip View, how its panels are arranged and folded and the panel selector's keys (R19 to R26). What each panel holds is in its own spec ([clip-properties.md](clip-properties.md), [launch-quantisation.md](launch-quantisation.md), [midi-tools.md](midi-tools.md), [sample-editor.md](sample-editor.md)). What each view shows is in its own spec: [piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md), [sample-editor.md](sample-editor.md) and [clip-envelopes.md](clip-envelopes.md) for the Clip View, [device-chains.md](device-chains.md) and [racks.md](racks.md) for the Device View, and [session-view.md](session-view.md) R26 for the Scene View. The second window's detail area is in [second-window.md](second-window.md).

## Rules

**Which views show**

- **R1.** The detail area shows the Clip View, the Device View, both, or neither. Each view has its own switch: `view.toggle_clip_view` (Cmd+Option+3, S-VIEW-015; View ▸ Clip View, M-VIEW-010; the Status Bar's Clip View toggle, L-STAT-004) and `view.toggle_device_view` (Cmd+Option+4, S-VIEW-016; View ▸ Device View, M-VIEW-011; the Device View toggle, L-STAT-006). Each shows or hides its own view and leaves the other as it is, so with the Device View showing, switching the Clip View on shows both. Each menu item is ticked while its view shows. (S1, S2, S4, S5, S6)
- **R2.** Option-click (Alt-click) on either toggle (S-VIEW-005, I-CLICK-027) shows both views, through `view.show_detail_views`. With both already showing it changes nothing (Beatlayer's own). (S4, S5, S6)
- **R3.** A click anywhere on a view's selector shows that view and hides the other: the Clip View selector (L-STAT-005), which reads the name of the clip the Clip View shows, and the Device View selector (L-STAT-007), which reads the selected track's name. A toggle is drawn on, in the theme's accent with its triangle pointing up, while its view shows, and off, with its triangle pointing sideways, while it doesn't. (S4, S6)
- **R4.** Double-clicking a clip in the Arrangement or in a Session slot (I-CLICK-005) selects it and then does what a click on the Clip View selector does; double-clicking a track's title bar in the Arrangement, the Session View or a mixer (I-CLICK-006) selects the track and then does what a click on the Device View selector does. Clicking a Session track's status field while the track plays a clip (L-SES-014) selects that clip and shows it in the Clip View the same way. With both views showing, none of these hides the other view (Beatlayer's own). (S1, S2, S6)
- **R5.** View ▸ Toggle Clip/Device View (M-VIEW-012), Shift+Tab or F12 (S-VIEW-004), `view.switch_detail_view`: with one view showing, shows the other in its place; with both showing, moves the keyboard focus to the Clip View's editor if the Device View has it, and to the Device View otherwise; with neither showing, shows the view that showed alone last, the Device View in a new session (Beatlayer's own). Shift+Tab does this only while Use Tab Key to Move Focus is off ([app-shell.md](app-shell.md) R11, [accessibility.md](accessibility.md) R3); F12 does it whatever the setting. With the second window open, the keys move a view between the windows instead ([second-window.md](second-window.md) R8). (S4, S5)
- **R6.** View ▸ Hide Device View (M-VIEW-014), Cmd+Option+L (S-VIEW-022), `view.toggle_detail_area`: hides whichever views show, and run again shows the same ones again; with neither showing and nothing hidden this way, it shows the Device View (Beatlayer's own). The menu item reads Hide Device View, Hide Clip View or Hide Clip and Device View for what it would hide, and Show Device View, Show Clip View or Show Clip and Device View for what it would bring back (Beatlayer's own words, beside the label read in Live with the Device View showing). (S4, S5, S6)

**Where they sit, and their heights**

- **R7.** One view alone sits right of the Info View ([info-view.md](info-view.md) R1), or across the detail area's whole width while the Info View hides ([info-view.md](info-view.md) R2), its bottom on the detail area's bottom edge. The Device View alone is 188 points tall at 100% (L-WIN-006, L-DEV-001); the Clip View alone is 225 points tall until it's resized (L-WIN-006, L-CLIP-001). The main view and the browser column stop 8 points above the detail area (L-WIN-009). (S6)
- **R8.** With both views showing, the Device View keeps its place and height at the bottom, the Clip View sits above it at the Clip View's own height, with 8 points between them (L-DEV-002; the gap is Beatlayer's own, L-WIN-009's margin between panes), and the detail area is the two heights and the gap. The main view and the browser column give up the room, stopping 8 points above the Clip View. (S2, S6)
- **R9.** With neither view showing, the main view runs down to the detail area's bottom edge; the Info View, while it shows, keeps the detail area's bottom left at the Device View's height, and the browser column stops 8 points above it (Beatlayer's own). (S6)
- **R10.** Dragging the detail area's top edge (I-DRAG-028) while the Clip View shows sets the Clip View's height, from 100 points to the height that leaves the main view 80 points (both Beatlayer's own), and the Device View's height doesn't change (Beatlayer's own). A drag that ends lower than the Clip View's smallest height closes the Clip View, leaving the Device View if it showed too; with only the Device View showing, a drag of the top edge ending lower than half the Device View's height closes it, and otherwise the edge goes back (Beatlayer's own). A view closed this way opens again at the height it had before the drag. Each frame of the drag runs `view.set_detail_height` (`points`, the Clip View's height at 100%), which is no undo step. (S2, S3, S6)
- **R11.** View ▸ Expand Clip View (M-VIEW-013), Cmd+Option+E with the keyboard focus in the Clip View (S-NOTE-030), `view.expand_clip_view`: gives the Clip View its largest height (R10's), and run again gives back the height it had before. The menu item is enabled while the Clip View shows, and ticked while it's expanded (Beatlayer's own). With both views showing, the Device View keeps its height below the expanded Clip View. Dragging the top edge while expanded ends the expansion at the dragged height, and hiding the Clip View ends it. (S3, S5, S6)
- **R12.** Every height and gap in R7 to R11 is in points at 100%, scaled by the interface zoom ([interface-zoom.md](interface-zoom.md) R1). Where the window is too short for the Clip View's height and an 80-point main view, the Clip View gets less, down to its smallest; below that, the main view gets less (Beatlayer's own). (S7)
- **R13.** A selected scene's Scene View ([session-view.md](session-view.md) R26) takes the place of the top view showing: the Clip View's place when the Clip View shows, the Device View's otherwise, at that view's height, and with neither view showing it waits until one shows; the views' switches stay as they were, and selecting a clip or a track puts the view back (Beatlayer's own). (S7)

**The keyboard focus**

- **R14.** Navigate ▸ Clip View, Option+3 (Alt+3; S-FOCUS-004), `focus.clip_view`, gives the keyboard focus to the Clip View's editor, and Navigate ▸ Device View, Option+4 (S-FOCUS-005), `focus.device_view`, to the Device View; each first shows its view, as its toggle would, if it's hidden (Beatlayer's own). Clicking in either view gives it the focus (I-FOCUS-001). (S5, S6)
- **R15.** With both views showing, each is an area of its own for the keymap ([app-shell.md](app-shell.md) R11): the contexts of the Clip View and its editors (`clip-view`, `note-editor`, `note-selection`, `sample-editor`, and `envelope` for its Envelopes tab) reach the Clip View only while the focus is in it, and `device-view` and `rack-chains` reach the Device View only while the focus is in that. With Outline View in Focus on, the outline goes round the view that has the focus ([accessibility.md](accessibility.md) R7). (S4, S7)

**State, commands and assistive technology**

- **R16.** Which views show, which showed alone last, what Cmd+Option+L hid, the Clip View's height and whether it's expanded are view state: none is part of the set or an undo step, and each lasts the session; a new session starts with the Device View alone, as layout.md's default state has it (Beatlayer's own). (S6, S7)
- **R17.** `view.toggle_clip_view`, `view.toggle_device_view`, `view.show_detail_views`, `view.switch_detail_view`, `view.toggle_detail_area`, `view.expand_clip_view`, `view.set_detail_height`, `focus.clip_view` and `focus.device_view` are registered commands with schemas and descriptions, all `read` (they move the view), each returning the detail area's state; `view.state` reports it under `detail`: whether each view shows, the view that showed alone last, what Cmd+Option+L hid, the Clip View's height, whether it's expanded, and which view has the focus ([app-shell.md](app-shell.md) R23). Every gesture on the toggles, the selectors and the top edge runs one of them. (S7)
- **R18.** The accessibility tree names the views as regions, "Clip View" and "Device View", in their order top to bottom; the toggles as buttons named Show/Hide Clip View and Show/Hide Device View, with `on` or `off`; each selector as a button named for what it reads (the clip's or the track's name); and the detail area's top edge, while the Clip View shows, as a splitter whose value is the Clip View's height in points, which a screen reader's increment and decrement move by 10 points (Beatlayer's own step) through `view.set_detail_height`. (S7)

**The clip panels (WP-6.72)**

- **R19.** Arranged horizontally, the clip's panels sit at the Clip View's left under the panel selector row (L-CLIP-003): the left group's panel ([clip-properties.md](clip-properties.md) R1) and the right group's beside it ([midi-tools.md](midi-tools.md) R1), at the places L-CLIP-004 and L-CLIP-006 give, with the editor to their right. Arranged vertically (L-CLIP-009), the right group's panel sits under the left group's, both as wide as the left group's panel, each under its own part of the selector row, and the editor takes the width the panels give up; where the two are taller than the Clip View, the panel column scrolls (the column's width and its scrolling are Beatlayer's own). (S8)
- **R20.** View ▸ Arrange Clip View Panels Automatically, Horizontally and Vertically (M-VIEW-015 to M-VIEW-017), and the same items in the clip title bar's menu ([session-context-menus.md](session-context-menus.md) R4), choose the arrangement, the one in use ticked: `clip_view.arrange_panels` with `automatic`, `horizontal` or `vertical`. Automatically arranges the panels vertically while the Clip View is at least 400 points tall and horizontally while it's shorter (Beatlayer's own height), following the Clip View's height as it's resized or expanded (R10, R11). A new install arranges them Automatically. (S8)
- **R21.** Dragging the panels' right edge, where they meet the editor (I-DRAG-029), to the left by more than half the right group's panel width chooses Vertically, and dragging it back to the right by as much chooses Horizontally; a shorter drag springs back (Beatlayer's own distance). The drag runs `clip_view.arrange_panels` once, when it ends. (S8)
- **R22.** The arrangement is an application preference, kept in the preferences file, so it holds for every clip, every set and the next start; it isn't part of the set or an undo step, and `clip_view.arrange_panels` is `edit`-class as a preference trivially changed back. (S8)
- **R23.** Double-clicking the Clip View's title bar (I-CLICK-008), or Fold in its menu ([session-context-menus.md](session-context-menus.md) R4), ticked while they're folded, folds the panels: the selector row and both panels hide, the title bar stays, and the editor takes the Clip View's whole width; doing it again unfolds them (what the folded Clip View shows is Beatlayer's own). Folding is view state for the session, kept for every clip the Clip View shows, no undo step, through `clip_view.fold_panels` (`read`, with `folded` to say which). (S8)
- **R24.** Navigate ▸ Clip Panels, Option+Shift+P (M-NAV-011, S-FOCUS-010), gives the keyboard focus to the panels, as [clip-properties.md](clip-properties.md) R11 says, and Option+8 (S-FOCUS-009) to the shown panel of the group the focus was last in, at its first control; with the panels folded either key unfolds them first (Beatlayer's own). In the selector row, Left and Right move between its buttons and Enter or Space shows the focused button's panel (Beatlayer's own). (S8)
- **R25.** The accessibility tree holds the selector row as two tab lists, the left group's and the right group's, each tab named for its panel and selected while it shows, and each panel as a tab panel named for it; the panels' right edge, while the panels show, is a splitter whose increment and decrement choose Horizontally and Vertically. (S7, S8)
- **R26.** `clip_view.arrange_panels` and `clip_view.fold_panels` are registered commands with schemas and descriptions, run by ID through the agent API, and `view.state`'s `clip_view` reports the arrangement chosen, the one in use (Automatically resolved), and whether the panels are folded ([app-shell.md](app-shell.md) R23). Every width and height here is in points at 100%, scaled by the interface zoom ([interface-zoom.md](interface-zoom.md) R1). (S7)
