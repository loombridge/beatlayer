# The pitch and frequency shifter

- **Status:** implemented (every rule below has a passing test; the display's cells and a layout that shows only the controls the chosen mode uses are not built yet)
- **Work packages:** WP-5.27. The framework it sits on is WP-2.5 ([the native device framework](device-framework.md)); its LFO, envelope follower and seeded random source are WP-5.42's (device framework R26 to R29); its MIDI sidechain takes the notes and channel messages WP-5.44 added ([routing](routing.md) R32 to R35)
- **Parity-matrix row:** Audio effects: Shifter (the second M5 row)
- **Checked against:** the Live 12 reference manual, chapter 29 "Live Audio Effect Reference", sections 29.35 to 29.35.6 "Shifter", read on 18 October 2026; the Live 12 release notes for 12.2 (11 June 2025), 12.4 (5 May 2026) and 12.4.3 (14 July 2026); the ranges, defaults and algorithms the manual does not give are Beatlayer's own

## The behaviour

Shifter shifts its input in one of three ways. **Pitch** moves the pitch by a number of semitones and cents, keeping the sound's length, with a Window that sets how long a grain it works on. **Freq** moves every frequency by the same number of Hz, which turns a harmonic sound inharmonic; a few Hz make phasing and large shifts a metallic tone. **Ring** multiplies the signal by a sine of that many Hz, which gives the sum and the difference of each frequency and the input's own is gone; Drive, which only this mode has, distorts the result. Spread detunes the two channels from each other, the left up and the right down, and Wide turns the right over. A delay with feedback and a Tone filter in its feedback path follows the shift, free in Hz or synced to the tempo.

An LFO and an envelope follower move the shift, each by its own amount, the amount in semitones in Pitch mode and in Hz in the other two. The LFO has ten waves, a rate in Hz or in beat divisions, Phase or Spin to differ between the channels, a duty cycle, and, for the two random waves, the one stereo control each has (Random uses Phase alone and Random S&H uses Width alone). Dry/Wet balances the result against the dry signal.

Pitch can come from the device itself, from Coarse and Fine, or from MIDI: the device's MIDI sidechain listens to the notes of another MIDI track, and the interval of the note held from a reference note sets the shift, with a Glide between notes and the track's pitch bend over a range. The sidechain's parameters are in the device's expanded part; its source is chosen in the left strip, as a sidechain's is.

This spec covers the device only. Where it sits in a chain and the frame around it are [the device chains spec](device-chains.md) and [the device framework spec](device-framework.md); the MIDI sidechain's input, its source and the notes it hands the device are [routing](routing.md)'s (R32 to R35).

## Rules

- **R1.** Beatlayer ships Shifter, an audio-effect device type with a MIDI sidechain input (routing R32), with Live's name and its own layout. Every parameter has a descriptor and a place in the layout: the shift's controls, the delay's, the LFO's and the envelope follower's with Dry/Wet in the body, and Pitch Source, Glide and PB in the expanded part, which the title bar's toggle shows (device chains R34). It has no display cell and no factory presets; it has no audio sidechain. (S4, S6, S7, S10)
- **R2.** Mode is Pitch, Freq or Ring. The parameters are split by the mode that uses them, because a descriptor's range and unit are fixed: **Coarse** (−48 to 48 semitones, whole), **Fine** (−50 to 50 cents) and **Window** (2 to 20 ms) are Pitch mode's; **Shift** (−5,000 to 5,000 Hz) is Freq mode's shift and Ring mode's modulation frequency; **Drive** (0 to 100 %) is Ring mode's. A parameter another mode uses is left as it is and does nothing. (S1, S3, S10)
- **R3.** Pitch mode reads the input through two heads that each run through a delay line for one Window, half a run apart, and crossfades them with a raised-sine window that sums to one. A head's delay moves by `1 − r` frames each frame, where `r = 2^(semitones/12)` and the semitones are Coarse plus Fine plus the modulation (R8, R9) and Spread (R6), so a steady tone leaves at `r` times its frequency. When a head ends, the next begins at the delay, within half a Window of the nominal one, where its recent output best matches the other head's (a normalised cross-correlation, refined to a fraction of a frame), so a steady tone keeps its pitch through the changes of head: a sine from 220 Hz to 6 kHz comes out within 1 cent of `r` times its frequency at a Window of 10 ms or more (and within 1 cent at 2 ms from 1 kHz). A tone lower than a few periods to a Window is shifted by the same ratio on average but not to the cent. At a shift of nothing the signal is the input delayed by the latency (R7), bit for bit; the device moves to it over 10 ms as the shift reaches 0, and leaves it at once. Pitch mode ignores Drive and Shift. (S1, S3, S10)
- **R4.** Freq mode is single-sideband modulation: with the input `x` delayed by the latency and its Hilbert transform `h`, the output is `x·cos φ − h·sin φ`, where `φ` is the phase of a carrier of Shift plus the modulation and Spread in Hz, accumulated frame by frame. The Hilbert transform is a Kaiser-windowed (β = 8) FIR of twice the latency plus one taps, odd taps only. A 1 kHz sine at Shift +100 Hz comes out at 1,100 Hz within 0.1 Hz and at the input's level within 0.1 dB, its other image (900 Hz) at least 60 dB down. A negative shift moves down. (S1, S3, S5, S10)
- **R5.** Ring mode is the input delayed by the latency times `sin φ`, with `φ` as R4 has it. A 1 kHz sine at Shift 100 Hz comes out as 900 Hz and 1,100 Hz at equal level (within 0.1 dB), each half the input's amplitude, and the input's own 1 kHz at least 60 dB down. Drive `d` from 0 to 100 % blends the product `p` toward `tanh(g·p)/tanh(g)` with `g = 1 + 15·d`: the product itself at 0 %, so the distortion of a clean product is none at 0 % and rises with Drive. (S3, S5, S10)
- **R6.** Spread from 0 to 100 % detunes the left channel up and the right channel down, each by `Spread × 50` cents in Pitch mode or `Spread × 10` Hz in Freq and Ring modes; Wide inverts the right channel's Spread, so both channels go the same way. At Spread 0 Wide changes nothing. (S1, S5, S10)
- **R7.** Every mode delays the dry signal and the shifted one by the same latency, which the device reports (delay compensation R1): 10 ms, `round(0.010 × sample rate)` frames (480 at 48 kHz), the same in every mode and at every setting, so the heads' run, the Hilbert transformer's centre and the dry path line up, and Dry/Wet mixes aligned signals. Dry/Wet from 0 to 100 % balances the dry signal against the shifted signal with its repeats (R10); at 0 % the device passes the dry signal delayed by the latency. (S3, S8, S10)
- **R8.** The LFO moves each channel's shift by `LFO Amount × 12` semitones in Pitch mode or `LFO Amount × 1,000` Hz in Freq and Ring modes, times its value, so at 100 % a shift moves up to 12 semitones or 1,000 Hz either way; a negative amount turns the wave over. Its waves, in the chooser's order, are Sine, Triangle, Triangle Analog, Triangle 8, Triangle 16, Saw Up, Saw Down, Rectangle, Random and Random S&H, as device framework R26 has them. Its rate is in Hz (0.01 to 40) or in a beat division (1/32 to 8 bars), the latter synced as framework R27 has it, with Offset in degrees against the beat grid. Phase puts the right channel's LFO a number of degrees ahead and Spin makes its rate a percentage faster; Random uses Phase alone, whatever the stereo choice, and Random S&H uses Width alone: the right channel's value is the left's times `1 − 2 × Width`, so the channels match at 0 % and mirror each other at 100 %. Duty Cycle is the wave's duty cycle, as framework R26 has it, and does nothing to the random waves. Random waves follow the device's seed (framework R29). (S2, S8, S10)
- **R9.** With Env Fol on, the envelope follower follows each channel's input level (the absolute value, up to 1) with Attack and Release as time constants, and moves the shift by Env Amount semitones in Pitch mode or Env Amount Hz in Freq and Ring modes, times the level. A step in the input moves the shift along the time constants' curve: the render of Ring mode on a steady input matches a model of the follower in continuous time to within 0.001. (S2, S8, S10)
- **R10.** With Delay on, the shifted signal goes into a delay line with the repeats fed back, and the repeats are added to the shifted signal. The time is `1 / Delay Time` seconds for Delay Time in Hz (1 to 1,000), or a beat division (1/32 to a bar, with dotted and triplet divisions) at the tempo of the stretch's first frame; it is cut to 4 seconds, and moves to a new time over 20 ms. Feedback from 0 to 95 % is the share of the repeats that goes back in, through a one-pole low-pass that Tone from 0 to 100 % lowers from 20 kHz to 500 Hz, exponentially, and is no filter at 0 %. The first repeat is the shifted signal delayed, whole, and its time lands on the frame (a quarter note at 120 BPM is 24,000 frames); later repeats take the feedback path and lose their highs with Tone. With Delay off the repeats already in the line play out and nothing goes in. (S1, S5, S10)
- **R11.** With Pitch Source MIDI, the shift in Pitch mode is the pitch of the source's notes, in semitones from the reference note C3 (key 60), plus the pitch bend, in place of Coarse and Fine. The newest note held sets the pitch, from the frame of its note-on; releasing it returns to the newest of those still held, and releasing the last leaves the pitch where it is. Glide from 0 to 2,000 ms is the time the pitch takes to move linearly to a new note's, in a whole number of frames; at 0 it moves at once. A pitch bend message from −1 to 1 moves the pitch by that fraction of PB, the bend range from 0 to 24 semitones, at once; the device starts with no note and no bend, a shift of nothing. With No Input the device hears no notes. The Modulation and Spread still apply, and Freq and Ring modes ignore Pitch Source. (S4, S8, S10)
- **R12.** Every block runs without allocating, locking or blocking, and a render is bit-identical at block sizes 1, 64, 512 and 4,096 and run after run, in every mode, with the delay, the LFO's random waves and the envelope follower on. (S8, S9)
- **R13.** Every parameter can be inserted, set by ID and read back through the agent API (`device.add` or `device.insert` with a MIDI sidechain, `device.set_param`, `device.parameters`), is automatable and modulatable (rule G2), and sets its MIDI source with `device.set_midi_sidechain_source` (routing R35). (S7)
- **R14.** A set with the device saves and reopens with every parameter and the MIDI source. (S7)
