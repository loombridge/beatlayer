## The behaviour

An automation lane is edited like the track lane above it. Dragging across a lane's background selects a span of time in it, and the breakpoints inside are selected with it: dragging any of them moves them all, and dragging a segment inside the span moves the whole stretch of envelope. Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate and Delete then act on the envelope alone, leaving the clips and the other envelopes as they are, and a copied stretch of envelope can be pasted onto another parameter. A time selection's context menu inserts ready-made shapes (waveforms, ramps, an envelope) and simplifies a recorded envelope to fewer breakpoints; handles round the selection stretch it in value or in time, or skew it.

By default an Arrangement clip's automation goes with it: moving, copying, cutting or deleting clips carries their tracks' envelopes over the same span. The Lock Envelopes switch above the track headers keeps envelopes where they are in the song instead.

Everything can be done from the keyboard too. In an automation lane, or the Clip View's Envelope Editor, Left and Right move the insert marker along the envelope, which shows its value there; Enter makes or selects a breakpoint at the insert marker; the arrows then move it and change its value, a typed number sets it, Enter keeps the change and Esc drops it. Option with Up and Down changes which parameter the lane shows.

This spec covers these edits in the Arrangement's automation lanes, the keys in both the lanes and the Envelope Editor, and Lock Envelopes. The envelopes themselves, their breakpoints and curves, the lanes and their choosers, and recording are [automation.md](automation.md)'s; the Envelope Editor's own rules are [clip-envelopes.md](clip-envelopes.md)'s; the …Time commands, which move every envelope whatever Lock Envelopes says, are [time-editing.md](time-editing.md)'s. Undo steps named in code type, such as `Insert Shape`, are Beatlayer's own names.

## Rules

**Selections in automation lanes (WP-6.25)**

- **R1.** Clicking an automation lane away from its envelope's breakpoints and segments selects that lane, and its track, and places the insert marker as a click in a track lane does ([arrangement-view.md](arrangement-view.md) R12). Dragging across lanes' backgrounds selects the time dragged over on the lanes dragged across, automation lanes and track lanes alike; Shift-click and Shift-drag extend it (I-DRAG-047). The time selection names the lanes it covers, each a track's own lane or its automation lane. Selecting is view state, not an undo step. (S1, S11)
- **R2.** An automation lane's breakpoints from the time selection's start up to, not including, its end are selected (the end's exclusion is Beatlayer's own) and drawn in the selection colour. Dragging any selected breakpoint moves every selected breakpoint by the same time and the same share of its parameter's travel, each kept within its range, as one undo step; a breakpoint the moved ones pass over is removed (I-DRAG-048). (S1, S10, S11)
- **R3.** A breakpoint's drag, one or many, snaps in time to the editing grid and to the positions of the breakpoints next to it, Cmd (Alt on Windows) doing the opposite of the grid's setting (S-ENV-006); dragging past a neighbour removes the neighbour where [automation.md](automation.md) R15's single drag refused the move; Shift keeps the drag to the axis it starts along and makes a vertical one finer (S-ENV-002). (S1, S9)
- **R4.** Clicking within 4 points of a segment but not on it, or Shift-clicking on it, selects the segment: its two breakpoints (I-CLICK-044). Dragging a selected segment moves its two breakpoints as R2 moves selected ones. Dragging a segment that lies inside the time selection first adds breakpoints at the selection's edges, at the envelope's values there, then moves every breakpoint between them, so the stretch of envelope inside the selection moves and the envelope outside stays. Each drag is one undo step. (S1)
- **R5.** With the time selection in automation lanes, Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate and Delete act on those lanes' envelopes only; the clips and the track's other envelopes stay as they are. Copy puts on the clipboard each selected lane's envelope over the span: its shape (positions from the span's start, values as positions on the parameter's travel, bends) and the parameter it came from. Delete removes the breakpoints inside the span and keeps the envelope's values at the span's edges with a breakpoint at each, so it runs straight across the span and is unchanged outside it. Cut copies, then deletes. Paste writes the clipboard's shape into the selected automation lanes, in the order they were copied, from the time selection's start or the insert marker, replacing each envelope over the shape's length; each value lands at the same position on the target parameter's travel, so a shape pastes onto another parameter. Duplicate writes a copy straight after the span and makes the copy the time selection. Each is one undo step; Cut and Delete are `destructive`. How Delete leaves the span, and pasting by travel position, are Beatlayer's own. (S6)
- **R6.** In the Arrangement, Option (Alt on Windows) held with Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate or Delete (S-EDIT-016) applies the command, as R5 does, to the automation lanes shown under the tracks of the time selection, even when the selection is in the tracks' own lanes (Beatlayer's reading of S9). (S9)
- **R7.** These are registered commands with schemas, descriptions and permission classes, run headless with explicit tracks, parameters and spans in ticks or as bars, beats and sixteenths, or with those left out on the selection: `automation.move_breakpoints` (several breakpoints by a time and a share of the travel), `automation.paste` and `automation.duplicate` are `edit`; `automation.cut` and `automation.delete_range` are `destructive`; `automation.copy` and `automation.select_segment` are `read`. `arrangement.select_time` takes the lanes a selection covers, and `automation.set_envelope` (the document's own) writes a whole envelope. The query tool's track (`automation`) reads them back, and `arrangement.state` reports the time selection's lanes. (S11)

**Lock Envelopes (WP-6.25)**

- **R8.** Lock Envelopes is the switch above the track headers (L-ARR-010) and Options ▸ Lock Envelopes (M-OPT-026), ticked while it's on: `song.set_lock_envelopes`, `edit`, read back by the query tool's song (`settings.lock_envelopes`). It's off in a new set and in a set saved before it, the set saves it, and each change is one undo step (both Beatlayer's own). (S5, S10)
- **R9.** With Lock Envelopes off, an edit of Arrangement clips carries their tracks' automation over the clips' spans. Moving clips in time (dragging, nudging, `arrangement.move_clips`) moves each track's envelopes over each moved clip's span with it, replacing the envelopes over the span it lands on, and leaves the span it left as R5's Delete leaves a span. Copying clips (Option-drag, Duplicate) copies the envelopes over their spans in the same way, and Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate and Delete of clips or of a time selection in track lanes ([arrangement-editing.md](arrangement-editing.md) R9 to R11) take the selected tracks' envelopes over the span with the clips. What moves is the envelope's shape over the span, with breakpoints at the span's edges holding its values there. A clip moved to another track takes the envelopes of the mixer controls and sends that track also has; a device's parameters stay on the track they're on. Resizing and splitting clips leave automation as it is. Each edit stays one undo step. The handling of the span left behind, and of a move between tracks, are Beatlayer's own. (S5, S6)
- **R10.** With Lock Envelopes on, clip edits leave every envelope where it is in song time. The …Time commands move envelopes whether it's on or off ([time-editing.md](time-editing.md) R14; Beatlayer's own). (S5)

**Shapes, stretch and skew, and Simplify Envelope (WP-6.25b)**

- **R11.** Right-clicking a time selection in an automation lane opens a menu (C-TIME-021) that holds the automation shapes, as two rows of buttons: Sine, Triangle, Sawtooth, Inverse Sawtooth and Square; then Rise, Fall, Rise To, Fall To and ADSR (the names Beatlayer's own, read by assistive technology). Choosing one writes it into that lane's envelope over the time selection's span, or with no time selection over one grid step from the insert marker, replacing the envelope there: one undo step, `Insert Shape`. (S4, S10)
- **R12.** The waveforms run one cycle across the span between the bottom and the top of the parameter's range: Sine from the middle, rising first; Triangle from the bottom to the top at the span's middle and back; Sawtooth from the bottom rising to the top; Inverse Sawtooth from the top falling to the bottom; Square at the top for the first half and the bottom for the second. Their breakpoints follow the shape within 1% of the parameter's travel at every tick. The envelope outside the span is unchanged, and where its value differs from the shape's at an edge it steps over one tick. The phases and the 1% are Beatlayer's own. (S4)
- **R13.** The ramps and ADSR join the envelope outside the span: Rise and Fall go straight from the envelope's value at the span's start to the top or the bottom of the range at its end; Rise To and Fall To go straight from the bottom or the top at the span's start to the envelope's value at its end; ADSR goes from the value at the span's start to the top at an eighth of the span, down to the middle of the range at a quarter, holds there until three quarters, and goes to the value at the span's end. The shapes' exact forms are Beatlayer's own. (S4)
- **R14.** Hovering a time selection in an automation lane shows eight handles on its box, the box being the span across the parameter's whole range (Beatlayer's own): the top, bottom, left and right centres and the four corners (I-DRAG-049). Dragging one shows the box's new outline as it goes; each drag is one undo step, and Esc during it puts the envelope and the time selection back. Where a breakpoint is under the pointer too, the breakpoint is grabbed instead (Beatlayer's own). (S2)
- **R15.** Dragging the top or bottom handle moves that edge of the box, not past the other edge: every breakpoint in the span moves to the same position between the new edges as it had between the old, and a value past the parameter's range is clipped to it. Shift makes the drag finer. (S2)
- **R16.** Dragging the left or right handle moves that end of the span, on the editing grid, Cmd (Alt on Windows) doing the opposite of the grid's setting: every breakpoint inside moves to the same place in the new span, and the time selection follows it. A breakpoint outside the span that the moving end passes over is removed; with Shift, the breakpoints beyond that end move with it instead, keeping their distance from it (Beatlayer's reading of S2). (S2)
- **R17.** Dragging a corner handle up or down moves that end of the box's top or bottom edge, tilting the edge: every breakpoint in the span moves to the same position between the bottom and top edges as they run at its own time, straight between their ends. Shift makes the drag finer. Option (Alt on Windows) held with any handle moves the opposite one the opposite way at the same time: the bottom for the top, the left for the right, and for a corner the other corner of the same edge (that last Beatlayer's reading of S2). (S2)
- **R18.** With a time selection in an automation lane, Simplify Envelope in its context menu (C-TIME-021) and in the Edit menu (M-EDIT-039) replaces the lane's breakpoints inside the span with as few as keep the envelope within 0.5% of the parameter's travel of what it was at every tick of the span, using curved segments ([automation.md](automation.md) R12) where one follows the shape; the values at the span's edges and the envelope outside it are kept, and no breakpoint is added. One undo step, `Simplify Envelope`; disabled with no time selection in an automation lane. The 0.5% is Beatlayer's own. (S3, S10)
- **R19.** These are registered commands, run headless with explicit tracks, parameters and spans, or with those left out on the selection: `automation.insert_shape` (a shape), `automation.stretch_values` (the new bottom and top edges as shares of the travel), `automation.stretch_time` (the span's new start and end, and whether the breakpoints beyond an end move with it), `automation.skew` (an edge, and where it runs at the span's start and end) and `automation.simplify`, each `edit`, read back by the query tool's track (`automation`). The undo steps are named `Insert Shape`, `Stretch Automation Values`, `Stretch Automation in Time`, `Skew Automation` and `Simplify Envelope`; `Stretch Automation Values`, `Stretch Automation in Time` and `Skew Automation` are Beatlayer's own names. The model functions behind them take the parameter's travel as a trait, so the Clip View's Envelope Editor can use them on the modulation scale ([clip-envelopes.md](clip-envelopes.md) R12, R13). (S11)

**The keyboard (WP-6.26)**

- **R20.** These keys work in an automation lane that has the keyboard and in the Clip View's Envelope Editor (the shortcuts' `envelope` context, ahead of the Arrangement's). An automation lane has the keyboard when it's selected (R1) and the Arrangement has the focus; with Automation Mode on, Up and Down move the selection through each track's lane and then its automation lanes, in the order they're drawn (Beatlayer's own). (S7)
- **R21.** Left and Right move the insert marker one grid step along the envelope (S-A11Y-018), and Option-Left and Option-Right (Alt on Windows) to the previous or next breakpoint (S-A11Y-020). The envelope's value at the insert marker is shown beside it, in the parameter's own units, and is the lane's value for assistive technology. The breakpoint at the insert marker is the focused one, drawn in the hover colour. (S7, S8)
- **R22.** Enter, with no breakpoint selected, selects the breakpoint at the insert marker, or makes one there at the envelope's value at that point (at the parameter's value when there's no envelope) and selects it (S-A11Y-019). Option-click (Alt-click) on a breakpoint selects it and moves the insert marker to it (S-A11Y-021). While a breakpoint is selected, Tab and Shift-Tab select the next or previous breakpoint (S-A11Y-022; doing so only while one is selected is Beatlayer's own). The selected breakpoint pulses. (S7, S8)
- **R23.** While a breakpoint is selected, Left and Right move it one grid step in time (S-A11Y-030), not past its neighbours (Beatlayer's own); Up and Down change its value by the step its parameter's control takes for an arrow key ([device-framework.md](device-framework.md) R17; Beatlayer's own), within the parameter's range (S-A11Y-023); and digits start a typed value, in the units its control shows, set with Return. Enter keeps the changes and deselects it; Esc puts it back as it was when it was selected, removing one Enter made, and deselects it (S-A11Y-024); Delete deletes it (S-A11Y-025). Everything from selecting a breakpoint to Enter is one undo step, and a Delete is one (Beatlayer's own). (S7, S8)
- **R24.** With no breakpoint selected, Delete deletes the breakpoint at the insert marker, if there is one, and otherwise does what Delete does in the view. After Undo or Redo of a breakpoint edit, the breakpoint it restored is the focused one. (S7, S8)
- **R25.** Option-Up and Option-Down (Alt on Windows, S-A11Y-026) change the parameter the selected track's automation lane shows, or the Envelope Editor shows, to the previous or next parameter that has an envelope, on the track or in the clip, in the order the choosers list them ([automation.md](automation.md) R14), going round at the ends; with Shift (S-A11Y-027), to the previous or next of every parameter the choosers list; in the Envelope Editor the same keys are S-A11Y-028 and S-A11Y-029. Like the choosers, this is view state. (S7, S8, S10)
- **R26.** Cmd-Delete (Ctrl-Delete on Windows, S-ENV-005) deletes the automation of the parameter the selected automation lane shows, as `automation.delete` does ([automation.md](automation.md) R6), and in the Envelope Editor clears the shown envelope, as `clip.delete_envelope` does ([clip-envelopes.md](clip-envelopes.md) R5). (S9)
- **R27.** Right-clicking a breakpoint offers Edit Value, which types a value for it, the other selected breakpoints moving by the same share of the travel; right-clicking the line where a click would add a breakpoint offers Add Value, which types the value of a new breakpoint there (C-TIME-026). Each is one undo step. (S1, S10)
- **R28.** Assistive technology reads an automation lane as a group named for the parameter it shows ("Filter Freq Automation", Beatlayer's own), valued as R21 says; the focused or selected breakpoint is its active descendant, read as its position in bars, beats and sixteenths and its value as the parameter reads, with whether it's selected, so a screen reader speaks each move and change. (S7)
- **R29.** The keys run registered commands, so an agent can do the same: the breakpoint edits of [automation.md](automation.md) R7 and [clip-envelopes.md](clip-envelopes.md) R5, and `automation.select_breakpoint` (`read`, view state, which `arrangement.state` reports). Every key in R21 to R26 is bound in the keymap, and the keymap test reaches S-A11Y-018 to S-A11Y-030 and S-ENV-005. (S9, S11)
