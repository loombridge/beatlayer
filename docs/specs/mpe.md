## The behaviour

MPE, MIDI Polyphonic Expression, gives each note its own controls: a pitch bend, a slide (the finger's movement up and down the key) and a pressure, each changing while the note sounds. In Beatlayer every note in a MIDI clip can carry three envelopes for them, Pitch, Slide and Pressure, beside its velocity and release velocity. While the clip plays, the envelopes reach the track's devices as that note's own expressions, so a chord's notes bend and swell apart. A controller that speaks MPE plays and records them: with its port's MPE switch on, each note's channel carries that note's bend, slide and pressure, and a recording keeps them on the notes it makes.

Native instruments and MIDI effects always take per-note expression. A plug-in takes it when its MPE Mode is on: a CLAP plug-in as CLAP note expressions, a VST3 plug-in as VST3 note expression, and an Audio Unit, or a plug-in without either route, as MPE MIDI, each note on a channel of its own. A track can also send MPE MIDI out of a MIDI port to a hardware synthesiser. The MPE/Multi-channel Settings choose the zone and channels MPE MIDI goes out on.

The Clip View's MPE tab (View ▸ Expression Editor) edits the envelopes. Selecting a note shows its Pitch envelope drawn over it in the note area and its Slide and Pressure envelopes in lanes underneath, with lanes for Velocity and Release Velocity as well. Breakpoints are added, moved, curved, typed and drawn as in Live, and on a track with a Drum Rack, where the note area's rows are pads rather than pitches, Pitch has a lane of its own.

## Rules

**Per-note expression in clips (WP-6.17)**

- **R1.** Every note in a MIDI clip can carry three expression envelopes besides its velocity and release velocity: Pitch, its per-note pitch bend in semitones from the note's own key, from −48 to +48; Slide, from 0 to 127; and Pressure, from 0 to 127. Each is a list of breakpoints, each a position counted from the note's start, from 0 to the note's length, a value in the envelope's range, and the bend of the segment starting at it, from −1 to 1, in position order with no two at one position, as a clip envelope's are ([clip envelopes](clip-envelopes.md) R1). Before its first breakpoint an envelope holds the first breakpoint's value, and after its last the last's (Beatlayer's own). An envelope with no breakpoints is none. (S1, S10, S13)
- **R2.** A note's Pressure envelope is its per-note pressure ([MIDI controller data](midi-controller-data.md) R2). A set saved with pressure points loads each point as a breakpoint and adds, one tick before each later point, a breakpoint at the value before it, so the pressure plays the steps it played before (Beatlayer's own). (S13, S14)
- **R3.** `note.set_expression` (`edit`) replaces one envelope (`pitch`, `slide` or `pressure`) of the notes it names, or of the notes selected in the Clip View, with the breakpoints it's given; an empty list clears it. It's one undo step, Set Note Expression (Beatlayer's own name). It refuses a value outside the envelope's range, a position before 0 or past the note's end, two breakpoints at one position, a bend outside −1 to 1, and a clip that isn't MIDI, and then changes nothing. (S13)
- **R4.** A note's envelopes stay with it through every edit, in the edit's own undo step. Moving a note in time or pitch, transposing, quantising, nudging, copying, pasting, duplicating, and dragging a drum chain out with its notes keep them unchanged, since they count from the note's start (S2, S8). Shortening a note, by a drag or by cutting it where a clip is split, cropped or consolidated, drops the breakpoints past its new end and adds one at the end holding the value there; lengthening it lets the last value hold to the new end. An edit that scales notes in time (the note stretch markers, ×2, ÷2 and Stretch) scales their envelopes' positions by the same factor (S2). Joining notes keeps each note's envelopes at the place it has in the joined note (S6). Splitting or chopping a note gives each part the stretch of each envelope over its span, with a breakpoint at the part's start holding the value there. (S2, S6, S8; shortening and splitting are Beatlayer's own)
- **R5.** While a note sounds, each of its envelopes reaches the track's devices as that note's per-note expression, with its key and note ID ([device framework](device-framework.md) R33): its value at the note's start on the note-on's frame, just after the note-on; then a step on its breakpoint's frame and, along a sloped or curved segment, a value at least every 5 ms while it changes, as a controller envelope streams ([MIDI controller data](midi-controller-data.md) R3); and nothing from the note-off on. A value that doesn't change isn't sent again. A note that doesn't sound (its chance, a muted note, a deactivated clip) sends no expression. (S10, S13)
- **R6.** At a device, a per-note pitch of `s` semitones is a bend of `s ÷ 48`, a share of MPE's per-note range of 48 semitones, so an instrument whose per-note range is 48 semitones plays the semitones drawn and one set to another range scales them ([polysynth](polysynth.md) R22); slide and pressure arrive as their value ÷ 127, from 0 to 1. (S7, S10, S13)
- **R7.** Every native instrument and MIDI effect receives per-note expressions; none has a switch for them. A MIDI effect carries them with the notes it moves or makes ([device framework](device-framework.md) R33), and a drum rack passes them to the chains its note reached ([drum racks](drum-racks.md) R29). What an instrument does with them is its own spec's (Drift's is [polysynth](polysynth.md) R21 and R22). (S7, S13)
- **R8.** Playing expressions allocates nothing, takes no lock and doesn't block on the audio thread; its work per block is bounded by the block's frames and the breakpoints in it; and a render is the same at any block size. (S13)
- **R9.** The set saves each note's envelopes with the note, leaving out an envelope with no breakpoints; a set saved before them loads with none but R2's pressure. A loaded envelope that breaks R1 doesn't load, and its note loads without it. The clip query reads every note's envelopes back ([MIDI controller data](midi-controller-data.md) R9). (S13)

**MPE controllers (WP-6.17)**

- **R10.** An input port whose MPE switch is on ([MIDI I/O](midi-io.md) R27) is read as MPE. It starts as one lower zone: channel 1 its master channel and channels 2 to 16 its member channels. An MPE Configuration Message the port sends sets the zones it describes: on channel 1, the lower zone's number of member channels from channel 2 up; on channel 16, an upper zone whose master is channel 16 and whose members run down from channel 15; a count of 0 removes that zone. A pitch bend sensitivity sent on a member channel sets its zone's per-note bend range, 48 semitones until then. (S10; reading the zones and the range from the controller is Beatlayer's own)
- **R11.** From an MPE port, a note-on on a member channel starts a note with a note ID of its own. While it's held, its channel's pitch bend is its per-note pitch (the bend's share of its zone's range, in semitones), its channel's controller 74 its slide, and its channel's channel pressure, and polyphonic pressure on its key, its pressure; each reaches the track's devices as R5 and R6 say, on the frame its message landed on ([MIDI I/O](midi-io.md) R15). The values a member channel received before the note-on are the note's starting values, sent just after its note-on. Two notes held on one member channel both take its messages (Beatlayer's own). A message on a master channel, and any other controller on a member channel, reaches the track as a channel message, as from a port that isn't MPE ([device framework](device-framework.md) R39). A message for a member channel with no note held changes only that channel's values for its next note. (S1, S10)
- **R12.** A note from an MPE port is recorded with its expressions wherever a take records notes: Arrangement recording and MIDI Arrangement Overdub, a Session slot's recording and overdub, and Capture MIDI ([MIDI controller data](midi-controller-data.md) R10 to R15). When the take ends, each of its pitch, slide and pressure becomes the note's envelope: a breakpoint at the note's start with its starting value, and one at each message's position held as a step until the next, as a controller is stepped ([MIDI controller data](midi-controller-data.md) R11), the later of two messages on one frame winning; messages after its note-off aren't kept. A master channel's messages become the clip's controller envelopes as the controller data spec says. Nothing adds an undo step: the expressions are in the recording's own ([MIDI controller data](midi-controller-data.md) R16). (S1, S13; the steps follow Live's recorded controllers)
- **R13.** Reading MPE on the audio thread allocates nothing: each zone's latest values per member channel and the notes held on each are made with the program, and a track's recording ring takes the expressions with its notes. (S13)

**Plug-ins and MIDI ports (WP-6.17b)**

- **R14.** Every plug-in device has an MPE Mode switch, an item of its title bar's context menu that's ticked while it's on (C-DEVICE-015), off in a new device. Switching it is one undo step, `plugin.set_mpe` (`edit`), read back by `plugin.info`; it's saved with the device in the set, in the device's presets and in its default preset, as Save as Default Preset saves it (S4). Native devices have no such switch (R7). (S4, S9; off for every plug-in is Beatlayer's own)
- **R15.** With MPE Mode off, a plug-in gets a note's pressure as polyphonic key pressure on the note's key and channel, and neither its pitch nor its slide; notes keep the channel they carry ([CLAP hosting](clap-hosting.md) R21, [VST3 hosting](vst3-hosting.md) R19, [Audio Unit hosting](au-hosting.md) R19). (S13; Beatlayer's own)
- **R16.** With MPE Mode on, a plug-in gets every per-note expression: by its format's own per-note route where it has one, CLAP note expressions ([CLAP hosting](clap-hosting.md) R22) and VST3 note expression ([VST3 hosting](vst3-hosting.md) R20), and as MPE MIDI (R17) otherwise ([Audio Unit hosting](au-hosting.md) R20). (S4, S10, S11, S12)
- **R17.** MPE MIDI lays a track's notes and expressions out as the MPE specification does, in the zone its MPE/Multi-channel Settings name (R18). Each note goes on one of the zone's member channels: of those holding no note, the one whose last note ended longest ago; when every one holds a note, the one whose newest note started earliest. On the note-on's frame, before the note-on, the channel takes the note's starting pitch bend, controller 74 and channel pressure; after it, each change as R5 says, on the note's channel: the pitch as a 14-bit pitch bend over 48 semitones, the slide as controller 74, the pressure as channel pressure; and the note-off on its channel. The track's channel messages go on the zone's master channel, 1 for the lower zone and 16 for the upper. Before any note, when the plug-in is activated, the port opens or the settings change, the zone's MPE Configuration Message and a pitch bend sensitivity of 48 semitones on its member channels are sent. In multi-channel mode notes go on the channels of its range by the same rule, and channel messages on every channel of the range. (S4, S10; the channel choice, the messages sent before any note and multi-channel mode's channel messages are Beatlayer's own)
- **R18.** The MPE/Multi-channel Settings (L-OTH-008) open from a plug-in device's title bar context menu (C-DEVICE-015) and from a track's or an External Instrument's MIDI To once MPE is its channel (R19). They set the zone, Lower (master channel 1) or Upper (master channel 16), and its number of note channels, 1 to 15; or Multi-channel mode with the first and last channel of its range, 1 to 16. New settings are the lower zone with 15 note channels (Beatlayer's own). A track or a device sends to one zone. The settings are saved with the plug-in device, or with the track's or External Instrument's MIDI To, and each change is one undo step: `plugin.set_mpe_settings` (`edit`, read back by `plugin.info`) and `routing.set_midi_output` with its `mpe` settings (read back by the track query's routing). They shape only MPE MIDI going out; an input's zones are R10's. (S4, S9)
- **R19.** A MIDI track's MIDI To, sending to a MIDI port, lists MPE after the 16 channels in its channel chooser; with MPE chosen the track's notes, expressions and channel messages leave on the port as R17 lays them out, in place of being rewritten onto one channel ([external devices](external-devices.md) R3), and the chooser, opened again, offers MPE Settings…, which opens R18's settings. An External Instrument's MIDI To does the same. (S4)
- **R20.** A plug-in with MPE Mode on that sends per-note expressions out, as CLAP note expressions, VST3 note expression values, or MPE MIDI from a CLAP plug-in's MIDI port, has them passed on from its node as per-note expressions of the notes it sent ([CLAP hosting](clap-hosting.md) R23, [VST3 hosting](vst3-hosting.md) R21), its MPE MIDI read as an MPE port's (R11). (S4, S11, S12)
- **R21.** Laying out MPE MIDI and sending each format's note expressions allocates nothing on the audio thread: the channel table and event lists are made with the node or the route, and an event past their room is dropped and counted. (S13)

**The MPE tab (WP-6.18)**

- **R22.** The Clip View's MPE tab, the third of the editor toolbar's tabs (L-CLIP-010), shows a MIDI clip's MPE editor: `view.clip_tab` with `mpe`, Option+Shift+3 (S-CLIPMODE-005), View ▸ Expression Editor (M-VIEW-020), and Option+Tab (Ctrl+Tab) cycling the three tabs (S-CLIPMODE-002). It's the note area and rulers of the MIDI Note Editor with the expression lanes in the velocity lane's place (L-CLIP-018). An audio clip has no MPE tab, and the menu item is disabled while no MIDI clip shows. Choosing a tab is view state, never an undo step ([the MIDI Note Editor](piano-roll.md) R22). (S5, S9)
- **R23.** The lanes are Slide, Pressure, Velocity and Release Velocity, in that order, each with a selector toggle at its left; a new set shows Slide and Pressure. A triangle button under the toggles hides the shown lanes, or shows them again; with every toggle off, or with Option (Alt) held, it shows all four (I-CLICK-048). Dragging the split line under a lane resizes it (I-DRAG-055), and dragging the split between the note area and the lanes resizes them together; hiding the lanes with the triangle, or by dragging that split to the bottom, hides the toggles with them. A lane's header menu swaps it for a lane not shown (S7). Which lanes show and their heights are view state saved with the set, never an undo step (`view.mpe_lanes`, `read`; saving them with the set is Beatlayer's own). (S1, S7)
- **R24.** Pitch has no lane: each note's Pitch envelope is drawn over the note in the note area, on the key track's scale from the note's own row, so +12 sits an octave above the note. Pitch envelopes are hidden while Fold is on ([the MIDI Note Editor](piano-roll.md) R16). On a track whose instrument is a drum rack, where the rows are pads, a Pitch Bend lane comes first among the lanes (its place is Beatlayer's own), drawing the Pitch envelopes from −48 to +48 semitones, and none is drawn over the notes. (S1, S2, S8)
- **R25.** With the MPE tab shown, clicking a note selects it as the note editor does ([the MIDI Note Editor](piano-roll.md) R5, R6), and each selected note shows its Pitch envelope over it, its Slide and Pressure envelopes in their lanes with their breakpoints, and its velocity and release velocity as markers in their lanes; unselected notes are grey and their envelopes dimmed. A breakpoint's value shows beside it while it's made, hovered over or dragged: semitones to two decimals for Pitch, a whole number for the others (the format is Beatlayer's own). (S1, S2, S14)
- **R26.** On a selected note's envelope: clicking a segment adds a breakpoint on it (I-CLICK-045); clicking a breakpoint deletes it (I-CLICK-046); dragging a breakpoint moves it, and every selected breakpoint with it, in time and value, and one dragged past a neighbour in time removes the neighbour (I-DRAG-050); dragging on the envelope's background selects the breakpoints it encloses (I-DRAG-051); clicking near a segment, or Shift-clicking on it, selects the segment (I-CLICK-047), and dragging a selected segment moves it in value (I-DRAG-050); a segment inside the time selection gets breakpoints at the selection's edges and moves whole. Edit Value, in a breakpoint's context menu (C-MPE-002), types its value and moves the other selected breakpoints by the same change; Add Value, on the preview breakpoint at the pointer (C-MPE-003), types a new breakpoint's value. Dragging a velocity or release velocity marker sets the note's value (`note.set_value`, [the MIDI Note Editor](piano-roll.md) R15). Breakpoints stay inside their note (R1). (S2)
- **R27.** The MPE tab has a grid of its own, off in a new clip, saved with the clip and switched with Snap to Grid or Cmd+4 (Ctrl+4, S-GRID-005) while the tab has the focus (`view.mpe_grid`, `read`; never an undo step, Beatlayer's own, as folding isn't). With it on, a breakpoint or segment being moved snaps to its neighbours' positions, and a new breakpoint near a grid line lands on the line. (S2, S9)
- **R28.** Shift held while moving keeps a drag to the axis it starts along, and Shift held while dragging a value moves it a fifth as fast (the fraction is Beatlayer's own, the clip envelopes' R9's). Option (Alt) and a drag on a segment curves it, and Option (Alt) and a double-click straightens it (`note.set_expression_curve`, I-DRAG-052). On a Pitch envelope, Cmd (Alt on Windows) held while making, moving or drawing a breakpoint snaps its value to the nearest semitone while the grid is off, and bypasses the grid while it's on (I-DRAG-053). (S2, S3)
- **R29.** Clicking outside a note and dragging up or down over its highlighted Slide, Pressure, Velocity or Release Velocity scales that value over the note's whole length, for every selected note (`note.scale_expression`, I-DRAG-054); Pitch doesn't scale. Cmd+A (Ctrl+A, S-NOTE-053) with an envelope focused selects all its breakpoints, which a drag then moves together: up or down by the same change in value, left or right by the same time. (S2)
- **R30.** With Draw Mode on (B, or B held; Options ▸ Draw Mode, M-OPT-017, S-GRID-001), dragging over a Pitch envelope or in the Slide or Pressure lane draws the envelope freehand over the stretch dragged, replacing its breakpoints there with steps (`note.draw_expression`); Shift gives finer values. With the grid on each step is one grid step wide; with it off, Cmd (Alt) held draws on the grid. (S3, S9)
- **R31.** Z (S-NOTE-023) zooms to the time selection, its height taken from the Pitch values in it (`clip_view.mpe_zoom`, `read`; until the editor's zoom of WP-6.54 exists it brings the key rows the Pitch values reach into view and leaves the time axis). Clear All Envelopes, in the note area's and each lane's context menu (C-MPE-001), clears every envelope of the selected notes (`note.clear_expressions`), one undo step. (S2)
- **R32.** Every edit in the tab is a registered command an agent runs by ID with the clip, the notes and the envelope spelled out: `note.add_expression_breakpoint`, `note.move_expression_breakpoints`, `note.delete_expression_breakpoints`, `note.set_expression_curve`, `note.scale_expression`, `note.draw_expression` and `note.clear_expressions` are `edit`, each read back by the clip query's notes; `clip_view.select_expression_breakpoints`, `view.mpe_lanes` and `view.mpe_grid` are `read`. A drag is one gesture and one undo step however long it lasts, and Esc during it puts the clip back ([the MIDI Note Editor](piano-roll.md) R11). (S13)
- **R33.** A MIDI track's MIDI meters ([MIDI meters](midi-meters.md) R1 and R2) light their lowest dot blue while per-note expression passes them ([MIDI meters](midi-meters.md) R5), which the engine publishes after each block and agents read headless ([MIDI meters](midi-meters.md) R6). (S1)
- **R34.** The tab reaches assistive technology: each lane with its name and range, the selector toggles and the triangle as switches with their states, and the selected notes' envelopes as lists of breakpoints with their positions and values; Tab reaches the toggles and the triangle. (S13)
