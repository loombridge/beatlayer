## The behaviour

A person launches a clip by its slot's launch button, and launches a scene by its launch button in the Main track's column. While the song plays, a launch waits for the next line of the global launch quantisation grid — the Control Bar's Quantization Menu (L-CTRL-010) — and the slot's triangle flashes until then; while the song is stopped the launch starts the transport at once from the Arrangement position and the clip starts with it. A track's stop button, and Stop All Clips at the left of Main's status row, stop a track's clip or every track's clip on the next grid line and don't stop the transport. Every audio and MIDI slot carries a Clip Stop button unless it was removed, and Cmd+E (Edit ▸ Remove Stop Button) adds or removes it. The engine holds one state machine per slot — queued, playing, queued to stop — publishes their states with the playhead after every block, and the track's player sounds a launched clip from its launch frame.

This spec covers the launch and stop state machine in the engine, the global launch quantisation and its switch, the stop buttons in the model, the commands, and what the engine publishes. It also covers the clip's launch mode (WP-3.3): what a press and a release of a slot's launch button do in Trigger, Gate, Toggle and Repeat, in the engine. It leaves to other packages: Follow Actions (WP-3.10, [follow-actions.md](follow-actions.md)), the clip's own launch settings and the Launch defaults in the Settings window (WP-3.4), a scene's tempo and time signature (WP-3.5), playing Session clips over the Arrangement and Back to Arrangement (WP-3.6, [session-view.md](session-view.md) R20 to R24), the grid's drawing, selection and clicks (WP-3.7), the launch buttons and the status row (WP-3.8), and the Clip View's Launch panel (WP-3.9).

A Session clip's own launch settings change what its launch button does. Its Quantize setting puts its presses on a grid of its own instead of the global one, Global following the Control Bar and None launching at once. With Legato on, a clip that starts while another clip plays on its track carries on from the point that clip had reached, so clips can be swapped mid-phrase in time. The Launch Properties panel's two nudge buttons move a playing clip's position back or forward by one step of the global launch quantisation, and in MIDI Map Mode a scrub control between them takes an encoder that nudges the clip as it turns. The velocity amount, which scales a launch's volume by the velocity that launched it, is [remote-mapping.md](remote-mapping.md)'s (R16).

## Rules

- **R1.** The global launch quantisation and its enabled switch save with the set, beside the song, not in the preferences: a set saved before WP-3.2 opens with 1 Bar and the switch on, and changing either is no undo step. The value is one of the menu's steps — None, 8 Bars, 4 Bars, 2 Bars, 1 Bar, 1/2, 1/2T, 1/4, 1/4T, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T, 1/32 — and 1 Bar is the default. (S2, S3, S4, S6)
- **R2.** While the song plays, a launch or a stop asked for takes effect on the exact frame of the first line of the quantisation grid after the playhead, whatever the audio block size: bar steps are counted from the bar lines the time-signature map places, from the start of the arrangement, and note values and their triplets from 1.1.1 in ticks. A launch or a stop queued when the transport stops keeps waiting for the playhead to reach its frame. (S1, S2, S5)
- **R3.** With quantisation off — the enabled switch off, or the value None — a launch or a stop takes effect at once. (S1, S2)
- **R4.** A launch or a stop already queued keeps the frame it was queued for when the value or the switch changes: the frame is worked out once, when the request arrives. (S6)
- **R5.** A slot launch with the transport stopped starts the transport at once, from the Arrangement position, and the clip starts with it: it doesn't wait for the grid. (S2)
- **R6.** A stop takes effect on the grid, and never stops the transport: a track's stop button stops the clip that track plays and drops a launch still queued on it; Stop All Clips stops every track's playing clip; Cmd+Enter (`session.stop_selected`) does the first for every track with a selected slot. A stop also takes its track over from the Arrangement ([session-view.md](session-view.md) R21). (S1, S2, S4)
- **R7.** A scene launch queues every slot of the scene that holds a clip, all on one frame, so the scene's clips start together. With the transport stopped, a scene that holds clips starts the transport as a slot launch does; a scene with nothing in it does nothing, and doesn't start the transport. (S1, S2, S3, S7)
- **R8.** Every audio and MIDI slot carries a Clip Stop button until it's removed: Add or Remove Stop Button (`slot.set_stop_button`, Cmd+E, Edit ▸ Remove Stop Button, and the slot's context menu item) adds it back or takes it away, one undo step, saved with the set. (S1, S3, S4)
- **R9.** Every launch and stop is a registered command with a schema, a description and the read class, runnable by ID: `session.launch` for a slot, `session.launch_scene` for a scene, `session.stop_track` for a track, and `session.stop_all` for every track. The launched clip's track plays the slot's clip from its launch frame and loops it while the clip's Loop switch is on and takes the track over from its Arrangement clips ([session-view.md](session-view.md) R20). (S5, S6)
- **R10.** The engine publishes the slots' states with the playhead after every block, for the UI to draw: a slot a launch or a stop is queued for, a slot whose clip plays, and a slot a stop is queued for; a slot that stopped isn't listed, so an empty list is a Session View at rest. When the transport stops, a playing Session clip keeps its slot's state and is silent while the playhead stands still. (S5)
- **R11.** One clip plays per track: launching a slot while another of the track's slots plays stops the old one exactly where the new one starts, and a launch or a stop queued for a track replaces one already queued, so two launches on one track act in order. (S1)
- **R12.** A second launch of a slot that is queued to start changes nothing: it keeps the frame it was queued for (R4). What a press means once the clip plays is its launch mode (R17 to R21). (S6)
- **R13.** S-GQ-001 to S-GQ-005 set the value to a sixteenth, an eighth, a quarter, one bar and None; S-SES-024 to S-SES-027, and Options ▸ Smaller, Larger, Triplet and Launch Quantization Enabled (M-OPT-011 to M-OPT-014), make the value smaller, larger, its triplet, and switch quantisation on or off. The Control Bar's Quantization Menu shows the value (WP-3.8 draws it). Setting a value doesn't change the enabled switch, and the switch doesn't change the value. (S3, S4)
- **R14.** A Session clip carries Legato, its own launch quantisation (none chosen means Global, following the global value; None is a step of its own) and a velocity amount from 0 to 1, saved with the clip and left out of the file while they are the defaults (off, Global, 0), so a set saved before WP-3.4 opens with the defaults. `clip.set_legato`, `clip.set_launch_quantization` and `clip.set_velocity_amount` change them, each one undo step, and refuse a clip that isn't in a slot. The engine reads Legato (R27 to R29) and the clip's own quantisation (R25 and R26; a follow launch reads it as [follow-actions.md](follow-actions.md) R10 says), and a launch's gain follows the velocity amount as [remote-mapping.md](remote-mapping.md) R16 says. (S1, S3)
- **R15.** The Launch section of Record, Warp & Launch holds Default Launch Mode (Trigger), Default Launch Quantization (Global), Select on Launch (on), Select Next Scene on Launch (on) and Start Recording on Scene Launch (off): preferences, kept between starts, not saved with a set and no undo step, each with a `settings.set_*` command and shown in `settings.state` under `launch`. A new Session clip starts with Default Launch Quantization. (S3)
- **R16.** While Select on Launch is on, launching a slot that holds a clip selects that slot; while Select Next Scene on Launch is on, launching a scene selects the scene after it, and nothing when it is the last. (S3)
- **R17.** A clip's launch mode is Trigger, Gate, Toggle or Repeat, and Trigger is the default. A Trigger press queues the clip's launch as R2 to R5 say; releasing has no effect. A press of a Trigger clip that already plays, or whose stop is queued, queues a restart on the next grid line instead: the clip plays again from its start there, and until then the slot reads `queued`. A restart already queued keeps its frame. (S8, S9)
- **R18.** A Gate press launches the clip as a Trigger press does, including R5 with the transport stopped. Releasing queues a stop on the next line of the grid, at the release frame with quantisation off, so the clip plays while the button is held and to the end of the quantum it was released in. The transport keeps running. (S8, S9)
- **R19.** A Gate or Repeat release never cancels a launch or a restart still queued: the launch happens on its line and the stop lands on the line after it, so a press and release inside one grid interval play the clip for one quantum. (S9)
- **R20.** The press of a Toggle clip that plays queues a stop on the next grid line, as a track's stop button does (R6), and releasing has no effect. A press while that stop is queued replaces it with a queued restart on the line, and a press while a restart is queued replaces it with a stop. A press of a Toggle clip that is not playing launches it. (S8, S9)
- **R21.** A Repeat press launches the clip as a Trigger press does; while the button is held the clip restarts on every line of the clip's own launch quantisation (R25), the global one while the clip's is Global. Releasing queues the stop for the next line, so the clip plays out the quantum it is in. With no quantisation (the clip's None, or Global while the global value is None or its switch is off), Repeat acts as Trigger: one start and no restarts. (S8, S9)
- **R22.** The mode is read from the clip at each press and at each release, so changing it while the clip plays does not touch the playing clip. A new Session clip starts with the Default Launch Mode setting (R15), and the clip's mode is saved with the set and changes in one undo step (`clip.set_launch_mode`, which refuses a clip that isn't in a slot); a press or release of a slot with no clip acts as Trigger. A scene's launch button acts as a Trigger press on each of its clips, whatever their modes. A queued launch, restart or stop looks the same in every mode. (S8, S9)
- **R23.** Releasing a launch button is a registered command, `session.release`, with the read class: `session.launch` is the press, and a host that has a release sends it. (S5)
- **R24.** While the Clip View shows a clip that sits in a Session slot, the Launch Properties panel (L-CLIP-005) takes the left of the view and the editor sits beside it; for a clip in the Arrangement there is no panel. Launch mode, Legato, Quantize (Global, then the global menu's steps) and Velocity (in percent, a hundredth a step) show the clip's saved settings, the recorded defaults for a clip without any, and change them through `clip.set_launch_mode`, `clip.set_legato`, `clip.set_launch_quantization` and `clip.set_velocity_amount`, one undo step each. The two nudge buttons, and the scrub control between them in the map modes, are R30 to R36's; the follow-action controls are [follow-actions.md](follow-actions.md) R13. The panel's sizes are the manual's reading: the Launch tab wasn't measured in Live. (S10)
- **R25.** A Session clip's own launch quantisation places every press and release of its launch button on its own grid, counted as R2 counts the global one: the launch, a Trigger or Toggle restart (R17, R20), Gate's and Repeat's stop on release (R18, R21), Toggle's stop (R20) and Repeat's restarts (R21). Global uses the global value and its switch (R1 to R3); a step of its own is that step's grid; None takes effect at once. The global switch (M-OPT-014) switches only the global value, so a clip with a step of its own keeps it while the switch is off (Beatlayer's own reading). A launch with the transport stopped still starts at once (R5), and a follow launch reads the clip's quantisation as [follow-actions.md](follow-actions.md) R10 says. (S8, S11)
- **R26.** The global quantisation, not a clip's own, places a track's stop button, Stop All Clips and `session.stop_selected` (R6), and a scene launch, whose clips all start on one line of the global grid whatever their own quantisation (R7, R22; Beatlayer's own reading). (S1, S11)
- **R27.** A clip with Legato on that starts on its launch frame while another clip plays on its track there doesn't play from its start: it takes over the clip time that clip has reached on that frame (the time since that clip started, its own Legato start and nudges included), and plays on from there through its own markers and loop as if it had played that long. The launch still waits for its quantisation (R25), so with None the takeover is at once. With no clip playing on the track on that frame (the track idle, or taken over with nothing playing, or the transport just started by the launch, R5), it plays from its start. The other clip stops on that frame as R11 says. (S8, S11; taking over the time since the start, rather than the place in the other clip's loop, is Beatlayer's own reading)
- **R28.** Legato is read from the clip that starts, at its start, whatever started it: a press in any mode, a scene launch, or a follow launch, so follow actions between Legato clips keep the play position. A restart of a Legato clip that plays already (a Trigger, Gate or Repeat restart, Toggle's restart, a follow's Play Again or a group of one) takes over its own clip time: the clip plays on with no jump, while its follow action counts again from the restart ([follow-actions.md](follow-actions.md) R2). (S8, S11; a restart taking over its own time is Beatlayer's own reading)
- **R29.** A clip that plays from a clip time other than its start, by Legato or a nudge (R30), plays exactly what it would play at that time had it played from its start: a MIDI clip plays the notes that begin at or after that time and none that began before it, every note still sounding from before the jump ending there; a warped audio clip plays from the beat that time reaches, an unwarped one from the seconds it reaches at the tempo on that frame (Beatlayer's own); a streamed file jumps as a locate does, crossfading and counting a miss ([disk-streaming.md](disk-streaming.md) R4 and R5), and an offline render waits for the disk (R8 there), so it renders the same at every block size. The clip's envelopes follow its clip time, and an unlinked envelope keeps counting from the launch (Beatlayer's own). (S11, S13)
- **R30.** Nudge Backward and Nudge Forward, the Launch Properties panel's two buttons (L-CLIP-005), move the playing clip the Clip View shows back or forward by one step of the global launch quantisation: at once, on the frame the engine receives the press, not on the grid, one step for each press, and releasing or holding the button does nothing more (Beatlayer's own). A step is the global value's length at the playhead (a bar step in bars of the signature there, R2); with the value None or the switch off, a thirty-second note (Beatlayer's own). (S11, S12)
- **R31.** A nudge moves only a clip that plays: one whose slot reads `playing`, or `queued_to_stop`, or `queued` for a restart (R17). A launch, a stop or a restart already queued keeps its frame, and a restart still plays the clip from its start (or by R28 for a Legato clip). A nudge of a slot that is empty, stopped or still waiting to start changes nothing. With the transport stopped a playing clip's position moves, and it sounds from there when the transport plays (R10). (S11; Beatlayer's own)
- **R32.** Forward, the clip time grows by the step: a looping clip goes on round its loop as it would have after that time, and a clip without its Loop switch on that passes its end acts as one that played to its end. Backward, the clip time shrinks by the step; where that would fall before the clip's start, a looping clip goes the fewest whole loop lengths later that bring it back into its loop, so it keeps its place in the loop, and a clip that doesn't loop goes to its start. (S11; Beatlayer's own)
- **R33.** Legato and a nudge move a clip's own time and nothing else: the frame the slot launched on, the slot's state, and the follow action's count ([follow-actions.md](follow-actions.md) R2) stay. A MIDI overdub into the playing clip ([session-recording.md](session-recording.md) R1) records at its clip time. An Arrangement take ([session-to-arrangement.md](session-to-arrangement.md) R1 and R3) records what plays: a Legato start's copy begins with its start marker at the time it took over, and a nudge splits the copy there, the part after it with its start marker at the clip time it jumped to. (S13; Beatlayer's own)
- **R34.** `session.nudge` (the read class, as the launch commands are, R9) takes a track, a scene, and `steps`, a whole number from −64 to 64 other than 0, negative backward, and moves that slot's clip by that many steps in one jump; each nudge button runs it with 1 or −1 for the slot of the clip the Clip View shows. A launch, a release and a nudge reach the engine as session commands and apply on the audio thread without allocating, locking or blocking and without a new program; each clip's Legato and own quantisation come to the engine with the program, so a change to them (R14) applies from the next press. `session.state` lists, under `launches` (where the slots the engine publishes already were, R10), each with its `state`, the tick it `launched` on and its `clip_time` in ticks, so an agent reads a launch, a Legato start and a nudge back without the window (Beatlayer's own format). (S5, S13)
- **R35.** In the map modes a scrub control shows between the two nudge buttons ([map-modes.md](map-modes.md) R3 and R22), and the buttons and the scrub are mapping targets ([map-modes.md](map-modes.md) R11). A relative controller's increment of size `n` on the scrub nudges the clip forward `n` steps in an accelerated mode and one step in a linear mode, and a decrement backward the same way ([remote-mapping.md](remote-mapping.md) R23 to R26), each message one `session.nudge`; an absolute controller on the scrub nudges by the change from its last value, its first message nudging nothing (Beatlayer's own). A mapped nudge button nudges one step on its press, as a click does. A mapping on these controls acts on the clip the Clip View shows when the message arrives, and does nothing while it shows none or an Arrangement clip. The scrub has no value of its own and sends no feedback ([remote-mapping.md](remote-mapping.md) R38; Beatlayer's own). (S11, S12, S13)
- **R36.** The nudge buttons are live while the Clip View shows a Session clip, whether or not it plays, and they are buttons in the accessibility tree named Nudge Backward and Nudge Forward, with descriptions saying they move the playing clip by the global quantisation; the scrub control is a slider named Scrub, shown only while a map mode is on (Beatlayer's own names, from the manual's). Neither changes the set: no undo step and no unsaved change. (S10, S11)
- **R37.** While the Arrangement loop plays, a launch or a stop whose grid line falls at or past the loop's end lands as the playhead wraps: the frames a launch, a restart, a stop, a scene's tempo and a follow action wait for move back by the loop's length with the wrap, so the change lands when the playhead reaches that line round the loop, and a line on the loop's end lands on the loop's start frame. A clip that plays keeps the frame it launched on (Beatlayer's own).
- **R38.** A launched clip, audio or MIDI, sounds only while the playhead is at or past the frame it launched on: after a locate back, a Play from an earlier place or a loop wrap that lands before that frame (R10, R37), it is silent until the playhead reaches that frame again, and then plays from its start (Beatlayer's own).
