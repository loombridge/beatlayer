## The behaviour

Every audio and MIDI track in the Arrangement has a main lane, the clips it plays, and can have any number of take lanes under it. Recording into the Arrangement puts each take on a take lane, a new one for each pass round the loop, and copies the latest take to the main lane so it plays. A take lane plays only while it's auditioned, which plays it in place of the main lane. A comp is built by copying stretches of the takes to the main lane: select a stretch on a take lane and press Enter, or drag across a take in Draw Mode, or step a selection through the takes with Cmd+Up and Cmd+Down. A copy on the main lane is a clip of its own, independent of the take it came from. The material the main lane uses is highlighted on the take lanes in the track's colour, the rest drawn paler, and dragging a highlight's edge moves the boundary between two parts of the comp. Take lanes can be inserted, duplicated, renamed, reordered, resized and deleted, and their clips edited like any Arrangement clip.

This spec covers take lanes and comping on one track. It leaves to other specs: how a recording's take is written and placed on the main lane ([recording](recording.md)); the Arrangement's edits themselves ([arrangement editing](arrangement-editing.md)), Crop and Consolidate ([audio clip editing](audio-clip-editing.md)), which act on take-lane clips as on any; fades and crossfades between adjacent clips (WP-6.51); what freezing and bouncing do with take lanes ([freeze and bounce](freeze-and-bounce.md) R30); and editing several linked tracks at once ([linked-track editing](linked-track-editing.md)). Undo step names in code type are Beatlayer's own.

## Rules

**Take lanes in the document (WP-6.21)**

- **R1.** Every audio and MIDI track has a main lane, the Arrangement clips it plays, and any number of take lanes under it, in an order of their own. A take lane has an ID that never changes, a name, and clips of its track's kind, audio on an audio track and MIDI on a MIDI track. Each is a clip as the main lane's are, with a clip ID of its own, its data, envelopes and activator, at a place in the Arrangement, and no two clips on a lane overlap (arrangement editing R6). Group, return and Main tracks have no take lanes. (S1, S6, S15)
- **R2.** A track plays its main lane only; a take lane's clips play only while the lane is auditioned (R14). A take-lane clip is found by its ID like any clip, so every command that acts on a clip by ID (its markers, loop, gain, fades, warp settings and warp markers, notes, envelopes, activator, name, Crop) acts on it as on a main-lane clip, and a command that places clips on a take lane clears only that lane under them. (S1, S6)
- **R3.** Take lanes are saved with the set, with their IDs, names, order and clips. A set saved before take lanes held clips of their own, whose passes had no clip IDs, opens with each pass as a clip of its lane under a new ID, and plays as it did. (S16)
- **R4.** Insert Take Lane (`take_lane.insert`; Create ▸ Insert Take Lane, M-CREATE-004; the track header's and a take lane header's menus; Shift+Option+T, S-COMP-004) adds an empty take lane to each selected audio or MIDI track: directly after its selected take lane where one is selected, after its last take lane otherwise. The new lane is named `Take N`, N one more than the highest number of a take lane of the track named `Take` and a number, 1 on a track with none. It's one undo step for every track, `Insert Take Lane`, and is disabled with no audio or MIDI track selected. Beatlayer's own: where the lane goes and its name. (S2, S8, S14)
- **R5.** Duplicate on take lanes (`take_lane.duplicate`; Cmd+D, S-COMP-005, with take lane headers selected; a take lane header's menu) puts a copy of each selected take lane directly after it, with its name and a copy of each of its clips under new IDs, and selects the copies; one undo step, `Duplicate Take Lane(s)`. Beatlayer's own: the copy's name and place. (S2, S8)
- **R6.** `take_lane.rename` gives a take lane a new name, one undo step, `Rename Take Lane`; an empty name is refused. Rename (Edit ▸ Rename, Cmd+R, S-EDIT-008) with take lane headers selected, or a take lane header's Rename, types the name in place (R28). (S2)
- **R7.** Delete with take lane headers selected (`take_lane.delete`; Delete or Backspace, S-EDIT-005; Edit ▸ Delete) deletes the selected take lanes with their clips. Delete All Take Lanes (`take_lane.delete_all`; the track header's and a take lane header's menus) deletes every take lane of each selected audio or MIDI track. Delete All Unused Take Lanes (`take_lane.delete_unused`; the same menus) deletes each selected track's take lanes none of whose material the main lane uses (R20). The main lane is left as it was by each. Each is one undo step named as its item, and `destructive`. (S2, S10)
- **R8.** `take_lane.move` moves take lanes to another place among their track's take lanes, keeping the order of the lanes it moves; the main lane stays first, and a take lane stays on its track. Cmd+Up and Cmd+Down with take lane headers selected (S-COMP-008) move them one place up or down, and do nothing past the first or last; dragging a header moves them where it's dropped (R35). One undo step, `Move Take Lane(s)`. (S2)
- **R9.** A track's take lanes are part of it: deleting the track deletes them, undoing the deletion brings them back with their IDs, a track's copy holds copies of them ([track context menu](track-context-menu.md) R13), and Duplicate Track Without Clips copies none, as it copies no clips (IMP-12). (S1, S16)

**Recording takes (WP-6.21)**

- **R10.** Every recording into the Arrangement puts each armed audio or MIDI track's take on a take lane as well as on the main lane: the clip the recording places on the main lane (recording R4, R7, R12) is a copy, under an ID of its own, of the clip it places on a take lane, and a take that goes round the loop puts each pass on a take lane of its own, as recording R12 does. Placing the take-lane clips is part of the undo step that places the main-lane clip (`Recorded Clip`, or `Record` for each pass). Audio recorded before the loop's start stays a main-lane clip of its own, as recording R12 places it. (S3, S15)
- **R11.** Each take, and each pass of one, goes on the first of the track's take lanes, nearest the main lane first, that holds no clip ending after the take's start; when no lane is free, on a new take lane after the last, named as R4 names one. So recording a second verse after the first reuses the first verse's lane, while a second pass over the same bars gets a lane of its own. (S3)
- **R12.** A MIDI track takes its recording as an audio track does: each take, or each pass round the loop, is one MIDI clip on a take lane holding what that take or pass received (recording R6, R7), and each pass is copied to the main lane over what was there in an undo step of its own, as recording R12 does for audio, rather than one clip merging every pass. With MIDI Arrangement Overdub on (recording R8), the main lane takes the notes as recording R8 says, each pass merging into the clips there, while the take lanes hold each take or pass alone. Beatlayer's own: the take lanes under MIDI Arrangement Overdub. (S3, S15)
- **R13.** The clips a recording puts on take lanes are named as its main-lane clips are: an audio clip after its file, a MIDI clip with no name. Each take-lane clip takes the colour [themes](themes.md) R20 gives a recorded clip, so with Clip Color on Random each take has its own, and its copy on the main lane keeps it. A recording that adds a take lane to a track shows the track's take lanes (R24), Beatlayer's own. (S3, S15, S17)

**Auditioning (WP-6.21; the keys WP-6.22)**

- **R14.** A track has at most one auditioned take lane. `take_lane.set_audition` (a take lane and `on`; the take lane header's Audition Take Lane button, L-ARR-020, I-CLICK-040) switches a lane's audition on or off; switching one on switches off the one auditioned before on its track. While a take lane is auditioned, the track's Arrangement plays that lane's clips in place of its main lane's, from the next block, as the clip player's data with no new program (audio clips R12); switching it off plays the main lane again. Auditioning is saved with the set and is one undo step, `Audition Take Lane`, Beatlayer's own. (S1, S5, S16)
- **R15.** T (S-COMP-003; `take_lane.audition`, whose lanes default to the selection's) switches on the audition of each track's selected take lane: with a time or header selection over several take lanes of a track, the last of them selected; each track with a take lane in the selection auditions one. When every take lane T would audition is auditioned already, T switches them off (Beatlayer's own). (S5, S8)
- **R16.** Auditioning changes what the track's Arrangement clips play and nothing else: a Session clip playing on the track plays as it does without it, its edits heard, and the main lane's edits made during an audition are what it plays once the audition is off. (S10, S12)

**Building a comp (WP-6.21)**

- **R17.** Copy Selection to Main Lane (`take_lane.copy_to_main_lane`, a take lane and a range in ticks or bars, or the selection; Enter, S-COMP-002, with a take lane selected; a take lane's menu) copies the take lane's material in the range to the main lane, at the same place: each of the lane's clips the range reaches, cut at the range's edges as Split cuts (arrangement editing R8), is copied under a new ID onto the main lane, where it replaces what it covers (arrangement editing R6). Where the lane has no clip in the range, the main lane is left as it was. With a selection over several take lanes of one track, the track copies from the last selected of them, as R15 chooses; a selection on several tracks copies on each. The selection stays as it was. One undo step, `Copy Selection to Main Lane`; `edit`, and `destructive` as well when it replaces or shortens a main-lane clip, as agent permissions R1 decides for Paste. Beatlayer's own: what an empty stretch of the lane leaves, the choice among several lanes, and the selection afterwards. (S6, S8, S15)
- **R18.** A clip copied to the main lane is independent of the take-lane clip it came from: editing, moving or deleting either leaves the other as it was. (S6)
- **R19.** `take_lane.replace_with_take` (a track, a range, and `next` or `previous`; Cmd+Down and Cmd+Up, S-COMP-006, with clips or a time selection on the main lane or a take lane) replaces the main lane's material in the range with the next take's below, or the previous take's above, placed as R17 places it, skipping take lanes with no clip in the range. With the selection on a take lane, the next or previous take is counted from that lane, and the selection moves to the lane taken from. With the selection on the main lane, each main-lane clip's part of the range is counted from the take lane it uses (R20), and a part that uses none, or a stretch with no clip, from before the first take lane, so Cmd+Down takes the first take lane with a clip there. With no take lane left in that direction, that part is left as it was. One undo step, `Replace with Next Take` or `Replace with Previous Take`, classed as R17. Beatlayer's own: counting from each part's own take, and the ends. (S6, S8)
- **R20.** A main-lane clip uses a take-lane clip of its track over the stretch where both lie when they match there: the same data apart from the Start and End markers, the loop brace and switch, and the fades; the same clip envelopes; and the same contents at every point of the stretch, for audio the same point of the same file through the same warp map, for MIDI the same notes at the same places in the song. A deactivated main-lane clip uses nothing. The material the main lane uses is the union of those stretches. `take_lane.comp_sources` (`read`, a track) reports each main-lane clip's stretches with the take lane and clip each uses, and each take-lane clip's used stretches. Beatlayer's own: what matching is made of. (S7, S12)
- **R21.** `take_lane.move_comp_edge` (a track, a boundary, and a new position) moves the boundary between two adjacent main-lane clips that each use a take-lane clip (R20): the earlier clip's right edge and the later clip's left edge move to the new position together, each playing on what its take-lane clip plays there, as resizing does (arrangement editing R7, R16). The boundary goes no further than the growing clip's take-lane clip reaches, and leaves each clip at least a tick long. One undo step, `Move Comp Edge`. Beatlayer's own: the limits. (S7)
- **R22.** A comp part keeps the fades of the clip it was cut from, as a split part keeps its data (audio clips R7). Where two comp parts meet on the main lane they are adjacent clips like any others: with Create Fades on Clip Edges on, the boundary gets the 4 ms crossfade adjacent audio clips get, and Create Crossfade (Cmd+Option+F, S-ARR-008) makes one there by hand, both as [audio clips](audio-clips.md) R24, R26 and R28 make crossfades between adjacent clips (WP-6.51); moving a comp boundary (R21) moves its crossfade with it. (S6, S9)

**Take lanes in the Arrangement (WP-6.22)**

- **R23.** Each shown take lane is a row under its track's main lane, in the lanes' order (L-ARR-012), with a header beside it in the header column holding its name and its Audition Take Lane button, lit while it's auditioned (L-ARR-020). A take lane row is 44 points tall when it's first shown, Beatlayer's own. Its clips are drawn as the main lane's are (arrangement view R4). (S1, S5, S14)
- **R24.** Show Take Lanes (`arrangement.show_take_lanes`, `read`; the track header's menu, C-TRACK-019, ticked while the track's take lanes show; Cmd+Option+U, S-COMP-001; the Show/Hide Take Lanes toggle in the main lane's header, L-ARR-021 and I-CLICK-043) shows or hides the selected tracks' take lanes. It's view state, kept as a track's folding is and never an undo step. The toggle shows on a track that has a take lane, unfolded, while Automation Mode is off. Take lanes are hidden while Automation Mode is on; Show Take Lanes and Insert Take Lane switch Automation Mode off and show them, and inserting a take lane shows its track's take lanes. (S1, S2, S10, S11)
- **R25.** Left (S-COMP-007), with a take lane's header selected, selects its track's title bar and hides the track's take lanes. (S1)
- **R26.** Selecting on take lanes works as on tracks (arrangement editing R1, R2): clicking a take lane's header selects it, Cmd-click adds it or takes it away, and Shift-click takes in the lanes and tracks between; clicking a take-lane clip's title bar selects the clip; dragging across take lanes selects time on the lanes dragged across, Shift extending it over lanes and tracks; Cmd-clicking inside a time selection adds or takes away the clicked lane, and when it's the only lane selected moves the insert marker instead. Esc with no time selection moves the focus to the selected lanes' headers. With the Clip View or the Device View open, selecting a take lane's header selects its clips, and the Clip View shows a selected take-lane clip as it shows a main-lane one. `arrangement.select_time` and `arrangement.select_clips` name take lanes beside tracks, and `arrangement.select_take_lanes` selects headers; clicking a take lane's background places the insert marker and selects the lane; selecting is never an undo step. (S12, S13; arrangement editing R1, R2)
- **R27.** The Arrangement's edits act on take-lane clips and on time selected on take lanes as on the main lane: moving and copying clips, to another lane or track of the same kind (arrangement editing R4, R5), resizing, splitting, deleting, duplicating, cut, copy and paste, nudging, Consolidate and Crop (audio clip editing R29); each places and clears on the lane it acts on. A clip moved from a take lane to the main lane is moved, not copied, as any move is (`arrangement.move_clips` with a `lane`, which runs `clip.move_to_lane`). Their span arguments name take lanes beside tracks. A take-lane clip can be copied or dragged to a Session slot as a main-lane clip can. (S6; arrangement editing R4 to R12)
- **R28.** In the `take-lanes` context, with a take lane selected: Enter (R17), T (R15), Cmd+Up and Cmd+Down (R19 with clips or time selected, S-COMP-006; R8 with take lane headers selected, S-COMP-008), Cmd+D (R5 with take lane headers selected, arrangement editing R10 with clips or time), Delete (R7 with take lane headers selected, arrangement editing R9 with clips or time) and Left (R25, S-COMP-007). Cmd+R with take lane headers selected types a new name in the first selected lane's header, Return keeping it, Esc leaving the name, Tab and Shift+Tab moving to the next or previous lane or track's name. Option with + or − (S-ARR-022), or with the mouse wheel (I-SCROLL-006), makes the selected take lanes 16 points taller or shorter, from 18 to 800 points, as tracks are (arrangement view R14; `arrangement.set_track_height` with a take lane). Heights are view state. Every S-COMP shortcut is bound. (S2, S8)
- **R29.** Insert Take Lane in the Create menu (M-CREATE-004) is enabled with an audio or MIDI track selected. The track header's take-lane items work as [track context menu](track-context-menu.md) R10 says. Right-clicking a take lane's header, selecting it first unless it's selected, opens the take lane's menu (C-TAKE-001 to C-TAKE-008): Copy Selection to Main Lane, Insert Take Lane, Duplicate, Rename, Delete, Delete All Take Lanes, Delete All Unused Take Lanes and Paste Bounced Audio ([freeze and bounce](freeze-and-bounce.md) R25), in the order of its rows in menus.md, each running its command with its keys shown, and disabled where its command can't run. Right-clicking a take-lane clip, or a take lane's background, opens the clip's or the time selection's menu as on the main lane (arrangement editing R17), with Copy Selection to Main Lane added after Paste Bounced Audio, Beatlayer's own place. (S2, S6, S10, S11)

Beatlayer's own: the lane on the other tracks. (S4, S14)

**Commands and agents (WP-6.21, WP-6.22)**

- **R37.** Every command in this spec is registered with a schema, a description written for a model, and a permission class, runs by ID through the agent API, and, unless it's `read`, names where its effect reads back: the query tool's `track` (fields `take_lanes`, with each lane's ID, name, clips and audition, and `clips`, the main lane's), or `take_lane.comp_sources`. An agent names a take lane by its ID or by its place, as `track "Vox" / lane 2`. The selection forms are the commands' own: `take_lane.copy_to_main_lane`, `take_lane.replace_with_take`, `take_lane.audition`, `take_lane.insert` and `take_lane.duplicate` take what they act on from the selection when their arguments leave it out. `track.add_take_lane` goes on adding a take lane with the clips it's given, on an audio or MIDI track. (S16)
