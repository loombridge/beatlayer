# The Drum Synths: DS Kick, DS Snare, DS Clap, DS HH, DS Cymbal, DS Tom, DS FM and DS Clang

- **Status:** implemented
- **Work packages:** WP-8.24 (the shared rules and DS Kick, DS Snare, DS Tom and DS Clap: R1 to R14), WP-8.25 (DS HH, DS Cymbal, DS FM and DS Clang: R15 to R20). DS FM's operators are [Operator](operator.md)'s core (R1 to R3); a Drum Rack's pads and choke groups are [drum racks](drum-racks.md)'
- **Parity-matrix row:** Instruments: Drum Synths (Kick, Snare, Clap, Hi-hat, Cymbal, Tom, FM, Clang)
- **Checked against:** the Live 12 reference manual (online edition), read 30 October 2026 (chapter 33 "Max for Live Devices", section 33.1 "Max for Live Instruments": 33.1.1 to 33.1.8); the Live 12 release notes, 12.0 to 12.3.5 (the Drum Synths' fixes)

## The behaviour

The Drum Synths are eight small instruments, each a synthesised drum sound with a few controls: a kick, a snare, a hand clap, a hi-hat, a cymbal, a tom, an FM percussion voice and a cowbell-and-clave. Each is usually played from a Drum Rack pad: a note starts a hit at its velocity, the hit runs its own length, set by its Decay, and a pad's choke group cuts it. Each has a display along its top with some controls inside it; clicking the display's upper half plays a hit.

Live's Drum Synths are Max for Live devices; Beatlayer's are native, with Live's names. The manual gives each device's controls and what each does, but no ranges or curves, so every synthesis law below is Beatlayer's own, from common drum-synthesis practice.

## Rules

**Every Drum Synth (WP-8.24)**

- **R1.** Beatlayer ships the eight Drum Synths with Live's names and type IDs of its own: DS Kick (`beatlayer.ds_kick`), DS Snare (`beatlayer.ds_snare`), DS Clap (`beatlayer.ds_clap`), DS HH (`beatlayer.ds_hh`), DS Cymbal (`beatlayer.ds_cymbal`), DS Tom (`beatlayer.ds_tom`), DS FM (`beatlayer.ds_fm`) and DS Clang (`beatlayer.ds_clang`), instruments listed under the browser's Instruments label, each with factory presets of Beatlayer's own. (S1 to S8)
- **R2.** A note on starts a hit on its frame at its velocity; the hit runs for its own length whatever the note's length, so a note's end changes nothing. A new hit while one sounds cuts the old one with a 2 ms fade from its frame, so a device sounds one hit at a time. (S1 to S8; Beatlayer's own)
- **R3.** A hit's level is Volume (−∞ to 0 dB) times its velocity's amplitude share, v / 127, so velocity 127 plays at Volume and a hit never plays above it (rule G1). (S1 to S8, S9; Beatlayer's own law)
- **R4.** Decay reads in per cent, 0 to 100 % (each device its own default), on a logarithmic travel from 10 ms at 0 % to 4 s at 100 %: 10 ms × 400^(Decay / 100). It is the hit's time to fall 60 dB from its peak, within 5 %; the hit ends when it falls below −90 dB. (S1 to S8; the per cent is how Live's panel reads it, the range and the travel are Beatlayer's own)
- **R5.** A device's pitch control (Pitch in hertz for DS Kick, from 30 to 120 Hz; Pitch for DS Tom, 0 to 100, which is 43.1 Hz × 10^(Pitch / 100); Tune, 0 to 100 %, for the others, which is −24 semitones at 0 %, none at 50 % and +24 at 100 %) sets its sound's pitch whatever key plays it: the key chooses a pad, not a pitch, as a Drum Rack plays twelve steps whatever the set's tuning ([tunings](tunings.md) R14). (S1 to S8, S9; Beatlayer's own reading)
- **R6.** Each device draws a display across the top of its body as Live's panel lays it out (layout L-DEV-047): a line drawing of the instrument in Beatlayer's own style, a note name beside it where the device reads one (DS Kick and DS Tom: the note nearest the pitch, in Live's octave numbering), and its display parameters inside it, as large numbers with their units, boxes in pairs, or buttons, with three knobs under it. The hit's amplitude envelope, worked out from the parameters, is what `device.display` reads, with a reading `Note` for the note and, for DS Tom, `Pitch` for its hertz. Clicking the display's upper half previews a hit at velocity 100 through the track, without recording and without an undo step (`device.preview_hit`, `read`). (S5 to S8; the drawing and the command's name are Beatlayer's own)
- **R7.** Every control is a parameter, automatable and modulatable (rule G2), and each device's banks hold every one. (S9)
- **R8.** Every random part (noise, the clap's scatter) comes from the device's seeded generator and starts again with each hit, so a hit sounds the same every time at the same settings and two renders are identical. (S9)
- **R9.** A Drum Synth on a Drum Rack pad plays the notes the pad receives and is cut by its choke group as any instrument there is ([drum racks](drum-racks.md)). (S9)
- **R10.** Each device's output is the same at any block size, and nothing on the audio thread allocates, locks or blocks. (S9)

**DS Kick, DS Snare, DS Tom and DS Clap (WP-8.24)**

- **R11.** DS Kick: a sine at Pitch (30 to 120 Hz, 50 Hz by default) whose pitch starts Env (0 to 100 %) × 4 octaves higher and falls exponentially to Pitch with a 30 ms time constant; Attack (0 to 100 %) fades the sine in over up to 10 ms, smoothing its start; OT (0 to 100 %) mixes in its second and third harmonics, up to −6 dB and −12 dB; Drive (0 to 100 %) drives it through a soft clipper by up to 24 dB with the output scaled back by half; Click (off by default) adds a 1 ms band-passed click at 4 kHz; Decay (default 48.2 %); a new DS Kick opens at Pitch 43.3 Hz, Env 47.4 %, and Attack, OT and Drive at 0 %. The Ø button (the name the panel gives it) inverts the whole sound's polarity, off by default. (S1; Beatlayer's own, with the defaults and the Ø button as Live's panel shows them)
- **R12.** DS Snare: a body of two sines at 185 Hz and 330 Hz times 2^(Tune / 12), each falling a semitone over 20 ms, whose Color (0 to 100 %) morphs them towards triangles; and noise from the device's seed through the chosen filter (low-pass, high-pass or band-pass, at 2 kHz to 8 kHz with Color), mixed in by Tone (0 to 100 %); the body decays four times faster than the noise, the noise over Decay (default 25.0 %); Tune reads in per cent (R5, default 41.0 %), Color opens at 81 % and Tone at 51 %, and the filter's choices read LP, HP and BP, HP by default. (S2; Beatlayer's own, with the defaults and the names as Live's panel shows them)
- **R13.** DS Tom: a sine at Pitch (0 to 100, 29 by default, which is 84.0 Hz; R5) whose pitch starts Bend (0 to 100 %) × an octave higher and falls to Pitch over half of Decay; Tone (0 to 100 %) mixes in four resonant band-passes at the circular membrane's next ratios (physical models R1: 1.594, 2.136, 2.296, 2.653 times Pitch) excited by the hit; Color (0 to 100 %) raises a low-pass's cutoff from 1 kHz to 10 kHz and its gain by up to 6 dB; Decay (default 56.0 %); Bend opens at 31.0 %, Color at 43 % and Tone at 100 %. (S3, S9; Beatlayer's own, with the defaults as Live's panel shows them)
- **R14.** DS Clap: four bursts of noise from the device's seed, 8 ms apart, each decaying in 6 ms, through a band-pass at 1.2 kHz times 2^(Tune / 12), then Tail (0 to 100 %, 65 % by default) adds band-passed noise decaying over Decay (default 30.0 %); the claps go through two delay lines panned left and right whose times differ by Sloppy (0 to 100 %, which is 0 to 30 ms; 24 % by default), so they line up tightly at 0; Spread (0 to 100 %, 76 % by default) sets the pan from mono to hard left and right; Tone (0 to 100 %, 32 % by default) tilts the band-pass's brightness; Tune reads in per cent (R5, default 18.0 %). (S4; Beatlayer's own, with the units and defaults as Live's panel shows them)

**DS HH, DS Cymbal, DS FM and DS Clang (WP-8.25)**

- **R15.** DS HH: six square waves at inharmonic ratios (1, 1.342, 1.2312, 1.6532, 1.9523, 2.1523) times a base of 400 Hz × 2^(Pitch / 12), band-limited, summed with noise (white or pink, white by default) through a resonant high-pass at Tone (2 to 16 kHz) of 12 or 24 dB per octave (12 by default); Attack (0 to 100 %) fades the squares in over up to 20 ms, the noise starting at once; Decay (default 41.5 %, about 120 ms); Pitch reads in per cent (R5, default 50 %). (S5; Beatlayer's own)
- **R16.** DS Cymbal: the same six inharmonic squares at a base of 500 Hz × 2^(Pitch / 12), band-limited at every pitch, with noise, through a high-pass at Tone (1 to 12 kHz), decaying over Decay (default 83.6 %, about 1.5 s); Pitch reads in per cent (R5, default 50 %). (S6; Beatlayer's own)
- **R17.** DS FM: two operators of the FM core ([Operator](operator.md) R1, R3), a modulator into a carrier at 200 Hz × 2^(Pitch / 12); Amnt (0 to 100 %) sets the index up to 8, the modulator's envelope decaying twice as fast as the carrier's; Feedb. (0 to 100 %) is the modulator's feedback; Mod (0 to 100 %) blends the modulator's ratio through 1, 1.41, 2.76 and 3.5 in turn; Tone (200 Hz to 20 kHz) is a low-pass's cutoff; Decay (default 56.8 %, about 300 ms); Pitch reads in per cent (R5, default 50 %). (S7, S9; Beatlayer's own)
- **R18.** DS Clang: two square waves at 540 Hz and 800 Hz times 2^(Pitch / 12), band-limited, their levels Tone A and Tone B, through a high-pass and a band-pass at Filter, with white noise mixed in by Noise; with Clave on (off by default) the hit is a clave instead, a 2.5 kHz sine burst decaying in 20 ms, sounded Repeat times (1 to 8) 25 ms apart, each repeat on the frame that follows the hit's start by a whole number of 25 ms at any block size; Decay (default 53.7 %, about 250 ms); Pitch reads in per cent (R5, default 50 %). (S8; Beatlayer's own)
- **R19.** The metallic devices (DS HH, DS Cymbal, DS Clang) fold back nothing above −80 dB at the highest Pitch. (S5, S6, S8)
- **R20.** Every device of this part plays from a Drum Rack pad and keeps R1 to R10. (S9)
