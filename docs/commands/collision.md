## Commands

### `collision.copy_resonator`

**Copy Resonator**. Permission: `edit`. Contexts: `device-view`.

Copies every setting of one of Collision's two resonators to the other, as the Copy buttons of its resonator tabs do: `from` is 1 to copy Resonator 1 into Resonator 2, or 2 to copy Resonator 2 into Resonator 1. The device is a Collision, by ID or the selected one. Every parameter of the resonator is copied, its switch included, and the device's `params` read back the result. One undo step (Copy Resonator).

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `from` | integer from 0 to 255 | yes | The resonator to copy from, 1 or 2; its settings go to the other. |
| `device` | [`DeviceId`](README.md#deviceid) or null | no | The Collision; the selected device if left out. |

Result: [`ResonatorCopied`](README.md#resonatorcopied).
