## The behaviour

A clip can carry envelopes of its own — *clip envelopes* — for the parameters of its track: a control of the track's mixer, one of its sends, or a parameter of a device in its chain. Each is a list of breakpoints joined by straight or curved segments, drawn and edited exactly as a track's automation envelopes are, over the clip's contents. A clip envelope isn't the track's automation: while its clip plays it *modulates* the parameter, changing the value the control or the track's own envelope gives, and while the clip doesn't play it contributes nothing. The Envelopes tab's Linked switch chooses whether the envelope follows the clip's own timeline or runs on its own: linked, it plays what the clip's contents play and repeats with the clip's loop, as its notes do; unlinked, it plays once from the clip's start, so an eight-bar fade can run over a one-bar loop.

This spec covers the model, the saving, the commands, and the playback of clip envelopes in the engine, and the Clip View's editor for them. The Clip View's Envelopes tab, where a person draws and edits them, is R8 to R11. It leaves to other documents: a Session clip's Automation envelopes, which define values rather than modulating them, and what they do to the Arrangement's automation for the same parameter, in [session-automation.md](session-automation.md) (WP-3.12); the MIDI controller envelopes of a MIDI clip ("MIDI Ctrl"), in [midi-controller-data.md](midi-controller-data.md) (WP-2.29); and an audio clip's envelopes, which play as a MIDI clip's do when the clip is warped, over the beats of its contents, and from the clip's start, one tick per tick, when it isn't, because an unwarped clip's contents run at the file's own speed ([warping.md](warping.md) R16, WP-4.2).

## Rules

- **R1.** A clip carries clip envelopes of its own, one per parameter of its track it modulates (a Session clip may also carry an Automation envelope for each, which [session-automation.md](session-automation.md) R10 describes; every rule here is about the Modulation ones, the kind each command makes unless `kind` says otherwise): a control of the track's mixer (Volume, Pan, or the Track Activator), one of its sends to a return track, or a parameter of a device in its chain; an audio clip's can also name one of its own sound settings, on the scale [warp-modes.md](warp-modes.md) R29 gives. Each envelope is a list of breakpoints, each a position in ticks from the beginning of the clip's contents (as a note's start is), a value in modulation's own units ([modulation.md](modulation.md) R4: a share from 0 to 100 % for Volume and a send's level, from −100 % to 100 % for every other parameter), and the bend of the segment starting at it, in position order with no two at one position, exactly as a track's automation envelope is. (S1, S2, S4)
- **R2.** A linked clip envelope (the Linked switch, on as an envelope is made) plays what the clip's contents play: it runs from the clip's start marker over its region and loop brace, repeating with the clip's loop, the way the clip's notes play (`midi-clips.md`). An unlinked one plays once from the clip's start, one tick per tick, for as long as the clip lasts: an envelope longer than the clip's loop runs on while the loop repeats underneath. (S1)
- **R3.** While its clip plays, a clip envelope modulates the parameter it names: the parameter plays, on each frame, its own value there — the control's, or the track envelope's where one drives it — moved by the clip envelope's share of the parameter's travel (modulation R3 and R4: a level is brought down to that share of its position, pan moves a share of the way to the nearer end, any other parameter adds the share to its position), clamped to the travel's ends. The value the control or the track's envelope gives, its base, is never changed: when the clip stops or its envelope is cleared, the parameter plays its base again from the next frame. A clip envelope contributes nothing while its clip doesn't play, and while the song is stopped the parameters hold their values. A set saved before relative modulation loads with each envelope's values turned into the modulation that plays what they played (modulation R5). (S1, S3, S4)
- **R4.** The read path is the automation player's: a node reads each modulated parameter frame by frame, so a value reaches the parameter on its exact frame and a render is the same in any block size; reading allocates nothing, takes no lock, and its work per block is bounded by the block's frames; an envelope is held back by its node's delay, as a track envelope is (automation R4, R10). (S3, S4)
- **R5.** Every edit is a registered command — `clip.set_envelope_breakpoint`, `clip.set_envelope_curve`, `clip.move_envelope_breakpoint`, `clip.delete_envelope_breakpoint`, `clip.delete_envelope`, `clip.set_envelope_linked` — with a schema, a description written for a model, and a permission class, run by ID with JSON arguments, and each is one undo step. A position before 1.1.1, a value that isn't a number, a bend outside −1 to 1, a breakpoint that isn't there, a move onto another breakpoint, a segment that starts at no breakpoint (or at the last one, which has none after it), and a parameter the clip's track doesn't have are refused. Deleting the last breakpoint takes the envelope with it, and Clear Envelope takes the whole envelope and changes nothing when there is none. A linked switch thrown at a parameter with no envelope gives it an empty one. (S2, S3, S4)
- **R6.** The set saves every clip envelope: the clip it belongs to, the parameter it modulates, its breakpoints with each segment's bend where it isn't straight, and whether it's linked (a linked envelope doesn't write that flag). A set saved before this package loads with no clip envelopes; a loaded envelope that breaks a rule (a position before zero, two breakpoints at one position, positions out of order, a value that isn't a number, a bend outside −1 to 1, or a parameter the clip's track doesn't have) doesn't load. (S3, S4)
- **R7.** A clip envelope names a parameter of the track the clip is on: a device that leaves the chain (deleted, or moved to another track) takes the clip envelopes naming its parameters with it, and a clip moved to another track keeps only the envelopes naming parameters its new track has. A split clip's parts each keep the clip's envelopes whole, and the parts' timelines are their own. (S4, automation R11)
- **R8.** The Clip View's Envelopes tab (`view.clip_tab`, Option+Shift+2; [piano-roll.md](piano-roll.md) R22) edits the envelopes of the clip the Clip View shows, a Session clip or an Arrangement clip. Under the editor a row holds the device chooser (MIDI Ctrl first, [midi-controller-data.md](midi-controller-data.md) R8; then the track's Mixer, then each device in its chain), the control chooser (the chosen device's controls: Volume, Pan, the Track Activator and the sends for the Mixer; a device's automatable parameters, named by its descriptions) and the Linked switch, on for an envelope with none yet. `view.clip_envelope` chooses the parameter the tab shows (`null` for none), refuses one the shown clip's track doesn't have, and is view state: no undo step, not saved with the set. Choosing a device shows its first control. The editor is the note area's width and draws the envelope over the editing grid on the modulation scale (modulation R9: 0 to 100 % for Volume and sends, −100 % to 100 % for the rest) in the theme's modulation colour, and with no envelope drawn it shows a dashed line where no modulation plays: at the top for a level and at the centre for the rest. (S2, S3, S4)
- **R9.** The editor's gestures are the Arrangement's automation lanes' (automation R13) on the clip's envelope: a click on a breakpoint deletes it (`clip.delete_envelope_breakpoint`), a click on a segment adds a breakpoint on the line (`clip.set_envelope_breakpoint`), a double-click off the line adds one at the clicked value, dragging a breakpoint moves it in position and value (`clip.move_envelope_breakpoint` with `value`; Shift scales the value change by a fifth) and Option (Alt on Windows) with a drag on a segment curves it (`clip.set_envelope_curve`), whose double-click straightens it. Positions snap to the editing grid, and Cmd (Alt on Windows) bypasses it. A drag sends its command on each frame, measured from where it began, as one gesture and one undo step however long it lasts; Esc puts the envelope back. `clip.move_envelope_breakpoint` takes an optional `value`, so a drag is one command per frame: left out, the breakpoint keeps its value. (S1, S3, S4)
- **R10.** The Linked switch runs `clip.set_envelope_linked` with the opposite of the envelope's state, and is one undo step. The editor's left edge is the contents tick a linked envelope plays from, the clip's Start marker, and the clip's start for an unlinked one (R2), so the same envelope draws where the clip plays it; the marker strip above shows the clip's markers and loop on that axis. (S1, S2)
- **R11.** The tab's controls reach assistive technology: the tabs (selected, or disabled for MPE), the editor with the parameter it shows, the two choosers with their choices and the Linked switch with its state; Tab reaches the choosers and the switch, and the choosers step with Up and Down. The breakpoints themselves are reached by pointer, and by the keys of R14. (S3)
- **R12.** In the Envelope Editor, the clip's envelope is selected and edited as an automation lane's is ([automation-editing.md](automation-editing.md) R1 to R6): dragging across the editor's background selects a span of the envelope, selected breakpoints and segments move together, and Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate and Delete with the selection in the editor act on the shown envelope alone, in positions on the clip's contents (R1) and values on the modulation scale (R8), each one undo step. They're the clip commands `clip.move_envelope_breakpoints`, `clip.copy_envelope`, `clip.cut_envelope`, `clip.paste_envelope`, `clip.duplicate_envelope` and `clip.delete_envelope_range`, in the permission classes their automation counterparts have, read back by the query tool's clip (`envelopes`). Lock Envelopes doesn't concern clip envelopes, which are always the clip's. (S5, S6)
- **R13.** A time selection in the Envelope Editor offers the automation shapes in its context menu, and the stretch and skew handles and Simplify Envelope, as an automation lane's does ([automation-editing.md](automation-editing.md) R11 to R18), scaled to the envelope's modulation scale (R8) in place of the parameter's range: `clip.insert_envelope_shape`, `clip.stretch_envelope_values`, `clip.stretch_envelope_time`, `clip.skew_envelope` and `clip.simplify_envelope`, each `edit` and one undo step. (S5, S6)
- **R14.** The Envelope Editor takes the keys of [automation-editing.md](automation-editing.md) R20 to R29 on the shown envelope while the detail area has the focus: the insert marker along it, Enter, the arrows, typed values, Esc, Delete, Tab, Option-Up and Option-Down through the clip's envelopes and with Shift through every parameter the choosers list, and Cmd-Delete to clear it; this gives R11's breakpoints their keys. (S5, S6)
