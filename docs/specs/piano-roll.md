## The behaviour

The Clip View lives in the detail area (L-WIN-006), and its MIDI Note Editor edits one MIDI clip's notes. A key track with the keys' names runs down the editor's left (L-CLIP-012) and the clip's notes sit on the clip's editing grid to its right (L-CLIP-013). The editor shows the clip most recently selected, in the Arrangement or in a Session slot, from its start: the clip's contents fill the editor's width, and the key track scrolls with the wheel.

The person clicks a note to select it, Cmd-clicks (Ctrl-clicks) to add or remove one, drags a marquee to select the notes it runs over, drags a note to move it in time and pitch, and drags a note's end to resize it — all snapping to the editing grid, with Cmd (Alt on Windows) held to bypass snapping. With Draw Mode on (B, the Control Bar's switch, or Options ▸ Draw Mode), clicking empty grid draws a note one grid step long and clicking a note removes it. Every edit is one undo step however many frames its drag takes; Esc during a drag puts the set and the selection back; selecting is never a step.

Under the note area run the lanes (L-CLIP-014), which [note-editing.md](note-editing.md) describes. Fold (F, View ▸ Fold to Notes) hides the key rows without notes, so the notes sit on neighbouring rows and the empty rows between them aren't shown.

Every one of those actions is a registered command that an agent can run with the clip and the notes spelled out, and the mouse runs those same commands. Clicking empty grid also places the insert marker, a point in the clip's time that the arrow keys move, and dragging selects time as well as notes. With notes selected, the arrow keys move and lengthen them a grid step at a time, and Cut, Copy, Paste, Duplicate and Delete act on them, or on the notes in the time selection.

This spec covers drawing, selecting, moving, resizing, Fold, quantise, transpose, the clip's Start and End markers and loop brace in the editor, the insert marker and the time selection, and the note clipboard. It leaves to other specs and packages: how clips play the notes and what each note holds ([midi-clips](midi-clips.md), WP-2.1); the lanes and the footer, and the note operations ([note-editing.md](note-editing.md)); the note filters ([find-and-select-notes.md](find-and-select-notes.md)); the Preview switch ([step-recording.md](step-recording.md)); several clips at once ([multi-clip-editing.md](multi-clip-editing.md)); the Sample Editor, the Envelope Editor, and the MPE editor; and Fold to Scale, highlighting and the rows' names in a scale or a tuning ([scales.md](scales.md), [tunings.md](tunings.md)). The editor's zoom, scroll and Follow keys, the clip markers' keys, and the …Time commands inside a clip are R32 to R42.

## Rules

- **R1.** The Clip View shows in the detail area in place of the Device View while `view.toggle_clip_view` (S-VIEW-015, the Status Bar's switch L-STAT-004) is on, and its MIDI Note Editor shows the clip most recently selected, from either view: the Arrangement's first selected MIDI clip, or the MIDI clip in the Session slot selected last (session-view R33), until the other view selects one. It shows the key track down the editor's left and the note area on the clip's editing grid to its right. With an audio clip selected the Clip View shows the Sample Editor in its place ([sample-editor.md](sample-editor.md) R1), and with nothing selected an empty editor. The Notes tab edits a Session clip as it does an Arrangement clip. Switching it on and off changes only the view, never the set, and is no undo step. (S1, S3, S6)
- **R2.** Draw Mode (`view.draw_mode`; B, the Control Bar's switch L-CTRL-019, Options ▸ Draw Mode) changes what a click in the note area does (R8). Switching it changes only the view, never the set, and is no undo step. (S2, S3)
- **R3.** A note is named by its place in the clip's notes in order: the earliest start is place 0, and of notes that start together the lowest pitch comes first. The note commands act on the places they're given, or, when they're left out, on the notes selected in the Clip View. (S5, S7)
- **R4.** The note editor's time axis is the clip's contents: contents tick `MidiData::start` — where the clip starts playing — sits at the clip's start, and the clip's length fills the note area; only the clip's span of the contents, from its Start marker, is drawn and edited. The commands' positions and lengths are ticks in the contents, counted from their beginning. (S5, S7)
- **R5.** With Draw Mode off, clicking a note selects it alone; Cmd-clicking (Ctrl-clicking) it adds it to or removes it from the selection; clicking empty grid clears the selection. Selecting is never an undo step and changes nothing in the set. (S4, S6)
- **R6.** Dragging from empty grid draws a marquee, and every note the marquee runs over becomes the selection. The marquee is no undo step. (S6)
- **R7.** Dragging a note moves the grabbed note, or the selection when the grabbed note is in it (a grabbed note that isn't selected becomes the selection first), in time and in pitch: the grabbed note's start lands on the nearest grid line, and the pitch follows the pointer's key row, a whole semitone to a row. Nothing moves, and the set is unchanged, if any note would start before the clip's contents or would leave MIDI keys 0 to 127. (S4, S5, S6)
- **R8.** With Draw Mode on, a click on empty grid adds a note: at the clicked key, starting on the nearest grid line, one grid step long, at velocity 100, selected afterwards. A click on a note removes it. A drag with Draw Mode on does nothing. Each is one undo step ("Add note", "Delete notes"). (S2, S6, S7)
- **R9.** Dragging a note's end makes the note longer or shorter, its start staying put, the end landing on the nearest grid line; every note keeps at least one grid step of length while snapping, and a tick when the grid is bypassed. Nothing changes, and the edit is refused, if a note would come out shorter than a tick. (S6)
- **R10.** A drag in the note area snaps the axis it moves along, in the clip's contents, to the nearest grid line, and holding Cmd (Alt on Windows) while dragging does the opposite of the grid's setting: it bypasses snapping, or snaps with the grid off. (S2, S6)
- **R11.** Every edit is one undo step however many frames its drag lasts, and a drag's Esc puts the set and the selection back as they were and records nothing. Selecting, Draw Mode, and the Clip View's switches are never steps. (S6)
- **R12.** The note area shows a window of the key track: a row for each key, named on every row when the rows are tall enough and on the C rows otherwise; the wheel scrolls the window by three rows to a notch. The names the rows carry follow the note spelling chosen ([scales.md](scales.md) R24) and, on a track with a tuning, the tuning's own names ([tunings.md](tunings.md) R29). (S1, S20)
- **R13.** Every edit is a registered command, and the mouse and keys run the same commands an agent does: `note.add`, `note.move`, and `note.resize` in the edit class, `note.delete` in the destructive class, and `clip_view.select_notes` in the read class; the view switches are read. (S6, S7)

- **R16.** Fold (`view.fold_notes`; F, View ▸ Fold to Notes, M-VIEW-039) shows only the key rows that hold notes, one under the next, and hides the empty rows between them; every note keeps its pitch. Folded, a drag in the note area moves a note row by row, and a click in Draw Mode lands on a row that shows. Folding is view state: it changes nothing in the set and isn't an undo step, and it is saved with the set beside the song, as the floating devices' windows are, so a reopened set shows the same folded editors and a new set starts unfolded. Folded with no notes, the editor draws nothing, because no row shows. Fold to Scale (G, M-VIEW-040) is [scales.md](scales.md) R23's. (S8, S9, S20)
- **R17.** Every lane and fold action is a registered command, and the mouse, the key, and the lane header's menu run the same commands an agent does: `note.set_value` and `note.ramp_value` in the edit class, and `view.lane` and `view.fold_notes` in the read class. (S7)
- **R18.** `note.quantize` (S-NOTE-011, Cmd-U) moves each note of the selection, or of `notes`, `amount` percent of the way from its start — or its end, with `target` `note_end`, leaving the start alone — to the nearest grid line of `grid`. The lines are laid out from each bar's start and a spacing of whole bars counts bars from 1.1.1, as the editing grid's are. 100 percent lands the note on the line, 50 percent halves its distance to it, and 0 changes nothing (no undo step). A quantise that would leave a note shorter than a tick is refused, changing nothing. One undo step, and the quantised notes stay selected. (S5, S6, S7)
- **R19.** `clip_view.quantize_settings` reads and changes the values R18's arguments leave out — `grid` (a positive spacing in ticks; unset it follows the note editor's editing grid), `target`, and `amount` from 0 to 100 — keeping the fields left out; the defaults are the editor's grid, note starts, and 100 percent. Like Draw Mode, the settings are view state: they change nothing in the set and are no undo step. The Quantize Settings dialog (M-EDIT-036, Cmd-Shift-U) is not built, so the menu item shows disabled and agents reach the settings through the command, and S-NOTE-012 stays unbound. (S6, S7)
- **R20.** With notes selected, Up and Down transpose them by a semitone and Shift-Up and Shift-Down by an octave (S-A11Y-008, S-A11Y-009), running `note.move`, as a vertical drag does. A transpose that would leave MIDI keys 0 to 127 is refused, changing nothing. (S4, S5, S6)
- **R21.** The editor ruler and marker strip (L-CLIP-011) shows the clip's Start and End markers and its loop brace on the same contents-tick axis as the note area (R4), the brace in the on and off colours the Arrangement's brace wears; the strip spans the Clip View's width, with the key track starting below it (L-CLIP-012), and draws no Loop switch of its own. Dragging a marker sets it to the contents tick under the pointer with the grid's snapping (Cmd, Alt on Windows, bypasses it, as R10 says); dragging an end of the brace moves that end, and dragging the body moves the whole brace with its length. The Start marker stays before the End marker and, while the loop is on, before the brace's end. Each drag is one gesture and one undo step however many frames it lasts, and Esc puts the clip back. The commands are the document's `clip.set_midi_region` and `clip.set_midi_loop`; the loop's switch stays `clip.set_loop_enabled`, whose control lives in the Main Properties panel (L-CLIP-004). (S1, S5, S6)
- **R22.** The editor toolbar along the Clip View's top (L-CLIP-010) holds the Notes, Envelopes and MPE tabs, 61 points apart and starting 436 points from the Clip View's left edge, and the editing grid's chooser at its right end; the marker strip, the note area and the lane sit under it. `view.clip_tab` shows a tab by name (`notes` or `envelopes`) and, with no name, goes on to the next, round to the first: Option+Shift+1 shows Notes (S-CLIPMODE-003), Option+Shift+2 shows Envelopes (S-CLIPMODE-004), and Option+Tab goes on to the next tab in the note editor and the sample editor (S-CLIPMODE-001, S-CLIPMODE-002). The tab is view state: no undo step, not saved with the set. The MPE tab is drawn disabled until its own package, and the cycle skips it. While the Envelopes tab shows, the keys of the `envelope` context apply in place of the note editor's, and the Notes tab's editing waits. The grid chooser lists the adaptive settings and then the fixed spacings, as the Arrangement's context menu does, and runs `arrangement.set_grid`. (S1, S3, S6)

**The insert marker, the time selection, the keyboard and the clipboard (WP-6.53)**

- **R23.** With Draw Mode off, a click on empty grid also places the editor's insert marker at the clicked tick of the clip's contents, on the nearest grid line while snapping is on (Cmd, Alt on Windows, does the opposite, as R10 says), and clears the time selection (R24). The marker is a flashing line across the note area and the lanes. With no notes selected, Left and Right move it one grid step (S-NOTE-045), Option-Left and Option-Right (Ctrl on Windows; S-A11Y-006) move it to the previous or next note start or end in the clip's span, and Home and End (S-NOTE-017, S-NOTE-018) move it to the first and last tick of the clip's span (R4). It stays inside the span. Placing or moving it changes nothing in the set and is never an undo step. (S10, S13, S15)
- **R24.** The editor has a time selection: a range of the clip's contents drawn across the note area and the lanes. A marquee (R6) makes the time it spans the time selection. With no notes selected, Shift-Left and Shift-Right (S-A11Y-004) select time from the insert marker a grid step at a time, or move the far end of the time selection a grid step; Option-Shift-Left and Option-Shift-Right (Alt-Shift on Windows; S-NOTE-046) move that end to the next or previous note start or end. Selecting notes by a click, a marquee, the keys or Select All makes the time from their earliest start to their latest end the time selection. Enter (S-A11Y-005) with time selected and no notes selected selects the notes that start inside it, keeping the time selection as it was; with notes selected it deselects them and keeps the time selection. Esc, a click outside the time selection, or a key that moves the insert marker clears it. None of this changes the set or is an undo step. (S10, S13, S14; which notes Enter takes is Beatlayer's own)
- **R25.** Option-Down and Option-Up (Alt on Windows; S-NOTE-019) select the note whose start is nearest the insert marker, the lowest-pitched of notes that start together, when no note is selected, and otherwise the previous or next note in the clip's order (R3), in place of the selection. Option-Left and Option-Right (S-NOTE-020) with a note selected select the previous or next note at the same pitch. With Shift too (S-NOTE-043, S-A11Y-007) the note is added to the selection. Cmd-A (Ctrl-A; S-NOTE-001, Edit ▸ Select All with the editor focused) selects every note in the clip's span, and Esc (S-NOTE-042) deselects them. (S10, S13, S15; the tie between notes that start together is Beatlayer's own)
- **R26.** Shift-clicking a note adds it to the selection, or takes it out when it's selected, as Cmd-click (Ctrl-click) does (R5). Clicking a key in the piano ruler (L-CLIP-012) selects every note at that pitch in the clip's span; Shift-clicking a key adds those notes to the selection, or takes them out when they're all selected. Selecting is never an undo step. (S10, S11)
- **R27.** With notes selected, Left and Right (S-A11Y-011) move them one grid step earlier or later (`note.move`), and Shift-Left and Shift-Right (S-A11Y-010) make them one grid step shorter or longer (`note.resize`); a press that R7 or R9 refuses changes nothing. Each press is one undo step, and the time selection follows the notes (R24). (S10, S13, S14)
- **R28.** Copy (Cmd-C, Ctrl-C; S-EDIT-002, Edit ▸ Copy) puts the selected notes, or with none selected the notes that start inside the time selection, on the note clipboard, keeping their pitches, values and places in time from the earliest of them. Cut (Cmd-X) copies them and deletes them, and Delete (S-EDIT-005) deletes them. Paste (Cmd-V) puts the clipboard's notes into the clip at the insert marker, or at the time selection's start, with their pitches, values and spacing, and selects them. Duplicate (Cmd-D) puts a copy of the notes straight after the time selection, selects the copy and moves the time selection onto it, so pressing it again lays down another. The note clipboard is separate from the Arrangement's clip clipboard ([arrangement-editing.md](arrangement-editing.md) R11). Copy changes nothing in the set; Cut, Delete, Paste and Duplicate are one undo step each. (S12, S15; Duplicate's place and the separate clipboard are Beatlayer's own)
- **R29.** Option-dragging (Ctrl-dragging on Windows) a note copies instead of moving (S-NOTE-002, I-DRAG-024): the notes stay where they are, their copies land where R7 would have moved them and become the selection. One undo step, and Esc puts everything back. (S4, S15)
- **R30.** Right-clicking a note, which selects it alone first if it isn't selected, or empty grid opens the MIDI Note Editor's context menu with menus.md's C-NOTE rows. Each item runs the command its shortcut runs, on the selection, and an item whose command isn't built yet is drawn disabled. (S12, S15)
- **R31.** Every action here is a registered command, and the mouse and the keys run the same commands an agent does: `clip_view.set_insert_marker` and `clip_view.select_time`, which take a MIDI clip's contents ticks as `beat`, `start` and `end` as they take a warped clip's, and `clip_view.select_notes`, which also selects the nearest, previous, next, same-pitch, every, and a key's notes, all in the read class; `note.copy` (read), `note.cut` and `note.delete` (destructive), and `note.paste`, `note.duplicate`, `note.move` and `note.resize` (edit). `view.state`'s `clip_view` reports the insert marker and the time selection, and the query tool's clip the notes. (S7)

**Zooming, scrolling, the clip markers and the …Time commands (WP-6.54)**

- **R32.** Z (S-NOTE-023) zooms the note area to the time selection and to the key rows of the selected notes; X (S-NOTE-024) goes back to the view before the last zoom, a step at a time, and with no zoom to go back to shows the whole clip; W (S-NOTE-009) fits the clip's span to the note area's width; H (S-NOTE-010) fits the key rows from the clip's lowest note to its highest to the note area's height; Plus and Minus (S-NOTE-025) zoom time in and out about the time selection, or about the insert marker with none. Each runs `clip_view.note_view` and changes only the view. (S16, S17; X going back a step, as the Sample Editor's does, and showing the whole clip after the last step, is Beatlayer's reading of 10.2 and 42.12)
- **R33.** Page Up and Page Down (S-NOTE-013) scroll the key rows an octave, and with Shift (S-NOTE-014) one row; Cmd-Page Up and Cmd-Page Down (Ctrl on Windows; S-NOTE-015) scroll time by the note area's width (the amount is Beatlayer's own). (S16, S17, S18)
- **R34.** Dragging up or down in the editor ruler zooms time about the pointer, and dragging sideways scrolls it; Cmd (Ctrl on Windows) with the wheel over the note area zooms time. Dragging sideways in the piano ruler, or Option (Alt) with the wheel over the note area, makes the key rows taller or shorter. Double-clicking the piano ruler fits the selected notes' rows to the height, or with none the clip's lowest note to its highest; double-clicking the editor ruler zooms to the time selection, or with none from the first note to the last; with the selection out of view, either double-click zooms out until it shows. None of these changes the set or is an undo step, and scrolling or dragging a ruler is no zoom that X goes back through. (S16)
- **R35.** Option-Shift-F (S-NOTE-021) and the Control Bar's Follow switch make the note area follow playback, turning the page when the playhead leaves it as the Arrangement does ([arrangement-view.md](arrangement-view.md) R10, `arrangement.follow`); an edit in the editor pauses it, and Follow again, starting or stopping playback, or a click in the editor's scrub area resumes it, as in the Sample Editor ([sample-editor.md](sample-editor.md) R11). (S16, S17, S20)
- **R36.** The Clip View Selector in the Status Bar (L-STAT-005) shows the whole clip the editor shows, with an outline round the part in view: dragging inside the outline sideways scrolls the note area and up or down zooms it, and dragging the outline's left or right edge widens or narrows the part in view. (S16)
- **R37.** Edit ▸ Clip Markers (M-EDIT-013a to M-EDIT-013d), the same submenu in the editor's context menu (C-NOTE-014), and Cmd-F9 to Cmd-F12 (Ctrl on Windows; S-LOOP-001 to S-LOOP-004) set the shown clip's Start marker and loop start to the time selection's start, and its loop end and End marker to its end; with no time selection, to the insert marker; with neither, while the clip plays, to where it is playing. Each is one undo step through `clip.set_midi_region` or `clip.set_midi_loop`, and one that R21's order between the markers forbids is refused. (S17, S18; the insert marker and the play position as fallbacks are Beatlayer's own)
- **R38.** With the Start or End marker selected (the `loop-brace` context), Cmd-click (Ctrl-click; S-LOOP-005) moves the Start marker to the clicked tick and Cmd-Shift-click (S-LOOP-006) the End marker, and Shift-Left and Shift-Right (S-NOTE-022) move the clip's region, its Start and End markers together, one grid step. Each is one undo step. (S17)
- **R39.** With the note editor focused, Edit ▸ Duplicate Time (M-EDIT-021) and Edit ▸ Delete Time (M-EDIT-022), on the keys the Arrangement gives them (Cmd-Shift-D and Cmd-Shift-Delete, S-ARR-018 and S-ARR-019), and Insert Time, act on the shown clip's contents, never moving its Start or End marker or its loop brace. Duplicate Time puts a copy of the notes that start in the time selection straight after it and moves the notes from its end on later by its length; Delete Time deletes the notes that start in the time selection, shortens a note that runs into it to end at its start, and moves the notes from its end on earlier by its length; Insert Time puts empty time as long as the time selection before it, moving the notes from its start on later by its length. Each is one undo step: `note.duplicate_time` and `note.insert_time` (edit) and `note.delete_time` (destructive). With no time selection each is refused. (S12, S19; what happens to a note across an edge is Beatlayer's own, after [time-editing.md](time-editing.md) R15 and R20)
- **R40.** While the note editor shows a clip and has a time selection, Crop Clip on that clip (its menus, and Cmd-Shift-J, S-ARR-003 in the Arrangement and S-SES-028 in the Session View) crops it to the editor's time selection, as [audio-clip-editing.md](audio-clip-editing.md) R17 says, writing no file; in the Arrangement, a time selection of the Arrangement's own inside the clip comes first ([audio-clip-editing.md](audio-clip-editing.md) R16). (S19, S20; which selection comes first is Beatlayer's own)
- **R41.** The Quantize Settings item (M-EDIT-036, Cmd-Shift-U, S-NOTE-012) with the note editor focused is [midi-transformations.md](midi-transformations.md) R21's. (S20)
- **R42.** Every action here is a registered command, run by the keys, the mouse, the menus and agents alike: `clip_view.note_view` (read: zoom to the selection, back, the whole clip, the height, in and out, a span of ticks and of key rows, and scrolling, as `clip_view.sample_view` does for the Sample Editor), `arrangement.follow`, `clip.set_midi_region` and `clip.set_midi_loop` (edit), `note.duplicate_time` and `note.insert_time` (edit), `note.delete_time` (destructive) and `clip.crop`. `view.state`'s `clip_view` reports the span and the key rows in view. (S7, S20)

**Colour and panning (WP-6.49b)**

- **R43.** Each note is drawn in its clip's colour, its saturation scaling linearly with its velocity from the theme's palest at 1 to the full colour at 127 (the line is Beatlayer's own); a deactivated note is grey whatever its velocity. (S21)
- **R44.** Dragging with the middle mouse button held over the note area pans it both ways within its scrolling limits, selecting and moving no note ([view-options.md](view-options.md) R1). (S18, S20)
