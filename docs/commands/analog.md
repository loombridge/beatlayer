## Commands

### `analog.quick_routing`

**Quick Routing**. Permission: `edit`. Contexts: `device-view`.

Sets Analog's filters, amplifiers and balances to one of Quick Routing's four routings, as its buttons do: `routing` is `parallel` (both filters and amplifiers on, Oscillator 1 and the noise to Filter 1, Oscillator 2 to Filter 2, Filter 1's To F2 at 0 % and the amplifiers centred), `split` (as parallel, the amplifiers panned hard left and right), `filter_1_only` (Filter 2 and Amplifier 2 off, every source to Filter 1) or `serial` (every source to Filter 1, To F2 at 100 %, Amplifier 1 off and Amplifier 2 on). No oscillator's level or tuning changes. The device is an Analog, by ID or the selected one, and its `params` read back the result. One undo step (Quick Routing).

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `routing` | [`QuickRouting`](README.md#quickrouting) | yes | The routing to set. |
| `device` | [`DeviceId`](README.md#deviceid) or null | no | The Analog; the selected device if left out. |

Result: [`QuickRoutingSet`](README.md#quickroutingset).
