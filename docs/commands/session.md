## Commands

### `session.back_to_arrangement`

**Back to Arrangement**. Permission: `read`. Contexts: `global`.

Returns every track to the Arrangement, as the Back to Arrangement button and F10 do. A Session clip that started has taken its track over from the Arrangement, and stays so after it stops, until this runs. It stops every track's Session clips at once, not on the launch quantisation grid, and drops launches and stops still queued; the Arrangement plays again from the playhead and the transport keeps running. It changes nothing in the set.

Default keys:

| Shortcut | macOS | Windows | Context | Action in the reference | Binding |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S-TRANS-009 | `F10` | `F10` | `global` | Return every track to the Arrangement (Back to Arrangement) | runs it |

Arguments: none (send `{}`).

Result: nothing (`null`).

### `session.back_to_arrangement_track`

**Back to Arrangement (Track)**. Permission: `read`. Contexts: `global`.

Returns one track to the Arrangement, as the button in the track's Arrangement header does: its Session clip stops at once, a launch or a stop still queued on it is dropped, and its Arrangement clips play again from the playhead. Other tracks are not touched, and the transport keeps running. A track nothing took over just keeps playing its Arrangement. It changes nothing in the set.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `track` | [`TrackId`](README.md#trackid) | yes | The track. |

Result: nothing (`null`).

### `session.cancel_scene_launch`

**Cancel Scene Launch**. Permission: `read`. Contexts: `session`.

Cancels the launch of the scene at `scene` that is still waiting for its line of the launch quantisation grid, as the scene menu's Cancel Scene Launch does: the clips of that scene waiting to start never start, one waiting to restart plays on, the stops that launch queued on the clips' tracks are dropped, and the scene's tempo and time signature never apply. Clips already playing keep playing and stopped ones stay stopped. A launch that has happened isn't undone, and with nothing of the scene waiting the command is refused. The slots' states, `queued` among them, show in `session.state`'s `launches`. It changes nothing in the set.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scene` | [`SceneId`](README.md#sceneid) | yes | The scene. |

Result: nothing (`null`).

### `session.capture_and_insert_scene`

**Capture and Insert Scene**. Permission: `edit`. Contexts: `session`.

Inserts a new scene holding copies of the clips that are playing, each in its own track's slot, as Create > Capture and Insert Scene (Cmd-Shift-I, Ctrl-Shift-I) does, and the playing clips carry on in the copies without a break. The scene goes after the scene launched last, or after the selected scene when none has been launched since the set opened, and its tempo and time signature are set to the ones in force now. With nothing playing it inserts an empty scene with the same tempo and signature, and launches nothing. One undo step. The playing clips are the engine's, so it needs an engine that runs.

### `session.copy_to_arrangement`

**Copy Clips to Arrangement**. Permission: `edit`. Contexts: `global`, `session`.

Copies Session clips (`clips`, or the clips in the selected slots) into the Arrangement, as dragging them onto the Arrangement View, between the windows, or pasting them there does, and leaves the slots as they were. The first track's first clip starts at `at` (left out, the time selection's start or the insert marker) on `track` (left out, the first selected track); the clips of other tracks go to the tracks as far down as they were across the grid, and the clips of one track from several scenes follow one another in scene order, each as long as it is. Each keeps its contents, loop and warp settings, and its modulation envelopes where the new track has what they modulate. The copies replace what they cover (destructive, so an agent asks first, when that's part of another clip). A clip that would land on a track of the other kind, or past the last track, isn't placed, and the result counts them. The copies are selected afterwards. One undo step, `Copy Clips to Arrangement`.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `at` | [`Position`](README.md#position) or null | no | Where the first clip of each track starts. Left out, the time selection's start, or the insert marker. |
| `clips` | array or null | no | The Session clips to copy, by the IDs they have in their slots. Left out, the clips in the selected slots. |
| `track` | [`TrackId`](README.md#trackid) or null | no | The track the first clip goes to; the clips of other tracks go to the tracks after it, as far down as they were across the grid. Left out, the first selected track of the Arrangement. |

Result: [`CopiedClips`](README.md#copiedclips).

### `session.copy_to_session`

**Copy Clips to Session**. Permission: `edit`. Contexts: `global`, `session`.

Copies Arrangement clips (`clips`, or the selected clips) into Session slots, as dragging them onto the Session View, between the windows, or pasting them there does, and leaves the Arrangement as it was. The clips of one track go down the scenes from `scene` in the order they play, one clip a slot, and the clips of other tracks go across the tracks from `track`, so playing the scenes in turn plays them back in order; `track` and `scene` left out, the selected slot's. Scenes are added at the end when the clips run past the last. A clip landing on a full slot replaces its clip (destructive, so an agent asks first); a clip that would land on a track of the other kind, a group, a return or Main isn't placed, and the result counts them. The copies' slots are selected afterwards. One undo step, `Copy Clips to Session`.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `clips` | array or null | no | The Arrangement clips to copy. Left out, the selected clips. |
| `scene` | [`SceneId`](README.md#sceneid) or null | no | The scene whose slot takes the first clip of each track; its next clips go down the scenes after it. Left out, the selected slot's. |
| `track` | [`TrackId`](README.md#trackid) or null | no | The track whose slot takes the first clip; the clips of other tracks go to the tracks after it. Left out, the selected slot's. |

Result: [`CopiedClips`](README.md#copiedclips).

### `session.drop_files`

**Drop Files into a Slot**. Permission: `external`. Contexts: `session`.

Puts audio files dropped from the browser, Finder, or Explorer into the Session slot at `track` and `scene`, as dropping them on a slot does: one after another down that track's scenes, or, with `spread` (Cmd, Ctrl on Windows and Linux, held while dropping), across the tracks at `scene`. Each target slot must be empty. Each file becomes a Session clip of its length, starting at the clip's beginning. One undo step. Reads the files, outside the project, and registers them so the set keeps them. Clip files (`.blclip`) are placed as clips: in an empty slot on a track of their kind, bringing the track's devices when it has no clips and no devices; with no `track` (the Session's drop area), each on a new track of its kind in the scene, named after the file, with its devices; with `spread`, each on a track of its own across the tracks at `scene`. A track of the other kind, a group, return or Main track, and a slot holding a clip take none, and the command is refused.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `paths` | array of string | yes | The files' paths, in the order dropped. |
| `scene` | [`SceneId`](README.md#sceneid) | yes | The scene whose slot the first one goes in. |
| `spread` | boolean | no | With the command modifier (Cmd, Ctrl on Windows and Linux) held, spread them across the tracks instead of down the scenes. |
| `track` | [`TrackId`](README.md#trackid) or null | no | The track whose slot they go in. Left out, the Session's drop area: clip files make a new track of their kind each, in the scene (audio files need a track). |

Result: [`ChangeSet`](README.md#changeset).

### `session.launch`

**Launch Clip**. Permission: `read`. Contexts: `session`.

Launches the clip in a track's Session slot at `scene`, as clicking the slot's launch button does. While the song plays the clip starts on the next line of the global launch quantisation grid, and until then the slot reads `queued`; while it's stopped the transport starts at once from the Arrangement position and the clip starts with it. A clip already playing on that track stops where the new one starts. The clip's launch mode (`clip.set_launch_mode`) says what the press does next: a Trigger, Gate or Repeat clip that already plays restarts on the grid, and a Toggle clip that plays is queued to stop. A Gate or Repeat clip stops when `session.release` follows. The engine publishes the slots' states with the playhead, so the UI can draw them. It changes nothing in the set.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `track` | [`TrackId`](README.md#trackid) | yes | The track whose slot it is. |
| `scene` | [`SceneId`](README.md#sceneid) | yes | The scene whose slot it is. |

Result: nothing (`null`).

### `session.nudge`

**Nudge Clip**. Permission: `read`. Contexts: `session`.

Moves the playing clip in the slot at `track` and `scene` by `steps` steps of the global launch quantisation, as the Launch Properties panel's Nudge Backward and Nudge Forward buttons do, at once and not on the grid: a negative `steps` moves it back, from -64 to 64 and never 0. A step is the length of the global value at the playhead, or a thirty-second note with the value None or the switch off. Forward, the clip goes on round its loop as if it had played that much longer. Backward, where the move would fall before the clip's start, a looping clip goes the fewest whole loops later and a clip without Loop goes to its start. A slot that is empty, stopped or still waiting to start changes nothing, and a launch or a stop already queued keeps its frame. The slot's `clip_time` in `session.state`'s `slot_states` shows the move. It changes nothing in the set.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `track` | [`TrackId`](README.md#trackid) | yes | The track whose slot it is. |

### `session.overdub`

### `session.paste`

### `session.record`

**Record to Session**. Permission: `external`. Contexts: `global`, `session`.

The Session Record button (Playback ▸ Record to Session, Cmd+Shift+F9; Ctrl+Shift+F9 on Windows and Linux). Records every armed track into the Session View: a MIDI track whose clip is playing overdubs it, one note pass at a time; any other armed track records a new clip into the selected scene's slot if it is empty, else into the next empty slot below it on that track, and a scene is inserted at the end when there is none. The recording starts on the next line of the launch quantisation grid while the song plays, and from the playhead, after any count-in, while it is stopped, which starts the transport. The clip exists from the start and grows as it records. While a recording is under way the command ends it instead, as pressing the button again does: the new clips play on as loops from the next grid line. Audio goes to the project's Samples/Recorded/; the set must have been saved. Recording captures input devices, including a microphone, so the person is asked first. To end a recording without asking, run `session.stop_recording`.
