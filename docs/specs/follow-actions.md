# Follow actions on clips and scenes

- **Status:** implemented
- **Work packages:** WP-3.10
- **Parity-matrix row:** Clips and launching: "Follow actions on clips and scenes"
- **Checked against:** the Live 12 reference manual, chapter 16 "Launching Clips", section 16.7, read 17 October 2026, and Live 12.4.3 on macOS, observed on 17 October 2026

## The behaviour

A Session clip or a scene can do something by itself once it has played for a while: stop, play again, or launch another clip of its track (or another scene). Each has a Follow Action switch, off by default, two actions A and B with the chance that splits them, and a follow time. When the time has passed after the clip started (or the scene launched), Beatlayer picks A or B by their chances and runs it. The choices are drawn from a generator seeded by the clip, so two renders of one set make the same choices. A global switch turns every follow action off and on.

The follow time of a clip is Linked (a multiple of the clip's loop, or the clip's end when its Loop switch is off) or Unlinked (a length in bars.beats.sixteenths). A scene's is the same with Longest in place of Linked: the multiple is of the scene's longest clip.

This spec covers the actions, the chance, the follow time, the global switch, scene follow actions, the seeded draw, the controls in the Launch Properties panel and the Scene View, and the two selection commands (Shift+Enter, Create Follow Action Chain). It leaves to other specs how a launch is queued and quantised ([launch-quantisation.md](launch-quantisation.md)) and the grid ([session-view.md](session-view.md)).

## Rules

- **R1.** A clip's and a scene's follow actions are off by default, with A Next, B No Action, A at 100 % and B at 0 %, Jump targets of 1, Linked with a multiplier of 1x, and an Unlinked time of one bar. They save with the clip or the scene and are left out of the file while they are the defaults, so a set saved before this package loads with them. (S1, S5)
- **R2.** The follow time of a clip counts from the launch tick of its start or restart; when it has passed the follow runs on its exact frame, whatever the audio block size, because the engine splits its block there as it does for a launch. The follow of a scene counts from the tick the scene launched on. A clip's settings are read when it starts, so an edit to a clip already playing takes effect at its next start. (S5, S7, S11)
- **R3.** Each of A and B has its own Jump target, a slot number (a scene number for a scene) from 1 to the number of scenes, or 0 for none; a number past the scenes is held at their count. A Jump to an empty slot, to a scene with no clip, to 0 or to a number past the scenes stops the clip at the follow time (a scene's clips, for a scene); the transport keeps running. (S3)
- **R4.** A and B are one split of whole percents that add up to 100: the command sets A's chance and B has the rest, and a chance over 100 is refused. A runs with the probability of its chance over 100, so 100 % always runs A and 0 % never does. (S4)
- **R5.** A clip's group is the run of occupied slots around it on its track, split by empty slots. Next at the group's last clip launches the first, Previous at the first launches the last, First and Last are the group's, Any may pick the clip itself, and Other never does unless it is the only clip. A clip alone in its group relaunches itself on each of them. The target launches on the follow time, and the clip playing stops where it starts. (S1, S2)
- **R6.** Stop stops the clip at the follow time without touching the transport, No Action leaves the clip playing, and Play Again relaunches it at the follow time, when its follow time counts again. (S6)
- **R7.** The Linked multiplier is a whole number from 1 to 999999 and the Unlinked time runs from 0.0.1 (a sixteenth) up, a bar being a 4/4 bar here (`shortcut:` the time is held in ticks, so it doesn't follow a set's other signatures); the commands hold a number past a limit at the limit. (S5)
- **R8.** The global switch (`session.set_follow_actions_enabled`, the button at the right of Main's status row) is on in a new set, saves with the set beside the launch quantisation, and is no undo step. With it off no follow action runs, whatever a clip's or scene's own Follow Action switch says; a clip that started while it was off follows nothing until it starts again. (S1, S11)
- **R9.** A Linked clip with its Loop switch on follows after the multiplier times its loop brace, and with Loop off at its clip end (the multiplier does not apply); an Unlinked clip follows after its time. An Unlinked time longer than the clip leaves a looping clip looping, and a clip with Loop off silent after its end, until the time; a shorter one runs the action mid-clip and the old clip plays until the new launch takes over. `shortcut:` an audio clip's loop brace is in seconds, so it counts the clip's length; convert it when warping brings audio loops in ticks. (S5)
- **R10.** A follow launch ignores the global quantisation: a target clip on Global starts exactly on the follow tick, and a target with its own launch quantisation starts on the first line of its grid at or after the follow tick. (S8)
- **R11.** A scene's actions run over the scenes as a clip's run over its slots, a scene with no clips splitting the groups (R5), and Stop stops the scene's clips. They run only when the scene itself is launched, by its button, `session.launch_scene` or another scene's follow, not when one of its clips is, and the clips' own follow actions keep running meanwhile. The scene launched last is the one that runs; launching another, Stop All Clips and Back to Arrangement end it. With Longest on, the time is the multiplier times the scene's longest clip (that clip's end if its Loop switch is off), and with it off the scene's own time. (S7, S11)
- **R12.** Every choice (A or B, Any, Other) comes from a generator seeded by the clip's, or the scene's, ID: a hash of the seed and the song frame the clip started on, so two renders of one set make the same choices bit for bit at any block size, and over many starts the split matches the chance. (S1, S11)
- **R13.** The Launch Properties panel shows a Session clip's Follow Action switch, its actions A and B with a Jump target box beside each while it is Jump, their chances, and Linked with its multiplier ("Clip End" while Loop is off) or the Unlinked time as bars.beats.sixteenths. Each control runs `clip.set_follow_actions` and shows the saved value back; B's chance moves A's the other way. (S5, S10)
- **R14.** The Scene View shows the same controls for a scene, with Longest in place of Linked, and they run `scene.set_follow_actions`. (S7, S10)
- **R15.** Shift+Enter (`session.toggle_follow_actions`) switches the selected clips' Follow Action on, or off when they are all on already. One undo step. (S9)
- **R16.** Create Follow Action Chain (`session.create_follow_action_chain`, Cmd+Shift+Enter) chains the selected clips, a chain for each track in scene order: every clip gets the switch on, A Next at 100 %, B No Action and Linked 1x, except that a clip whose next selected clip is not the next slot gets A Jump to that slot, and the last clip gets A Jump to the first, so the chain loops within the selection. Loop switches don't change. One undo step. (S1, S9)
- **R17.** `clip.set_follow_actions` and `scene.set_follow_actions` change the fields they are given and leave the others, each as one undo step, and read back from the clip's `launch.follow` and the scene's `follow`; the first refuses a clip that isn't in a slot. Every command here is registered with a schema and a description and runs by ID. (S10)

## To observe in Live

- What Other does (it wasn't run), and whether Any and Other pick across the same groups.
- A scene's Jump target: whether it is a scene number as Beatlayer reads it, and what a Jump or a Next to a scene with no clips does.
- What Stop does on a scene (Beatlayer stops the scene's clips).
- Create Follow Action Chain on a selection with a filled slot between selected clips (Cmd-click): Beatlayer jumps over the gap to the next selected clip.
- Whether a follow that lands exactly on a target's own grid line starts there or a step later (Beatlayer starts there), and what a follow action does while a stop is queued by hand (Beatlayer leaves the stop).
- Whether the scene whose action runs ends when another scene launches, and what turning the global switch back on does to a clip already playing.
- The Unlinked time in a set whose time signature isn't 4/4.
- The countdown a track's status field draws while a follow time runs (m:ss when Unlinked, the loop count when Linked).
- Typing values into the follow controls: Beatlayer's boxes step with Up and Down and drag.
