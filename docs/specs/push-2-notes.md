## The behaviour

In Note Mode the 64 pads play the selected track. On a track whose instrument is a drum rack, or a Simpler slicing a sample, the pads take one of three drum layouts: the Loop Selector, which splits the grid into sixteen drum pads, sixteen loop length pads and 32 steps of a step sequencer; 16 Velocities, which puts sixteen velocities of the selected drum where the loop length pads were; and 64 Pads, which plays drums across the whole grid. On any other MIDI track the pads play notes of the set's scale, laid out in fourths, thirds or in sequence, in key or chromatically, or turn into a melodic step sequencer, alone or beside 32 playable notes. Selecting a MIDI track arms it, so it plays at once.

Push 2 records what it plays: Record starts a Session clip, stops it and overdubs it in turn, or records into the Arrangement when that has the focus; Fixed Length gives new clips a set length; Repeat retriggers a held pad in time; Accent plays at full velocity; Quantize moves notes onto the grid. The Automate button arms automation recording, and holding steps while turning an encoder writes automation for just those steps. Simpler shows its sample and its main controls in Device mode, in each of its three modes, with the controls to slice, crop, reverse and warp it, and Convert turns a Simpler, a drum pad or an audio clip into a new track or rack.

Every edit here runs the same registered command the app's own editors run, as one undo step, and every state the pads show is in `push.state`.

## Rules

**Note Mode on a track (WP-7.21)**

- **R1.** In Note Mode the pads take a drum layout when the selected track is a MIDI track whose chain's first instrument is a drum rack, or a Simpler in Slice mode, and a melodic layout on any other MIDI track, with or without an instrument. On an audio track, a return track or the Main track the pads are unlit and do nothing (Beatlayer's own). Changing the track's instrument, or Simpler's mode, changes the layout at once. (S1, S3, S6)
- **R2.** What the pads play reaches the tracks as a surface's own notes do ([control surfaces](control-surfaces.md) R5): every MIDI track whose input is All Ins or Push 2's Live port, monitored through its chain and recorded while it records, stamped with the time Push 2 sent them; with the selection arm of R3 that is the selected track. Releasing a pad ends its note, whatever the layout has become since. (S1, S3, S9)
- **R3.** While the surface runs, selecting a MIDI track, on Push 2 or in the app, arms it by selection, and disarms the track it had armed by selection before. A track armed by selection shows its Arm switch in a colour of its own, the pink of the theme, unlike a manual arm's (the colour is Beatlayer's own); the arm isn't an undo step and isn't saved. When the surface stops, its selection arm is released. (S3)
- **R4.** Holding a lower display button, or pressing it while Record is held, arms that track by hand, as its Arm switch does ([recording](recording.md)), or disarms it if it was; a manual arm stays when another track is selected. On the display, a manually armed track's label carries an arm icon (Beatlayer's own). (S3, S6)
- **R5.** The surface keeps, for each track, its drum or melodic layout, whether the loop length pads are locked, the step size, and Repeat's state, while the surface runs, as surface state ([control surfaces](control-surfaces.md) R12). Selecting a track brings back its own. (S1, S2, S3)

**Pad pressure and the touch strip (WP-7.22)**

- **R6.** Beatlayer sets Push 2's aftertouch to polyphonic key pressure and passes each held pad's pressure on as the per-note pressure of the note that pad plays ([MPE](mpe.md) R5), so native instruments and MIDI effects receive it per note, a plug-in with MPE Mode off receives polyphonic key pressure ([MPE](mpe.md) R15), and a drum chain receives it as [drum racks](drum-racks.md) R29 says. A recording records it as the notes' Pressure envelopes ([MPE](mpe.md) R12). (S7, S8, S9)
- **R7.** In a melodic layout that plays notes, the touch strip sends pitch bend into the selected track by default, Push 2 lighting it as [Push 2](push-2.md) R11's pitch bend; Select held while tapping the strip switches it to the modulation wheel (controller 1, R11's modulation) and back, and the display notifies the new use. Pitch bend reaches the track as a 14-bit bend of the strip's value, 8192 at the centre. In a drum layout the strip chooses the drum bank (R13), and in a melodic sequencer the note range (R41), each as R11's range. (S6, S8)

**Drum layouts (WP-7.21)**

- **R8.** In the Loop Selector layout, the lower-left 4 × 4 pads play the sixteen drum pads of the bank (R13), the lower-right 4 × 4 pads are the loop length pads (R26), and the upper four rows are 32 step pads (R22). (S1, S6)
- **R9.** Layout steps a drum track through Loop Selector, 16 Velocities and 64 Pads, and back; a drum rack track starts in Loop Selector and a slicing Simpler's in 64 Pads (R75). Holding Layout shows another layout while it's held: 16 Velocities from Loop Selector, and the loop length pads from 16 Velocities and 64 Pads, in the lower-right 4 × 4 pads; Shift and Layout keeps that view after Layout is released, and Layout alone then returns to the layout before, without stepping. (S1, S6)
- **R10.** In 16 Velocities, the lower-right 4 × 4 pads hold sixteen velocities, from round(127/16) at the bottom left to 127 at the top right, rising left to right and row by row (Beatlayer's own values). Tapping one plays the selected drum pad at that velocity, and new steps take that velocity until another is tapped; Accent overrides it (R54). The other pads are as in Loop Selector. (S1, S2)
- **R11.** In 64 Pads, the whole grid plays drums: the left four columns hold the first 32 notes from the bank's lowest note, four to a row from the bottom up, and the right four columns the next 32 the same way. Switching between 64 Pads and the other layouts leaves the bank where it was. (S1, S4)
- **R12.** A drum pad is lit in the track's colour when a chain receives its note, a lighter version of it when none does, green while it plays, white while it's the selected pad, blue while soloed and grey while muted. (S1)
- **R13.** The drum bank is the rack's pad view ([drum racks](drum-racks.md) R11): the sixteen notes the view shows are the sixteen pads, and moving either moves the other (`rack.move_pads`). Octave Up and Down move it sixteen notes, or one row of four with Shift held, and are off when it can't move further; the touch strip moves it to the position touched, by rows with Shift held, and its LEDs light the bank's place in the 128 notes. (S1, S6, S9)
- **R14.** Tapping a drum pad selects it, as `pad.select` does, plays it, and makes it the pad the steps edit; with Select held it selects without playing, as tapping one of the 16 Velocities pads with Select held does. (S1, S6)
- **R15.** With Mute held, a drum pad mutes or unmutes (`pad.set_mute`); with Solo held, it solos or unsolos (`pad.set_solo`). (S1, S6)
- **R16.** With Delete held, a drum pad deletes its notes in the clip the steps edit (R20); a pad with no notes there deletes its chains instead (`pad.delete`). One undo step each. (S1, S6)
- **R17.** With Duplicate held, tapping a drum pad and then another copies the first pad's chains onto the second, replacing the second's chains and leaving every clip's notes as they were (`pad.copy`, `edit`, one undo step). (S1, S6)
- **R18.** In Device mode with a drum rack, while a pad is selected the second upper display button shows that pad, with a square icon and its name; pressing it puts the pad in the rack's place, so the encoders control the pad: encoder 1 its Choke group (None, 1 to 16) and encoder 2 its transposition, the semitones its chains' Play note lies from the pad's own note ([drum racks](drum-racks.md) R1). Browse Mode then loads onto that pad, as a drop on it does ([drum racks](drum-racks.md) R14); the pad blinks there, and tapping another pad makes that one the target. The first upper button returns to the rack. (S1, S9)
- **R19.** Shift held while tapping a drum pad opens the colour picker for it ([Push 2](push-2.md) R27). A pad's colour is saved with the drum rack, in the set and its presets, is shown only on Push 2 in place of the track's colour, and is set by `pad.set_push_colour` (`edit`, one undo step). (S1)

**Step sequencing (WP-7.21; the melodic sequencers in WP-7.22)**

- **R20.** The steps edit the clip in the selected track's slot of the selected scene. With that slot empty, the first step tapped creates a MIDI clip there one bar long (Beatlayer's own length) and launches it, in the same undo step as the note. (S1, S9)
- **R21.** The scene/grid buttons set the step size, from 1/32 triplet at the top to a quarter note at the bottom, 1/16 for a new track; the chosen one is lit. The same choice is Repeat's rate (R55). With a triplet size the right two columns of the step area are unlit and do nothing, leaving six steps to a row. (S1, S2, S6)
- **R22.** In a drum layout, tapping a step with no note of the selected pad adds one at the step's start, one step long, with the velocity R10 or R54 gives, or 100 (Beatlayer's own); tapping a step that has one deletes it. With Mute held, a step deactivates its note or activates it again (`note.deactivate`). One undo step each, running the note editor's commands. (S1, S6, S9)
- **R23.** A drum step is grey without a note, in the clip's colour with a note (brighter for a higher velocity), and in a lighter version of it with a deactivated note. The step playing is green, red while the track records. (S1)
- **R24.** Delete pressed alone in Note Mode deletes the clip the steps edit (`clip.delete`). (S1, S6)
- **R25.** Holding one or more steps and turning encoders changes those steps' note parameters in Clip mode ([tracks on Push 2](push-2-tracks.md)) and records automation for them in Device and Mix mode (R66). (S5)
- **R26.** Each loop length pad is one page of the clip from its start, the first pad the first page. A page is 16 steps in a drum layout, so the 32 step pads show two pages at once, always an odd page and the next; 8 steps in the Melodic Sequencer; and 32 steps in the Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes (the pairing and the last page's size are Beatlayer's own). (S3)
- **R27.** Holding a loop length pad and tapping another sets the clip's loop to run from the first page's start to the last page's end; a double tap within 300 ms sets it to that one page; a tap on a page outside the loop sets the loop to that page. Each turns the loop on and is one undo step (`clip.set_loop`). (S3)
- **R28.** The step area follows the page that plays. A single tap on a page in the loop shows that page and stops following, without changing the loop; Page Left and Right show the page before or after and stop following too. Holding Page Left or Right, or setting the loop again to the same pages, starts following again. (S3)
- **R29.** A loop length pad is unlit outside the loop, grey inside it, white while its page is in the step area, green while its page plays and red while it records. (S3)
- **R30.** With Duplicate held, tapping a loop length pad and then another copies the first page's notes onto the second, adding them to its notes; with Delete held, tapping one deletes that page's notes. One undo step each. (S3)
- **R31.** The loop length pads of the Loop Selector layout use Push 2's reduced pad sensitivity (Select Pad Settings 1), which the surface sets when the layout appears and returns to regular when it goes (Beatlayer's own). (S8)

**Melodic layouts and scales (WP-7.22)**

- **R32.** In the 64 Notes layout each pad plays one note. In Key, a pad's note is a degree of the current scale ([scales](scales.md) R2): moving right is one degree up, and moving up a row is 3 degrees with the 4ths layout, 2 with 3rds and 8 with Sequent. Chromatic, moving right is a semitone, and up a row 5 semitones with 4ths, 4 with 3rds and 8 with Sequent (the Chromatic thirds are Beatlayer's own). With Direction Horizontal, rows and columns swap, so moving right is the 4th and moving up the next note. A new set's layout is In Key, 4ths, Vertical, Fixed off (Fixed off is Beatlayer's own). (S3, S6)
- **R33.** With Fixed off, the bottom-left pad plays the scale's root in octave 1 (C1 is note 36); with Fixed on, it plays C1, or in a key without C the scale note nearest C1 (the higher on a tie). Octave Up and Down move every pad an octave and are off when the bottom-left pad can't move an octave further within notes 0 to 127; a pad whose note falls outside them is unlit and silent. (S3, S6)
- **R34.** A melodic pad is in the track's colour when it plays the scale's root, white when it plays another note of the scale, unlit when Chromatic puts it outside the scale, green while its note plays, with every pad that plays the same note, and red instead of green while the track records. (S3)
- **R35.** Scale, in Note Mode with a melodic layout, opens the Scale menu: the upper display buttons 2 to 7 choose the keys C, G, D, A, E and B, and the lower buttons 2 to 7 F, B♭, E♭, A♭, D♭ and G♭ (Beatlayer's own places), the chosen key white and the others grey; any of encoders 2 to 7 moves through the scale list ([scales](scales.md) R1) shown across those columns, the chosen scale highlighted; encoder 1 chooses Layout (4ths, 3rds, Sequent) and encoder 8 Direction (Vertical, Horizontal); lower button 1 switches In Key and Chromatic, and lower button 8 Fixed. (S3, S6)
- **R36.** The key and the scale are the set's current scale ([scales](scales.md) R9): choosing them in the Scale menu sets them as the Control Bar's choosers do ([scales](scales.md) R12), leaving Scale Mode as it is, and the pads follow the current scale whenever it changes, by selection, a launch or an edit ([scales](scales.md) R10, R11). (S3, S9)
- **R37.** In Key or Chromatic, Fixed, Layout and Direction are saved with the set, as settings of its own that aren't undo steps (Beatlayer's own), and a new set takes them from the set it's made from. (S3)
- **R38.** With Delete held in 64 Notes, tapping a pad deletes every note of its pitch inside the loop of the clip the steps would edit (R20), as one undo step. (S3)

**The melodic sequencers (WP-7.22)**

- **R39.** On a melodic track Layout steps through 64 Notes, the Melodic Sequencer and the Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes, and back. Holding Layout shows the loop length pads while it's held, in the top row in 64 Notes and the Melodic Sequencer and in the fifth row in the Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes; Shift and Layout keeps them there, and Layout alone then hides them without stepping. (S3, S6)
- **R40.** In the Melodic Sequencer, each column is a step of the page and each row a pitch: In Key, the scale's notes one degree apart from the bottom row up; Chromatic, semitones, with the rows outside the scale unlit but usable. The bottom row is the scale's root for a new track, and the rows holding the root are white. While the loop length pads show in the top row, seven rows hold pitches. (S3)
- **R41.** Octave Up and Down move the Melodic Sequencer's rows an octave, or one scale note with Shift held; the touch strip moves them to the position touched, by octaves with Shift held. The display notifies the range, from the lowest row's note to the highest's. The strip's LEDs light the range shown brightly, and dimly where the clip has notes outside it. (S3)
- **R42.** In the Melodic Sequencer, tapping an empty step adds the row's note, one step long, at velocity 100, or 127 with Accent (R54); tapping a step with a note deletes it; with Mute held, it deactivates it or activates it again. Steps take R23's colours. (S3, S6)
- **R43.** In the Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes, the lower four rows hold 32 notes, eight to a row from the bottom left: In Key, consecutive notes of the scale from its root in the range's octave; Chromatic, consecutive semitones (Beatlayer's own start). Tapping one plays it and makes it the selected note; pads held together are selected together; with Select held, tapping selects without playing. The root is in the track's colour, other scale notes white, selected notes a lighter version of the track's colour, and a playing note green. Octave Up and Down move them an octave, or one scale note with Shift held, and Shift with the touch strip moves them by octaves. (S3)
- **R44.** In the Melodic Sequencer + 32 Notes, the upper four rows are 32 steps of one page. Tapping a step adds every selected note to it; holding a step shows its notes among the 32 in the lighter colour, and tapping one of them removes it from the step; pressing several steps together adds the selected notes to each. With Duplicate held, tapping a step and then another copies the first's notes to the second; with Delete held, tapping a step deletes its notes (Beatlayer's own). A step is in the clip's colour with notes, grey without, light grey with deactivated notes, white while held and green while it plays. (S3)

**Navigating in Note Mode (WP-7.22)**

- **R45.** Left and Right select the track before or after the selected one, and the surface moves its ring a track at a time to keep the selected track among its eight ([control surfaces](control-surfaces.md) R29); the lower display buttons select tracks as [Push 2](push-2.md) R26 says. (S3)
- **R46.** Up and Down select the scene above or below and launch at once, each launched clip taking over the play position of the clip before it on its track, as a Legato launch does ([launch quantisation](launch-quantisation.md)): with Workflow on Scene, the new scene launches; on Clip, only the selected track's slot in it, leaving other tracks playing. An empty slot launched with Clip launches nothing (Beatlayer's own). Up is off at the first scene and Down at the last. (S3, S5)
- **R47.** A melodic layout's pads, steps and Scale menu, and a drum layout's, show and act on the track R1 picks for the selected track at every change of selection, of its devices, or of the scale. (S3)

**Recording (WP-7.23)**

- **R48.** With the Session View in focus, Record starts Session Record ([session recording](session-recording.md) R1); pressed while that recording runs, it ends the recording and the clip plays on ([session recording](session-recording.md) R4); pressed while the selected track's clip plays without recording, it starts overdubbing that clip, and pressed again it stops overdubbing. Record is red while recording or overdubbing. (S2, S6)
- **R49.** With the Arrangement View in focus, Record switches Arrangement Record on and off ([recording](recording.md)), and Shift with Record does what R48 says; with the Session View in focus, Shift with Record switches Arrangement Record. While Arrangement Record runs, what Push 2 plays and the automation it records go into the Arrangement. (S2)
- **R50.** Fixed Length switches fixed-length recording on and off. Holding it shows its menu, where the lower display buttons choose the length, 1 beat, 2 beats, 1 bar, 2, 4, 8, 16 or 32 bars, and the first upper button Phrase Sync (the lengths and the places are Beatlayer's own); a new set starts with Fixed Length off at 1 bar and Phrase Sync off. The settings are preferences. (S2, S6)
- **R51.** A Session recording started with Fixed Length on creates an empty clip of the length and records into it from its start on the launch grid; with Phrase Sync on, it starts at the place in the clip that the song position has in a phrase of that length, counted from the song's start (at bar 7 with 4 bars, bar 3 of the clip). The recording ends when it reaches the clip's end, and the clip plays on. Without Fixed Length a recording goes on until Record, New or Play/Stop ends it. Switching Fixed Length on while a recording runs ends the recording and loops the clip's last stretch of the length, up to where the recording ended. (S2, S6)
- **R52.** New stops the selected track's clip and selects an empty slot on it to record into. With Workflow on Scene it inserts a scene after the selected one holding copies of the clips playing on the other tracks, which carry on from the new scene without a break, as Capture and Insert Scene does, and selects the selected track's empty slot in it; with Workflow on Clip it selects the next empty slot below on the selected track, inserting a scene if there is none (Beatlayer's own), and leaves the other tracks alone. One undo step. (S2, S5)
- **R53.** Duplicate pressed alone, with Workflow on Scene, runs Capture and Insert Scene and plays the new scene without a break. With Workflow on Clip it copies the selected clip into the slot below, inserting a scene if that slot holds a clip (Beatlayer's own), and launches the copy as a Legato launch, leaving the other tracks playing; Shift with Duplicate captures a scene as Scene does. One undo step. (S2, S5)
- **R54.** Accent, while on, gives every note the pads play and every step added velocity 127, over R10's velocities. A tap switches it on or off; held longer than a tap, it's on until released ([Push 2](push-2.md) R7). (S2, S6)
- **R55.** Repeat, while on, replays a held pad's note at the step size (R21), on the song's grid of that size while the transport runs and from the press while it's stopped (Beatlayer's own), each note one step long, its velocity the pad's pressure scaled to 1 to 127, or 127 with Accent (the scaling is Beatlayer's own). A tap switches it on or off, and a hold turns it on until released. The swing amount (R56) delays every second note of the size by that share of half a step (Beatlayer's own law). (S2, S6)
- **R56.** The swing amount is one value from 0 to 100 %, 0 in a new install, set by the swing encoder ([Push 2](push-2.md) R10) and by Quantize's menu (R58), kept as a preference; Quantize and Repeat use it, Record Quantize doesn't. (S2)
- **R57.** Tapping Quantize quantises the selected notes of the clip the steps edit, or all its notes when none is selected, as `note.quantize` does with Quantize To and Quantize Amount (R58) and the swing amount, one undo step; with an audio clip it quantises its transients ([MIDI transformations](midi-transformations.md)' audio Quantize). With Quantize held, tapping a drum pad quantises only that pad's notes. (S2, S6)
- **R58.** Holding Quantize shows its menu: Swing Amount on encoder 1, Quantize To on encoder 2, Quantize Amount on encoder 3 (those places Beatlayer's own), Record Quantize switched by upper display button 5 and its value on encoder 5. Record Quantize is the Edit menu's Record Quantization (M-EDIT-038), the same setting. If a value changed while it was held, the menu stays after Quantize is released, and the next tap of Quantize closes it without quantising. (S2, S6)
- **R59.** The count-in's bar and the playing clips' progress show on the display ([Push 2](push-2.md) R19). (S2)
- **R60.** Notes the pads play into a recording track are recorded as a port's notes are, with their pressure (R6), in the recording's own undo step. (S2, S9)

**Automation on Push 2 (WP-7.23)**

- **R61.** Automate switches Automation Arm (`arrangement.automation_arm`), lit red while it's on. While it's on and a recording runs, turning an encoder records the parameter as moving the control does ([session automation](session-automation.md) R1 to R4 in the Session, [automation](automation.md) R17 in the Arrangement), the encoder's touch beginning and ending the touch. (S5, S6)
- **R62.** With Delete held, touching an encoder deletes its parameter's automation: in the clip the steps edit, its Automation envelope for the parameter ([session automation](session-automation.md) R10); with the Arrangement in focus, the parameter's envelope in the Arrangement ([automation](automation.md) R6). A parameter with no such automation goes back to its default value instead. One undo step. (S5, S6)
- **R63.** Shift with Automate re-enables every overridden automation (`automation.re_enable`). Delete with Automate deletes every Automation envelope of the clip the steps edit, as one undo step. (S5, S6)
- **R64.** A parameter column's white dot ([Push 2](push-2.md) R20) shows while the parameter has an enabled Automation envelope in the clip playing on its track, or an Arrangement envelope that drives it while the Arrangement plays; the grey dot shows while either is overridden ([session automation](session-automation.md) R9, [automation](automation.md) R5). (S5, S9)
- **R65.** Moves recorded from Push 2 go into the clips that Record Session automation in names, the armed tracks' or every track's ([session automation](session-automation.md) R5), as moves from any control do. (S5, S9)
- **R66.** In Device or Mix mode, holding one or more steps and turning an encoder writes the parameter's value into the Automation envelope of the clip the steps edit for each held step's time, steps with or without notes alike: the envelope takes the value from the step's start to its end and keeps its earlier values on either side, with breakpoints at the step's edges (Beatlayer's own shape). While steps are held, the column shows the value at the first held step. One undo step for each touch. (S5)

**Simpler on Push 2 (WP-7.24)**

- **R67.** In Device mode, a Simpler with no sample fills the content band with a message to press Browse to load one (the wording is Beatlayer's own). With a sample, its main bank shows the waveform across the content band, the region between Start and End marked and the rest dimmed, and the parameters of R68 on the encoders, labelled in the upper band of their columns over the waveform. (S4)
- **R68.** The main bank's encoders, by mode, are: Classic, Zoom, Mode, Start, End, S Start, S Length, S Loop Length and Gain; One-Shot, Zoom, Mode, Start, End, Fade In, Fade Out, Transpose and Gain; Slice, Zoom, Mode, Start, End, Slice By, the Slice By choice's own value (Sensitivity, Division or Regions), Playback and Nudge (the order is Beatlayer's own). Start and End are the region's edges in the sample; S Start and S Length place the playback inside the region as shares of it (50 % and 25 % play its third quarter); S Loop Length is the share of that the loop repeats, active while Loop is on ([the sampler](sampler.md) R3). (S4, S9)
- **R69.** Zoom zooms the waveform in as it turns clockwise, around the position control last touched (Start, End, S Start or S Length; with slices, the selected slice), and changes only the display. (S4)
- **R70.** Pressing Simpler's upper display button again opens its Edit Mode ([tracks on Push 2](push-2-tracks.md)), where the upper buttons switch and run: in Classic, Loop On/Off, Warp as…, ÷2, ×2, Crop and Reverse; in One-Shot, Trigger/Gate, Warp as…, ÷2, ×2, Crop and Reverse; in Slice, Trigger/Gate, Pad Slicing (with Slice By Manual), Split Slice, Warp as…, ÷2, ×2 and Reverse. Warp as… sets the warping so the region plays in the number of bars shown, Beatlayer's guess from the sample's length at the set's tempo, the nearest power of two bars (Beatlayer's own guess); ÷2 and ×2 halve and double it (`device.scale_tempo`). Crop (`device.crop_sample`) and Reverse (`device.reverse_sample`) work on a copy of the sample, leaving the file as it was. The lower buttons choose Simpler's other banks. (S4, S9)
- **R71.** In One-Shot, Trigger plays the region to its end however long the pad is held, and Gate fades out from the pad's release over Fade Out ([the sampler](sampler.md) R10); Transpose moves the sample up to 48 semitones either way. Shift with Play/Stop silences the selected track's Simpler at once, as well as doing what [Push 2](push-2.md) R12 says. (S4)
- **R72.** Edit Mode's second lower display button shows the Global bank, where Glide Mode and Voices are; with Glide Mode on Glide and one voice (Voices shows in Classic only), notes played legato move the sample's pitch without restarting it ([the sampler](sampler.md) R9). (S4, S9)
- **R73.** In Slice mode, Slice By chooses Transient (its Sensitivity making up to 64 slices), Beat (Division), Region (Regions) or Manual ([the sampler](sampler.md) R13, R14). Playback chooses Mono (one slice at a time), Poly (several together) or Through (one at a time, playing on past the slice's end to the region's end). Nudge moves the selected slice's start, finer with Shift held (`device.move_slice`); Split Slice adds a slice halfway through the selected one (`device.add_slice`); with Delete held, tapping a pad deletes its slice (`device.delete_slice`). Tapping a pad selects its slice. Each is one undo step. (S4, S9)
- **R74.** With Slice By on Manual and Pad Slicing on, tapping a pad that has a slice plays from it, and tapping an empty pad while the sample plays adds a slice at the place playing, with the slices after it moving one pad on. Pad Slicing switched off ends it. (S4)
- **R75.** A Simpler in Classic or One-Shot mode makes the track melodic (R1). In Slice mode the track takes the drum layouts, 64 Pads first, and the slices fill the pads in groups of four from the bottom-left pad: the left half row by row upward, then the right half the same way, up to 64; each pad plays its slice's note. (S4)
- **R76.** Convert acts on what is selected: with Simpler in Classic or One-Shot, it makes a new MIDI track after the selected one with a drum rack holding a copy of the Simpler on its pad for note 36, and copies of the track's other devices in their places; with Simpler in Slice mode, it replaces the Simpler with a drum rack whose pads hold its slices, as Slice to Drum Rack does ([slice to MIDI](slice-to-midi.md)); with a drum rack, it makes a new MIDI track holding copies of the selected pad's devices; with an audio clip selected, the display offers a new MIDI track with a Simpler holding the clip's sample, or with a drum rack holding it in a Simpler on its first pad, both keeping the clip's warp markers and settings and copies of the track's devices, and lists Convert Harmony, Melody and Drums to New MIDI Track as disabled until they're built. Each is one undo step. (S4, S6)
