## The behaviour

Auto Filter is Beatlayer's Auto Filter, as Live 12.2 redesigned it. It filters its input with one of ten types: low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch and notch plus low-pass at 12 or 24 dB per octave; Morph, which moves between low-pass, band-pass and high-pass, at 6, 12, 24 or 48 dB; DJ, whose one bipolar control sweeps a low-pass into a high-pass; Comb; Resampling; and Vowel. Most types have a cutoff and a resonance, and each of the others has its own control in their place. Four circuits (SVF, DFM, MS2 and PRD) give the first five types their character at high levels, and Drive saturates the signal before the filter.

An LFO and an envelope follower move the cutoff, each by its own amount. The LFO has eight waves, a rate in Hz, in seconds, in beat divisions or in sixteenth notes, Phase or Spin to differ between the channels, and a quantiser that holds it in steps or on a beat-time interval; the envelope follower has an attack, a release, a Hold that finishes the attack, and an optional beat-time hold. The follower listens to the device's own input or, with the sidechain's External on, to another track, through the device's own sidechain filter; Mono Sidechain makes it hear one mono signal, so both channels move alike. Clip soft-clips the output, Output sets the filtered signal's level and Dry/Wet balances it against the dry signal. Mono Sidechain and Hi-Quality, which runs the filter at twice the sample rate, are items of the device's menu.

The display shows the filter's curve and, as the engine runs, the curves of the left and right channels at their modulated cutoffs. This spec covers the device only; where it sits in a chain and the frame around it are [the device chains spec](device-chains.md) and [the device framework spec](device-framework.md), and the sidechain section is [routing](routing.md)'s.

## Rules

- **R1.** Beatlayer ships Auto Filter, an audio-effect device type with a sidechain input, of its own layout: every parameter has a descriptor and a place in the layout, except Mono Sidechain and Hi-Quality, which are menu options (R12) and have none. The layout is the filter's controls, the display cell of its curves, the LFO's controls, the envelope's, and the sidechain filter's with Clip, Output and Dry/Wet. (S6, S7, S10)
- **R2.** Type is one of Low-pass, High-pass, Band-pass, Notch, Notch + LP, Morph, DJ, Comb, Resampling and Vowel. Slope is 6, 12, 24 or 48 dB per octave; low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch and Notch + LP offer 12 and 24 dB (6 plays as 12, 48 as 24), Morph all four, and DJ, Comb, Resampling and Vowel none (DJ is 12 dB). A slope of 12 dB is one two-pole section, 24 dB two in cascade, 48 dB four, and Morph's 6 dB a single pole. (S1, S5)
- **R3.** A section is a two-pole state-variable filter at the cutoff with a quality factor Q; with `k = 1/Q` and `s = j·tan(πf/fs)/tan(πfc/fs)` its low-pass is `1/(1 + k·s + s²)`, its high-pass `s²/(1 + k·s + s²)`, its band-pass `k·s/(1 + k·s + s²)` (0 dB at the cutoff) and its notch `(1 + s²)/(1 + k·s + s²)`. Res from 0 to 100 % sets the first section's Q exponentially from 0.7071 to 10; later sections have Q 0.7071. The type's response is the product of its sections', and the device's small-signal response (Drive 0 %, −40 dBFS) matches that curve within 0.5 dB to two octaves either side of the cutoff, at any cutoff and resonance, and falls at 6, 12, 24 or 48 dB per octave within 1 dB. `response_db` and the display give the same curve. (S1, S10)
- **R4.** The types with a control of their own. **Notch + LP:** a notch of Q 2 at the cutoff times `2^(3 × (1 − Morph))`, so at Morph 0 % it is three octaves above the cutoff and at 100 % at it, followed by the low-pass sections. **Morph:** low-pass at 0 %, band-pass at 50 %, high-pass at 100 %, crossfading between each pair, in every section of the cascade; at 6 dB a crossfade of a one-pole low-pass and high-pass. **DJ:** with Control `c` from −100 to 100 %, above zero a high-pass whose cutoff is 20 Hz × 1,000^c, below zero a low-pass whose cutoff is 20 kHz × 1,000^(−|c|), at zero both out of the audible band; Freq is not used, and Res counts for `|c|` of its Q's rise above 0.7071. **Comb:** a delay of one cycle of the cutoff with feedback `0.95 × Res`, so its peaks fall on multiples of the cutoff, crossfaded by Morph with a feedforward comb of the same delay whose notches fall there; 0 % is the peaks, 100 % the notches. **Resampling:** the input is held for as long as one cycle of the cutoff, so what is above half the cutoff aliases; Res is not used. **Vowel:** three bandpass formants, each at the frequency and level of the vowel table below at Formant 0 % (a), 25 % (e), 50 % (i), 75 % (o) and 100 % (u), moved between two vowels by interpolating their frequencies logarithmically, and by Pitch semitones; Morph evens their levels out; Res raises their Q from 8 to 16; Freq is not used. The table, as frequency in Hz and relative level, is: a 730 (1), 1,090 (0.45), 2,440 (0.2); e 530, 1,840, 2,480; i 270, 2,290, 3,010; o 570, 840, 2,410; u 300, 870, 2,240, the levels as a's. (S1, S10)
- **R5.** Circuit is SVF, DFM, MS2 or PRD, and acts on low-pass, high-pass, band-pass, notch and Morph; the other types run as SVF. The circuits differ in where they soften a section's integrators, `a·tanh(x/a)`: SVF nowhere; DFM both, with `a` = 8; MS2 the band-pass integrator, with `a` = 3, so its resonance is bounded; PRD the low-pass integrator, with `a` = 8, its resonance not bounded. At −40 dBFS all four play R3's curve; at high levels with a high resonance they part. (S3, S5, S10)
- **R6.** Drive from 0 to 100 % saturates the signal before the filter, in every type: `tanh(d·x)/d` with `d` = 10 × Drive, which is the signal itself at 0 %, so the harmonic distortion of a sine is none at 0 % and rises with Drive. (S3, S10)
- **R7.** The LFO moves each channel's cutoff by LFO Amt × 4 octaves × its value, so at 100 % a cutoff is moved up to four octaves either way. Its waves are Sine, Triangle, Saw (the effects LFO's Shark Tooth), Square (Rectangle), Ramp Up (Saw Up), Ramp Down (Saw Down), Wander and S&H (Sample & Hold), as device framework R26 has them. Its rate is in Hz, in seconds per cycle, in a beat division (1/32 to 8 bars, with dotted and triplet divisions) or in sixteenth notes per cycle (1 to 64), the last two synced as framework R27 has it, with Offset in degrees against the beat grid; Phase puts the right channel's LFO a number of degrees ahead, Spin makes its rate a percentage faster, and Spin is Phase 0° with Wander and S&H. With a wave other than S&H, LFO Morph from 0 to 100 % is the wave's duty cycle; with S&H it is Smooth, blending the held steps toward the eased random wave. Quantize Steps holds the value an LFO has at the start of each of N equal parts of its cycle (2 to 64); S&H holds it for each beat-time interval (1/32 to 8 bars) at the tempo of the stretch, from the first frame. Random waves follow the device's seed (framework R29). (S2, S8, S10)
- **R8.** The envelope follower follows the level of the detector (R9) with Attack and Release as time constants and Hold as framework R28 has them. A level of 1 (full scale) with Env Amt 100 % moves each channel's cutoff up eight octaves, in proportion to the level below it; a negative amount moves it down. Env S&H holds the modulation for each beat-time interval, as the LFO's S&H does. (S2, S8, S10)
- **R9.** With External on in the sidechain section, the follower's detector hears what the engine hands the device (routing R24), and otherwise the device's own input; the sidechain changes no audible level. SC Filter, when on, filters the detector's signal first: Low Shelf and High Shelf by SC Shelf Gain (−24 to 24 dB), Peak by SC Peak Gain, and Low Pass, Band Pass (0 dB at the centre), High Pass and Notch, at SC Freq and SC Q (0.1 to 10), as the cookbook of a second-order section has them, so only the band it passes moves the cutoff. With Mono Sidechain on (the default of a new device) the detector hears the mean of the two channels and one follower serves both, so the envelope moves both channels alike; with it off each channel has its own detector. (S4, S5, S8)
- **R10.** The filtered signal passes through Output (a gain reaching −∞ dB, rule G1) and then, with Clip on, through a soft clipper that leaves what is within 0.8 of full scale as it is and rounds off what is beyond to a peak of 1; the Clip light is lit for a block in which the signal went past 0.8. Dry/Wet from 0 to 100 % balances the dry signal against the result; at 0 % the device passes the dry signal as it came. (S3, S7, S10)
- **R11.** With Hi-Quality on, the filter, Drive included, runs at twice the sample rate through the framework's oversampler, and the device reports that trip's latency; the dry signal is delayed by as much so that Dry/Wet mixes aligned signals. With it off the device has no latency. A change reaches the latency with the next block. (S8, S10)
- **R12.** Mono Sidechain (on in a new device) and Hi-Quality are switches the device's type marks as menu options: its menu lists them after Save as Default Preset, in that order, with Mono Sidechain ticked in a new device, and choosing one is one undo step. They are automatable like any parameter. The device has no expand toggle. (S4, S6, S7)
- **R13.** After each block the device publishes three values through the framework's display data (device framework R35): the left and the right channel's cutoff in Hz after the LFO and the envelope, and 1 if Clip clipped since the last block. Its display (device framework R36) holds the Filter Curve, the response from 20 Hz to 20 kHz in 96 points from the parameters, and with an engine publishing the Left Curve and Right Curve at the published cutoffs, a marker at each cutoff, and the readings Left Cutoff, Right Cutoff and Clip. The cell is named Filter. (S1, S5)
- **R14.** Every block runs without allocating, locking or blocking, and a render is bit-identical at block sizes 1, 64, 512 and 4,096 and run after run, including with random waves and S&H. (S8, S9)
- **R15.** Every parameter can be inserted, set by ID and read back through the agent API (`device.insert`, `device.set_param`, `device.parameters`), is automatable and modulatable (rule G2), and the gain (Output) reaches −∞ dB (rule G1). (S7)
- **R16.** A set with the device saves and reopens with every parameter. (S7)
