# Freeze and bounce: Freeze Track, Bounce Track in Place, Bounce to New Track, Bounce Group, and Paste Bounced Audio

- **Status:** draft
- **Work packages:** WP-4.14 (Freeze Track and unfreezing, with group and return tracks, IMP-19: R1 to R17, and R27 to R29 for freezing), WP-4.15 (the bounces and Paste Bounced Audio, with mono bounces, IMP-10 and rule G3: R18 to R26, and R27 to R29 for bouncing)
- **Parity-matrix row:** Audio clips and warping: Freeze Track; Bounce Track in Place, Bounce to New Track, Bounce Group, Paste Bounced Audio
- **Checked against:** the Live 12 reference manual (online edition), read 17 October 2026, and the UI parity reference's observations of Live 12.4.3 on macOS (10 October 2026)

## The behaviour

**Freezing** a track renders it to audio so it costs almost nothing to play: each of its clips plays a rendered file instead of running its devices, while its mixer stays live. A frozen track's devices and clip settings are locked until it's unfrozen; its clips can still be launched, moved, copied, trimmed, and consolidated, and its volume, pan, sends, and mixer automation still edited. In the Arrangement the render runs on past each clip's end, so effect tails go on playing after their clips, drawn crosshatched. Live freezes only audio and MIDI tracks that hold clips; Beatlayer also freezes group and return tracks (IMP-19).

**Bouncing** commits audio for good. Bounce Track in Place replaces a track with a new audio track holding its rendered clips and keeping its mixer settings; Bounce to New Track (Cmd-B) renders the selected clips or time into a new audio track and switches the source material off; a group's bounces render the group after its mixer, with the returns its tracks feed; and Paste Bounced Audio (Cmd-Option-V) pastes copied material as audio rendered at the moment of pasting. A track's render is taken after its devices and before its mixer, as a freeze's is. Every bounce keeps its source's channel count, so a mono source bounces to a mono file (rule G3, IMP-10).

This spec covers what each command renders, what it does to the tracks and clips, the files it writes, what a frozen track allows and refuses, and the commands. It leaves to other specs: the render loop and plug-ins in offline renders ([export](export.md), [offline plug-in renders](offline-plugin-renders.md)); where processed files go and how a set never saved keeps them ([audio clip editing](audio-clip-editing.md) R1 to R6); warped playback and Beats mode ([warping](warping.md), [warp modes](warp-modes.md); WP-4.2, WP-4.4), through which frozen and bounced clips play; and freezing tracks with external hardware, which renders in real time (WP-5.35).

## Rules

**Freezing (WP-4.14)**

- **R1.** Freeze Track (Edit ▸ Freeze Track, M-EDIT-028; the menus' rows C-TRACK-007, C-SLOT-001, C-CLIP-SES-010, C-CLIP-ARR-013, C-TIME-008; Cmd-Option-Shift-F, S-TRACK-017) freezes the selected tracks. An audio or MIDI track freezes when it holds at least one clip, in either view; a group or return track freezes as R14 and R15 say; Main and a track without clips are refused. When every selected track is frozen, the item reads Unfreeze Track and the key unfreezes them. (S1, S6, S7)
- **R2.** Freezing renders the track offline, off the audio thread, through the engine code that plays it ([export](export.md)'s render, with plug-ins lent as [offline plug-in renders](offline-plugin-renders.md) say), taken Post FX: its clips with their clip settings and the clip envelopes on its devices, through its devices and their automation, before its mixer. Volume, pan, sends, the Track Activator, mixer automation, and mixer clip envelopes aren't in the render; they act on it afterwards. (S1, S2, S9)
- **R3.** The Arrangement freezes as one render that plays the track from 1.1.1, as Play from the start does, and the file holds it from the track's first clip's start to the end of its last tail. A tail is the sound that goes on after a clip's end where no clip is: it lasts until the render has stayed below −120 dBFS for 100 ms, the next clip starts, or 60 s have passed, Beatlayer's own bounds. Each clip plays its own span of the render, and each tail plays after its clip as a tail region, drawn crosshatched; tail regions aren't clips of the set, and they go when the track is unfrozen. (S1, S2)
- **R4.** Each Session clip freezes as a render of its own: the clip launched alone, with every device parameter held at its value at the Arrangement's 1.1.1 (S2). An unlooped clip renders from its Start marker to its End marker; a looped one from its Start marker to the end of its second time round its loop brace, so a longer tail is cut. A frozen looped clip plays its file and repeats its two cycles; an unlooped one plays it once. Beatlayer's own: repeating the two cycles. (S1, S2)
- **R5.** Freeze files go into `Samples/Processed/Freeze/` (or the Temporary Folder for a set never saved), one for the Arrangement render and one for each Session clip, named `<track label> Freeze [<stamp>].wav`, with the media table, naming, and Temporary Folder rules of audio clip editing R2 to R6: 32-bit floating-point WAVs at the engine's rate. A file has one channel when its two channels are equal at every frame, and two otherwise (rule G3). (S1, S2, S7, S10)
- **R6.** A frozen track runs none of its devices: the engine plays its frozen files through its clip player, then its mixer, exactly as it mixes the unfrozen track. Frozen files play warped, in Beats mode, with warp markers that keep every frame on the beat it was rendered at under the tempo map, so at that tempo a frozen track gives the frames the unfrozen track gave, to the bit, and after a tempo change its audio follows the beats. A device whose plug-in is missing doesn't stop a frozen track playing, and random device parameters play the values they had in the render, every time. (S1, S2; [warp modes](warp-modes.md) R4, a warped clip at speed 1 playing its file's frames unchanged)
- **R7.** On a frozen track these still work, as in Live: launching its Session clips, with their launch settings; volume, pan, sends, the Track Activator, solo, and its output routing (Beatlayer's own for the routing); drawing and editing mixer automation and mixer clip envelopes; cutting, copying, pasting, duplicating, moving, deleting, and trimming clips, each clip keeping its own stretch of frozen audio and a tail moving with its clip only when it's selected too; Consolidate, whose new clip plays the frozen audio over its span; recording Session launches into the Arrangement, the recorded clips playing their Session clips' frozen audio; making, moving, and duplicating scenes. A frozen clip's edges can move inwards and back, but not past the frozen audio it was given (Beatlayer's own). (S1)
- **R8.** On a frozen track these are refused, with a message that the track is frozen: any change to its devices (parameters, adding, deleting, moving, switching off, presets, sidechains, device automation); any change to a clip's own settings (notes, markers, loop, warp, gain, pitch, Reverse, Crop, clip envelopes on devices); and new clips, by drawing, dropping files, pasting a clip that isn't frozen, or recording, so the track can't be armed. Beatlayer's own: new clips and arming. The Device View and Clip View show the track's devices and clips with their controls disabled. (S1)
- **R9.** Dragging a frozen MIDI clip onto an audio track makes an audio clip there that plays the clip's frozen audio, the file it's in staying in the project. Dragging a frozen clip, or the selected part of an Arrangement clip, to the drop area makes a new frozen track: a copy of the track, devices and mixer included, holding only that clip or part, frozen. (S1)
- **R10.** Unfreeze Track plays the track through its devices again, from its document as it now is, so edits made while frozen (mixer settings, clips moved, trimmed, copied, or consolidated) are kept; tail regions go. The freeze files stay on disk, so undoing the unfreeze freezes the track again without rendering. Beatlayer's own: keeping the files. (S1)
- **R11.** A track with a device that can't render offline isn't frozen, and the refusal names the device: until offline plug-in renders R11 changes, a VST3 or Audio Unit plug-in; any device no factory makes a node for; and Beatlayer's equivalents of Live's External Instrument and External Audio Effect, which freeze in real time (WP-5.35). Freezing a track with a CLAP device stops the transport first, as an export does (offline plug-in renders R7). (S1, S8)
- **R12.** A freeze renders on a worker thread, with its progress in the Status Bar and a Cancel; the rest of the set stays editable while the track's devices and clips refuse edits as R8 says. It lands between blocks, as one undo step, once every file is complete; cancelling, or a failure, leaves the track unfrozen and no file behind. The same track freezes to the same files at any block size, run after run. (S1, S8)
- **R13.** `track.freeze` and `track.unfreeze` (one or more tracks) are registered commands in the `edit` class, each one undo step for all its tracks; undoing a freeze unfreezes and undoing an unfreeze freezes again, from the files. Both read back through the query tool's `track`, field `frozen`, which says whether the track is frozen and names its files and tail regions. A frozen track is saved with the set as frozen, with its files by content hash, its clips' stretches, and its tails, and reopens frozen without rendering. (S7; D17)

**Freezing group and return tracks (IMP-19, WP-4.14)**

- **R14.** Freeze Track on a group track, Beatlayer's own (IMP-19): the Arrangement renders as R3 renders a track's, the group's Post FX output, which is its tracks through their devices and mixers, summed, then through the group's devices, from its tracks' first clip to their last tail. For each return its tracks send to, the sum of those sends renders too, as a file that feeds that return while the group is frozen. A frozen group runs none of its own or its tracks' devices; R8 applies to every track inside it, and their mixers and sends are locked too, since they're in the render, while the group's own mixer stays live. Its tracks' Session clips can't be launched while it's frozen. Unfreezing the group unlocks its tracks; a track inside it frozen on its own before stays frozen. (S7)
- **R15.** Freeze Track on a return track, Beatlayer's own (IMP-19): its Post FX output renders over the whole Arrangement, from 1.1.1 to the end of the last clip in the set plus its tail (R3), with every send into it as the set plays them. A frozen return plays that file in time with the Arrangement, whatever reaches its input; sends into it keep their values and can still be edited, but aren't heard until it's unfrozen. Its devices are locked as R8 says, and its own mixer stays live. (S7)
- **R16.** A track inside a frozen group can't be frozen or unfrozen on its own. A group with a frozen track inside renders that track as it plays, from its frozen files. (S7)
- **R17.** Freeze Track on a group or a return is one undo step and reads back as R13 says; a set saves and reopens a frozen group or return as R13 says. (S7)

**Bouncing (WP-4.15)**

- **R18.** A track's bounce is taken where its freeze is (R2 to R4): Post FX, with the clip envelopes on its devices and its device automation in the audio, its mixer left out (S3). Arrangement clips each take their span of one render of the lane, and a clip's tail, the sound that goes on after its end where no clip is (R3's bounds), becomes part of its bounced clip, which grows to hold it, up to the next clip's start (Beatlayer's own). Session clips render as R4 renders them. A frozen track bounces its frozen audio without rendering again. (S1, S2, S3)
- **R19.** Bounced files go into `Samples/Processed/Bounce/` (or the Temporary Folder), named `<track label> Bounce [<stamp>].wav`, with audio clip editing R2 to R6's rules. Each bounced clip plays warped as a frozen clip does (R6), so at the tempo it was bounced at, the new track gives the frames the source gave through the same mixer settings, to the bit. (S3, S10)
- **R20.** A bounce's channels follow the bounce commands' `channels` argument: `auto`, the default, writes one channel when the render's two channels are equal at every frame and two otherwise (rule G3); `mono` writes one channel, the mean of the two; `stereo` writes two, a mono render's in both. The menu items and keys bounce with `auto`. (S7)
- **R21.** Bounce Track in Place (M-EDIT-029, C-TRACK-008, C-SLOT-002, C-CLIP-SES-011, C-CLIP-ARR-014, C-TIME-009) replaces each selected audio or MIDI track with a new audio track in its place, in both views: no devices; the track's name with " (Bounce)" after it and its colour; its volume, pan, sends, Track Activator, solo, output routing, and mixer automation. Each of its Arrangement clips becomes an audio clip at its place and each Session clip one in its slot, with its launch settings, named after it with " (Bounce)" after the name, or "(Bounce)" for an unnamed clip. A MIDI track with neither an instrument nor an audio effect makes no audio, and bouncing it is refused. (S3)
- **R22.** Bounce to New Track (Cmd-B, S-BOUNCE-001; M-EDIT-030, C-SLOT-003, C-CLIP-SES-012, C-CLIP-ARR-015, C-TIME-010) renders the selected clips, or the time selection on the selected tracks, into a new audio track for each source track with selected material, placed directly after its source track (Beatlayer's own), named and coloured as R21 names them and with its source's mixer settings. In the Arrangement the new clips sit where their sources were; from the Session View they sit in the same scenes' slots and the new track's Arrangement is empty. Then the source material is switched off: the selected clips' activators go off, and with a time selection, clips its edges cross are split there and the parts inside switched off. (S3)
- **R23.** On a group track, Bounce Track in Place reads Bounce Group in Place (the group's header menu, its slots' menus, its main lane's menu) and replaces the group and every track inside it with one new audio track where the group was. A group's bounce is taken after the group's mixer, from what reaches Main: the group's output, with the return tracks its tracks send to wherever their output reaches Main, without Main's devices and fader. Tracks inside the group routed elsewhere aren't in it. The new track takes the group's name with " (Bounce)" after it and its colour, and its mixer stays at rest (0 dB, centre, no sends), since the group's mixer is in the audio (Beatlayer's own). (S4)
- **R24.** Bounce Group to New Track (C-SLOT-009, C-TRACK-017, Cmd-B with a group's slots or main lane selected) renders the selected clips or time inside a group at R23's point into a new audio track placed after the group, and switches the source material off as R22 does. (S4)
- **R25.** Paste Bounced Audio (Cmd-Option-V, S-BOUNCE-002; M-EDIT-031, C-SLOT-004, C-CLIP-SES-013, C-CLIP-ARR-016, C-TIME-011) renders what Copy last put on the clipboard as it is at the moment of pasting, at R18's point for a track's material and R23's for a time selection on a group's main lane, and places the audio as Paste places clips (arrangement editing R11): in the Arrangement at the time selection's start or the insert marker, the first source track's audio on the selected track and the rest on the tracks after it, and in the Session View in the selected slot. A destination must be an audio track, an empty MIDI track, which becomes an audio track first, or a take lane. The audio replaces what it covers (arrangement editing R6). With nothing copied, or with the copied source deleted, the command is disabled. (S5)
- **R26.** `track.bounce_in_place` (tracks), `track.bounce_to_new_track` (tracks and a range, or clips, or the selection), and `track.paste_bounced_audio` (a destination track or slot, and a position) are registered commands, each with `channels` (R20) and each one undo step. `track.bounce_in_place` is `destructive`, since it deletes the track's devices and clips; undoing it gives back the source track exactly, with every device's state. The other two are `edit`, and `track.paste_bounced_audio` is destructive as well when it would replace or shorten a clip, as agent permissions R1 decides for Paste. They read back through the query tool's `song`, field `tracks`, and `track.paste_bounced_audio` through its `track`, field `clips`. A bounce's files stay on disk when it's undone. (S10; D17)

**For every freeze and bounce (WP-4.14 and WP-4.15)**

- **R27.** Every render runs as offline renders do, each block a real-time section under the allocation trap, and the same set renders the same files at any block size, run after run; a bounce renders on a worker thread with its progress in the Status Bar, and lands as one undo step when every file is complete, a cancel or a failure leaving the set and the disk as they were. A bounce of a track with a device R11 names is refused as a freeze is. (S8)
- **R28.** The items sit at their menus.md rows in Live's order, with their keys: Freeze Track and Bounce Track in Place enabled with a track that can be frozen or bounced selected, Bounce to New Track with clips or time selected, and Paste Bounced Audio once a source is copied; S-TRACK-017, S-BOUNCE-001, and S-BOUNCE-002 are bound in their contexts. Frozen clips are drawn as frozen, in Beatlayer's own colours, and tail regions crosshatched. (S1, S6)
- **R29.** An agent reaches every freeze and bounce through its command, with the tracks, clips, and ranges spelled out or the selection, and the call returns when the edit has landed, with the new tracks' and clips' IDs. (D17)
