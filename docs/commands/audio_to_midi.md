## Commands

### `audio_to_midi.cancel`

**Cancel**. Permission: `read`. Contexts: `global`.

Stops the conversion or the package download under way and closes the window. A cancelled conversion changes nothing in the set; a cancelled download keeps the packages it finished. Changes nothing in the set.

Default keys: none.

Arguments: none (send `{}`).

Result: [`AudioToMidiStatus`](README.md#audiotomidistatus).

### `audio_to_midi.convert`

**Convert to New MIDI Track**. Permission: `edit`. Contexts: `global`.

Converts an audio clip to notes on a new MIDI track: `kind` is `harmony` (pitches of polyphonic audio, played by an Instrument Rack named Harmony), `melody` (pitches of monophonic audio, played by an Instrument Rack named Melody whose first macro, Synth to Piano, moves its sound from a synth to an electric piano) or `drums` (kick, snare and hi-hat, on notes 36, 38 and 42 of a Drum Rack named Drums). `clip` is the audio clip, in the Arrangement or a Session slot; with none, the one audio clip selected. The audio is the clip's file from its Start marker to its End marker, or through its loop. The new track goes directly after the clip's track, named after the clip and the kind, and is selected; its clip starts where the source clip starts (in the same scene's slot for a Session clip) and is as long, with the source's loop, markers, name and colour, its notes timed through the source's warp markers (an unwarped clip at the set's tempo). Transient markers the person placed or moved are hints: an onset within 30 ms of one moves to it, and a held note crossing one is split. Harmony and Melody need the packages the Packages manager downloads on first use; the first time, this opens the package page instead and runs once they're installed. Returns at once; `audio_to_midi.status` reports the progress and, once it lands, the new track and clip, and `song` `tracks` and `clip` read them and the notes back. The track, its instrument, its clip and its notes are one undo step named after the command; the source clip is left as it is. Refused for a MIDI clip, a missing file, or while another conversion or a stem separation runs.

Default keys: none.

Arguments:

| Name | Type | Required | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `kind` | [`ConvertKind`](README.md#convertkind) | yes | What to find: `harmony`, `melody` or `drums`. |
| `clip` | [`ClipId`](README.md#clipid) or null | no | The audio clip, in the Arrangement or a Session slot. With none, the one audio clip selected in either view. |

Result: [`AudioToMidiStatus`](README.md#audiotomidistatus).

### `audio_to_midi.status`

**Audio to MIDI Status**. Permission: `read`. Contexts: `global`.

Reads the state of Convert Harmony, Melody and Drums to New MIDI Track: the conversion under way (its kind, clip and progress) or the last one's outcome and the track and clip it made, whether its packages are installed (and each package's name, version, project page and download size), the download's progress, and the window if it's open.

Default keys: none.

Arguments: none (send `{}`).

Result: [`AudioToMidiStatus`](README.md#audiotomidistatus).
