## The behaviour

Cabinet is what a guitar amplifier plays through: a box of loudspeakers, heard through a microphone. The person chooses the cabinet by its speakers' count and size, the microphone's position (close and on the speaker's axis, close and off it, or far) and its type (dynamic or condenser). The result is mono or processed in stereo (Dual) and mixed with the dry signal. Each combination is a short impulse response the device plays on the convolution engine, with no latency.

The device takes Live's name with Beatlayer's own type ID, `beatlayer.cabinet`. Its thirty responses are Beatlayer's own, generated by a script from models of a loudspeaker, its cabinet and a microphone, not recorded, and none is named after or modelled on any manufacturer's product.

## Rules

- **R1.** Cabinet (`beatlayer.cabinet`) is an audio effect with Live's name, Beatlayer's own layout and no display cell: the Speaker chooser, the Microphone chooser, the microphone type switch (Dynamic, Condenser), the Output switch (Mono, Dual) and Dry/Wet. A new device has 4x12, Near On-Axis, Dynamic and Mono. (S1; the layout and defaults are Beatlayer's own)
- **R2.** Speaker chooses among five cabinets: 1x12, 2x12, 4x12, 4x10 and 8x10, each named by its speakers' count and size in inches. Microphone, type and Speaker are choices, and so automatable parameters (rule G2), not media slots. (S1; the five are Beatlayer's own)
- **R3.** Each of the 5 × 3 × 2 = 30 combinations plays its own response, generated by `cargo xtask impulses` with the bundled reverb responses ([impulse responses](impulse-responses.md) R16), shipped read-only with them and checked by the same test. Each response is a mono 32-bit float WAV at 48 kHz, 100 ms long, normalised as [impulse responses](impulse-responses.md) R7 says. (S4; Beatlayer's own)
- **R4.** A response is the product, in frequency, of three models: the loudspeaker in its box, a second-order high-pass at the cabinet's resonance (60 to 110 Hz) with the box's Q (Thiele and Small's closed or vented alignment), and the cone's break-up as three resonant peaks and a fourth-order roll-off above the speaker's size's limit (a 12-inch speaker from 5 kHz, a 10-inch from 6 kHz); the cabinet, a comb of its panel's reflections (two to four short delays of 0.3 to 2 ms, set by its size, at −12 to −20 dB) and a low-shelf for its enclosed volume; and the microphone, its position and type: Near On-Axis flat, Near Off-Axis a first-order low-pass at 3 kHz for the off-axis loss, Far a 1.5 ms delay, the room's first reflection at 4 ms and −10 dB and less proximity boost; Dynamic a presence peak of +4 dB at 5 kHz and a roll-off above 12 kHz, Condenser flat to 18 kHz; the near positions' proximity effect a +6 dB shelf below 150 Hz. The design table in the generator holds each cabinet's figures. (S3; every model and value is Beatlayer's own)
- **R5.** Running the generator twice gives the same bytes, and each response's measured −3 dB points and largest resonance lie within 5 % (in frequency) and 1 dB (in level) of its design. (Beatlayer's own)
- **R6.** The device convolves its input with the chosen response on the shared convolution engine, with no latency: the response measured through the device (a unit impulse in, Dry/Wet 100 %) equals the shipped file, resampled to the engine's rate, within −90 dB. (S1, S4)
- **R7.** Changing Speaker, Microphone or the type crossfades from the old response to the new over 20 ms with no click, the new response being prepared in advance: every response is prepared when the device's node is made, so a change on the audio thread swaps between prepared responses and allocates nothing. Each change from a control is one undo step. (S5; Beatlayer's own)
- **R8.** With Output Mono, the input's channels are summed and one convolution runs, its output on both channels; with Dual, each channel runs its own, so a signal on the left alone leaves nothing on the right. (S1)
- **R9.** Dry/Wet, 0 to 100 % (100 % by default), balances the dry signal against the cabinet's. (S1; the default is Beatlayer's own)
- **R10.** A render is bit-identical at block sizes 1, 64, 512 and 4,096, the device reports no latency, and nothing on the audio thread allocates, locks or blocks. (S5)
- **R11.** Every parameter is automatable and modulatable (rule G2) and set and read back through the agent API. (S5)
- **R12.** Cabinet declares one bank of eight ([control surfaces](control-surfaces.md) R19): Speaker, Microphone, Mic Type, Output, Dry/Wet, so every automatable parameter is in a bank. (S5; Beatlayer's own)
- **R13.** A set with Cabinet saves and reopens with every parameter. (S5)
- **R14.** No speaker, cabinet or microphone is named after a manufacturer or a product, in the device, the generator or the responses' names. (Beatlayer's own)
