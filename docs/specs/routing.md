## The behaviour

Every audio and MIDI track has an In/Out section: where its signal comes from (Audio From or MIDI From, with a channel), whether its input is heard (Monitor: In, Auto, Off), and where its signal goes (Audio To or MIDI To, with a channel). Group and return tracks have only Audio To; Main has Cue Out and Main Out, where Cue Out plays the cue-switched tracks in Cue mode ([mixer](mixer.md) R26). The section sits at the top of each mixer column in both views and in the second column of each Arrangement track header.

A track can take its input from another track, and can send its output into another track's input. Taken as an input, another track's signal can be tapped before its devices (Pre FX), after its devices and before its mixer (Post FX), or after its mixer (Post Mixer, the default). A track's devices can also offer extra audio outputs past their main one — a plug-in's further audio output ports, an instrument's individual outputs — which another track takes as its input beside those taps. Sent as an output into another audio track, it arrives at that track's input (Track In), which the receiving track hears only while it monitors. Resampling takes the Main mix as a track's input; a resampling track's own output is silenced so it can't feed back. Sends Only sends a track's audio only through its sends. Live accepts a routing that makes a loop; Beatlayer does too, and its engine plays the routing that closes the loop as silence.

There is no limit on return tracks (IMP-01). Every audio, MIDI, and group track has a send knob per return, from −∞ to 0 dB, and each return's Pre/Post switch, on Main's column, decides whether its sends take the signal before or after each sending track's mixer.

Every chooser lists what Live's lists, in Live's order, with Live's defaults chosen. Entries for what other packages bring are listed, disabled, and name the package. Every routing change is a registered command and one undo step.

A device can have a sidechain input: a second signal its detector listens to, as a compressor ducks one track under another. In Live 12 the device's own sidechain section picks that signal's source, a track tapped before its devices, after them, or after its mixer; tracks aren't routed into a sidechain from their Audio To, whose channel lists Track In alone. The section's External switch decides whether the detector hears the sidechain or the device's own input, Mix blends the two, SC Gain scales the sidechain, and Sidechain Listen plays what the detector hears. The source track goes on playing as before.

A device can also have a MIDI sidechain input: the notes and channel messages of a MIDI track, which the device hears beside its audio, as Shifter takes its pitch from another track's notes. Its source is a MIDI track, chosen in the same left strip, and the track goes on playing as before.

Duplicate Track Without Clips (IMP-12), next to Duplicate in the Edit menu, copies a track's devices, routing, mixer settings, and name, but no clips or arrangement automation, as one undo step.

This spec covers the In/Out section, the sends' knobs and the Pre/Post switches as controls, devices' sidechains, Duplicate Track Without Clips, and how the engine carries routing. What the sends, Pre/Post, and solo do to the sound is [mixer](mixer.md)'s; where the sections sit is [mixer-view](mixer-view.md)'s.

## Rules

- **R1.** A new audio track takes Ext. In on the first pair (1/2) and a new MIDI track All Ins, both with Monitor Auto; both go to Main (or their group), and a MIDI track's MIDI To is No Output. A track taken as an input is tapped Post Mixer unless another tap is chosen. A new return's Pre/Post switch is Post. (S2, S7, S8)
- **R2.** An audio track takes as its input Ext. In, Resampling, another audio or group track, a MIDI track that holds an instrument ([racks](racks.md) R38), a return, Main, or nothing; a MIDI track All Ins, the computer keyboard, one input port by name, another MIDI track, or nothing. Group and return tracks and Main take no input. A track never takes itself. (S7, S8)
- **R3.** Audio, group, and return tracks send their audio to Main, an audio track's Track In (another track, or, for a group, one of its own tracks), the group the track is in, Sends Only, or Ext. Out. Ext. Out plays the track on the interface's output channels and not into Main: on a pair, its left and right on the pair's two channels; on a single channel, the two sides added and 6 dB down. Where it shares channels with Main Out, or with another track on Ext. Out, they add; a channel the device lacks plays nothing, and a track that hears Main plays nothing there (R14). MIDI tracks send their audio to Main or their group, and Main nowhere else. A track never sends to itself. (S7, S8, S15)
- **R4.** A MIDI track's MIDI To is another MIDI track's Track In, or No Output. (S7, S8)
- **R5.** A routing that makes a loop is accepted and kept in the set, as Live accepts it. (S8)
- **R6.** Putting a track routed to Main into a group sends it to the group; a track routed elsewhere keeps its routing. A track in a group can be sent to Main, which it then keeps; Main from a top-level track, and a track's own group, are the default. Taking a track out of its group sends a track that went to the group to Main. (S5, S8)
- **R7.** Deleting a track leaves every Audio To that named it at Sends Only, every input that named it at No Input, and every MIDI To that named it at No Output. Undo restores the track and the routings. (S8)
- **R8.** Routing is saved with the set only where it differs from a new track's, so a set saved before WP-2.19 loads with every track at its kind's default. Ext. Out's channel is saved unless it's the first pair, `1/2`, and a set opened on a device with fewer outputs keeps it. A saved routing that breaks R2 to R4 doesn't load. (S11)
- **R9.** There is no limit on return tracks: fifty play, save, and load. Returns past Z take two letters (AA, AB, …), in the mixer and the choosers. (S11, IMP-01)
- **R10.** A track's output sent into an audio track's Track In arrives at that track's input, before its devices, and is heard only while that track monitors; the receiving track's devices and mixer act on it. Sends Only sends a track's audio nowhere but its sends. (S1, S4, S8)
- **R11.** A track hears its input (its own Audio From, and every track routed to its Track In) while its Monitor is In, or Auto with the track armed and not playing a clip; with In, its clips are silent. Under Auto the input closes while the track's clips play and opens when they end, which the audio thread sets each block; a Session slot clip counts while it is launched and not before. Monitor and arm changes, and a clip starting or ending, open and close the input and the clips with the mixer's 20 ms glide ([mixer](mixer.md) R18), without a new program; a routing change makes a new program. (S2)
- **R12.** A track's input taken from another track carries that track's signal before its devices (Pre FX: its clips and its own input), after its devices (Post FX), or after its mixer (Post Mixer). A device with extra audio outputs past its main one is taken instead: the input then carries that output's signal, whatever tap it names, and the device's main output goes on down its chain as before. A device's extra outputs are what its type declares: a CLAP plug-in's audio output ports past its main one ([clap hosting](clap-hosting.md) R2), and a native instrument's own ([the eight-slot drum sampler](eight-slot-drum-sampler.md) R14). A VST3 instrument's auxiliary output buses and an Audio Unit's further output busses reach this the same way, and are wired to it as their hosts' tasks take them up. The node renders each into a buffer made with the program; a device whose type declares none offers none. Deleting the device, its chain, its rack or its track leaves the input at No Input, and undo restores both (R7). (S4, S7, S18)
- **R13.** A resampling track's input is the Main mix, and its own output and sends are silent, so Main carries the mix without it. (S3)
- **R14.** The engine runs every track after every track that feeds it. It breaks loops so: among audio, MIDI, and group tracks, outputs into groups come first, then outputs into other tracks, then inputs taken from other tracks, each in display order, and one that would close a loop with those before it carries silence. A track that hears Main (it resamples, or its input comes from Main or from a track that hears Main) has a silent output and silent sends; a track that hears a return (its input comes from a return, or a return's output reaches it) has silent sends. (S3, S5; S11)
- **R15.** Solo: the solo path is every soloed track, every track a soloed track's output reaches through groups and Track In short of Main, and every track whose output reaches a soloed track that way. Every track outside the path is silent ([mixer](mixer.md) R10). An input tapped Pre FX or Post FX is never silenced by solo; one tapped Post Mixer is silenced with its source's output. So soloing a track plays the tracks routed into it, and soloing a source plays it through the track it's routed to. (S4, S6)
- **R16.** The choosers list, in order (─ a separator): Audio From, Ext. In · Configure… ─ Resampling ─ the group and audio tracks, the MIDI tracks that hold an instrument, the returns, and Main, in display order, without the track itself ─ No Input; its channel, Pre FX · Post FX · Post Mixer with a track chosen, followed by `rack | chain | tap` for each chain of that track's racks ([racks](racks.md) R38, R39) and then `device | output` for each extra audio output of each of its devices, at any depth, in signal order (R12), the interface's inputs with Ext. In (the single channels, then the pairs), nothing otherwise. MIDI From, All Ins · Computer Keyboard (both disabled, WP-2.2) · Configure… ─ the other MIDI tracks ─ No Input; its channel as Audio From's, or All Channels with a port (disabled, WP-2.2). Audio To, Ext. Out · Configure… ─ Main, the track's group, and the audio tracks in display order, without the track itself ─ Sends Only, a group's list with a separator after Main; its channel, Track In with a track chosen, the interface's outputs with Ext. Out (the single channels, then the pairs; dimmed with no device open, or when the chosen channel is past the device's outputs), nothing with Main, a group, or Sends Only. A new Ext. Out choice takes the first pair, `1/2`, and choosing Ext. Out again keeps its channel. MIDI To, Configure… ─ the other MIDI tracks ─ No Output; its channel, Track In with a track chosen. Main's Cue Out and Main Out list Configure… ─ the interface's output pairs (`1/2`, `3/4`, …), the chosen one lit; with no device open, Configure… only, as Live shows with no audio device. A track is listed as its number and name (`4-Audio`), a return as its letter and name (`A-Reverb`), Main as `Main`, a name's own leading number or letter not repeated. Configure… opens Settings on the Audio page, or Tempo & MIDI for MIDI. (S7, S8; Audio To's channel lists Track In alone even when the chosen track's devices have sidechain inputs, S12)
- **R17.** A click on a chooser opens its list, and a click on an entry runs its command; disabled entries say which package brings them. With the focus on a chooser, Up and Down choose the entry before or after the chosen one, Configure… aside. The Monitor switch shows In, Auto, and Off side by side, the chosen one lit; a click on one chooses it, and with the focus on it Left and Right choose the one beside. The choosers and the Monitor switch take the keyboard focus in Live's Tab order (S9): in a Session strip after the title bar and before the sends; in an Arrangement header after its title and before the switches; a disabled chooser takes the focus too. (S7, S9)
- **R18.** An audio, MIDI (with an instrument), or group track's send knob runs from −∞ to 0 dB: a vertical drag turns it, its whole travel in 62.5 points, as one undo step named `Change "Send"`; Up and Down step a dB, Shift a tenth; a double-click or Delete returns it to −∞; with several tracks selected, each moves by the same dB. Each knob is named by its return (`A-Return`) and reads its level in dB. A return's own sends are dimmed until the track context menu brings Enable Send, and a MIDI track without an instrument shows no sends. (S5, S9, S10)
- **R19.** Each return's Pre/Post switch, on Main's column where the sends are, reads Pre or Post; a click, or Enter with the focus on it, switches it with `mixer.set_send_tap`. In the Tab order the switch comes where a track's sends do. (S5, S7, S10)
- **R20.** Every routing action is a registered command (D17) in the `edit` class, runnable by ID with JSON arguments: `routing.set_input`, `routing.set_monitor`, `routing.set_output`, `routing.set_midi_output`, `routing.set_main_out`, and `routing.set_cue_out`; `routing.set_output`'s `external` takes a `channel`, the first pair when it's left out. Each is one undo step; the input, output, Main Out, and Cue Out ones are named `Change "Routing"` as Live names them, and a Monitor change is named `Change monitoring`. (S8, S11)
- **R21.** MIDI routing (MIDI From another track, MIDI To) is kept in the set and shown, and carries MIDI once MIDI reaches tracks (WP-2.1). It carries no audio: a MIDI track taking another MIDI track's MIDI doesn't sum that track's audio into its own, though the source still runs before it. (S7, S8)
- **R22.** A device type may declare a sidechain input; every device of that type has one, and no other device does. A new device's sidechain has External off, Source No Input, Mix 100 %, SC Gain 0 dB, and Sidechain Listen off. It's saved with the set where the device has one, each setting only where it differs from a new device's, so sets saved before WP-2.19b load unchanged. (S12, S11)
- **R23.** A sidechain's Source is No Input or a track: an audio or group track (the device's own included), a return, or Main, tapped Pre FX, Post FX, or Post Mixer as R12 describes. Choosing a track from the Source chooser takes it Post FX and leaves External as it is. The source goes on playing where its own routing sends it. A MIDI track, Ext. In, or Resampling is refused. (S12)
- **R24.** While External is on, the device's detector hears SC Gain × Mix × the source plus (1 − Mix) × the device's own input; while it's off, its own input alone, whatever the source. With Sidechain Listen on and External on, the device's output is what its detector hears; with Listen on and External off, it is the device's normal output. SC Gain and Mix glide over 20 ms; changing them, External, or Listen sends no new program, and changing the Source makes one. (S12, S13)
- **R25.** Deleting a sidechain's source track leaves its Source at No Input and External as it was; deleting the device takes its sidechain with it. Undo restores both. (S12)
- **R26.** The engine runs a sidechain's source before the device's track. A Source that would close a loop (the device's track already feeds the source by any route, R14's order taken first) carries silence; a device listening to its own track hears it Pre FX, which is there before its chain runs, and silence Post FX or Post Mixer. (S11)
- **R27.** Solo never silences a sidechain: a device keyed from a track that solo silences still hears it. (Beatlayer)
- **R28.** Duplicate Track Without Clips (`track.duplicate_without_clips`, IMP-12), in the Edit menu straight after Duplicate, copies the selected tracks (or those named), each straight after itself, with its name (renumbered for its own place where it starts with the track's number or a return's letter, as Live names "4 Audio"'s duplicate "5 Audio"), mixer settings, sends, routing, and devices with their settings and sidechains, but no clips and no arrangement automation; a group's copy holds copies of its tracks, without clips. The copies become the selected tracks. One undo step, `Duplicate Track(s) Without Clips` (Live's Duplicate Track(s), made distinct). Main can't be duplicated. Colour waits for track colours; the track context menu's item waits for that menu. (S12, S14)
- **R29.** A device with a sidechain input has a strip down its left edge, under its title bar, "Sidechain" written up it, with the section's show/hide toggle at its top and Sidechain Listen under it. The toggle shows the section beside the strip; showing and hiding change only the view and aren't undo steps. The section, top to bottom: External, a text button lit while on; the Source label and chooser; the channel chooser; Mix and SC Gain, number boxes lit while External is on, a vertical drag changing either as one undo step, a double-click or Delete setting it back. Mix runs from 0 to 100 % and SC Gain from −∞ to +24 dB, a drag covering −70 to +24 dB of it, at Live's knobs' 0.8 % of the range per point. The strip is a title bar wide (16 points), the section 80 points wide, and its rows 14 points high. Every control takes the keyboard focus in the Device View: Enter presses a button, Up and Down step the choosers as R17's do and move a number box by a hundredth of its range. (S12)
- **R30.** The Source chooser lists the audio and group tracks in display order, the device's own track among them, the returns, and Main ─ No Input, labelled as R16 labels tracks; its channel chooser lists Pre FX · Post FX · Post Mixer with a track chosen and nothing with No Input. (S12)
- **R31.** Each sidechain setting is a registered command in the `edit` class (`device.set_sidechain_input`, `device.set_sidechain_enabled`, `device.set_sidechain_mix`, `device.set_sidechain_gain`, `device.set_sidechain_listen`), one undo step each, named `Change "Routing"`, `Change "Sidechain On"`, `Change "Mix"`, `Change "SC Gain"`, and `Change "Sidechain Listen"`; showing the section is `device.show_sidechain`. (S12)
- **R32.** A device type may declare a MIDI sidechain input; every device of that type has one, and no other device does. A new device's MIDI sidechain has no source (No Input). It is saved with the set where the device has one, as `midi_sidechain` with a `source` where one is set, so sets saved before WP-5.27 load unchanged. The engine hands the device, as its block's notes (device framework R30, and the channel messages of R39 beside them, in time order), the notes and channel messages its source track's clips and MIDI input played in the block, as the track played them, before its own devices changed them; the notes of the device's own chain aren't handed to it, and with No Input it hears none. Its own track's notes are those of its clips and input, which are there before the chain runs. A bypassed device passes its chain's notes along. (S16, S17)
- **R33.** A MIDI sidechain's source is No Input or a MIDI track, the device's own included; an audio, group or return track or Main is refused. The engine runs the source before the device's track, in R14's order. A source that would close a loop (the device's track already feeds it by any route) carries nothing. Deleting the source track leaves the source at No Input; undo restores both. (S16)
- **R34.** A device with a MIDI sidechain input and no audio sidechain has the left strip of R29, "Sidechain" written up it, with the section's show/hide toggle at its top and no Sidechain Listen. - **R39.** A device whose spec gives its MIDI sidechain a tapping point (Roar, Corpus, Auto Shift and Spectral Resonator) has a chooser of Pre FX, Post FX and Post Mixer under its source, Post FX in a new device. A device whose spec gives no tapping point (Shifter) stays Pre FX. A Post FX source that would close a loop carries nothing, as R33 says. (S20; Post FX for MIDI, Post Mixer matching it and Post FX as the default are Beatlayer's own)
